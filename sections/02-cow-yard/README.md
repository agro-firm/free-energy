# 02 — Cow Yard

## Status
Planning baseline. Detailed construction design will be added later.

## Baseline
18 × 28 ft

## Purpose
Compact outdoor/open movement and holding area for cows.

## Inputs
Cows, drinking water, wash water as required.

## Outputs
Animal movement back to the shed; dirty drainage to the appropriate waste/drainage route.

## Interfaces
Cow shed, water/drainage and internal circulation.

## Current design notes
Must remain non-slip, drainable and easy to wash. It is not intended as pasture.
