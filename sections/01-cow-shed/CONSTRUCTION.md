# Cow Shed — Construction and Quality Plan

## 1. Scope

This file defines the recommended construction sequence for Section 01.

It is not a structural design or bill of quantities.

---

## 2. Pre-construction requirements

Before work:
1. survey plot and section coordinates
2. verify finished floor level and waste-outlet elevation
3. geotechnical/foundation review
4. confirm scraper vendor requirements
5. confirm cow-stall system
6. confirm solar structural load
7. confirm water/electrical routes
8. issue coordinated drawings

Critical point: the manure outlet elevation and scraper floor geometry must be confirmed **before concrete is poured**.

---

## 3. Construction sequence

### Phase 1 — setting out
- establish local 48 × 26 ft footprint
- mark structural grid
- mark manure lanes
- mark feed alley
- verify diagonals

### Phase 2 — earthwork and subgrade
- remove unsuitable topsoil
- compact fill in controlled layers
- provide drainage/ground moisture strategy
- prepare foundations and slab subbase

### Phase 3 — foundations
- construct engineer-designed column footings/pedestals
- install anchor bolts with templates
- verify level and spacing
- protect embedded items

### Phase 4 — underground/embedded services
Before slab:
- manure/waste outlet sleeves
- water sleeves
- electrical earthing
- drainage sleeves
- any planned wash points

### Phase 5 — floor and manure geometry
- pour reinforced slab
- create specified non-slip finish
- form cow-platform falls
- form scraper lanes/gutters
- form manger bases
- verify no low points
- cure concrete properly

### Phase 6 — steel frame
- erect columns
- rafters/trusses
- bracing
- purlins
- corrosion protection
- torque/connection checks

### Phase 7 — roof
- install roof sheets/panels
- ridge vent
- flashing
- gutters
- downpipes
- prepare solar attachment zones

### Phase 8 — stall/manger fit-out
- rails/dividers
- mangers
- gates
- trough supports
- guards

### Phase 9 — manure scraper
- install drive
- scraper blades/chain/cable
- end switches
- guards
- emergency stop
- dry testing

### Phase 10 — MEP
- water header
- trough connections
- electrical subpanel
- cable tray
- fan circuits
- lighting
- sensors/data

### Phase 11 — fans and controls
- mount fans
- verify guards
- commission controls
- test scraper interlocks

### Phase 12 — solar interface
Section 15 installs roof solar after structural/roof acceptance.

---

## 4. Material quality requirements

### Concrete
Engineer to specify:
- concrete grade
- reinforcement
- thickness
- joint spacing
- foundation sizes

Surface must be:
- durable
- non-slip
- washable
- not so rough that it injures hooves

### Steel
Require:
- certified structural steel
- protective galvanizing/coating
- protected cut edges/welds
- corrosion-resistant fasteners in wet areas

### Roof
Require:
- corrosion resistance
- watertight laps
- secure wind fastening
- thermal strategy
- controlled penetrations

Do not proceed past these without inspection:

| HP-03 | embedded drains/sleeves before slab |
| HP-04 | floor/scraper slope formwork |

| HP-06 | roof watertightness |
| HP-07 | scraper dry commissioning |
| HP-08 | water pressure/leak test |

---

## 6. Commissioning tests

### Dry
- gates
- stall hardware
- scraper travel
- limit switches
- emergency stops
- fan rotation
- lights
- sensors

### Water
- trough fill
- overflow
- wash hose
- drain flow
- leak inspection

### Manure simulation
Before animals:
- use safe test material/water
- confirm scraper route
- confirm downstream transfer
- confirm no backflow/flooding

### Operational
Introduce animals gradually and observe:
- footing
- movement
- water use
- feed access
- manure capture
- ventilation
