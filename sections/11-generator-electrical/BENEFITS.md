# Generator + Electrical — Benefits

## Waste-to-electricity
The section converts recovered biogas into usable farm electricity.

## Fuel matching
5 kW is intentionally small enough to match the ~8–9 m³/day gas resource.

## Better engine loading
Running near 4 kW avoids operating an oversized generator at very low load.

## Essential-load resilience
Milk chilling, vaccine refrigeration, controls, water and selected ventilation can continue during outages when gas is available.

## No-grid-parallel simplicity
The baseline ATS topology avoids synchronization/export complexity and reduces risk.

## Data
Gas meter + generator kWh enables:
```
kWh per m³ biogas
```
tracking and maintenance diagnostics.

## Solar complement
Solar serves daytime energy; biogas can be dispatched in stored-gas cycles, including night/outage operation.

## Safety
Dedicated room isolates:
- exhaust
- heat
- electrical panels
- gas shutoff
from animals and clean food areas.
