# Rooftop Solar — Operating Process

## Sunrise
- inverter wakes when DC voltage available.
- runs MPPT.
- synchronizes to utility.
- feeds main bus.

## Daytime
- farm loads use PV.
- surplus may export under approved net metering.
- monitor string power.

## Grid loss
- inverter anti-islands.
- PV stops exporting.
- biogas generator may power Essential Load Board independently.

## Grid return
- inverter waits vendor reconnect interval.
- resynchronizes.

## Fault
- isolate DC/AC according to LOTO.
- do not disconnect live DC connector under load unless device is rated/procedure allows.

## Cleaning
- early morning/cool module.
- safe roof access.
- inspect connectors/rails simultaneously.
