# Confined Space Program

## Candidate confined spaces
- Section 07 receiving/emergency sumps.
- Section 08 digester.
- Section 12 tanks.
- Section 13 ground water tank.
- other pits/chambers after assessment.

## Normal rule
NO ENTRY for routine operation.

## Entry permit requires
- process isolation.
- electrical/mechanical LOTO.
- atmospheric testing.
- ventilation.
- attendant.
- communication.
- rescue plan.
- retrieval equipment where appropriate.
- trained entrants.
- permit time/location.
- continuous/periodic monitoring as required.

## Atmosphere
Test for:
- oxygen.
- LEL/CH₄.
- H₂S.
- CO where relevant.

Exact acceptance limits follow the approved confined-space standard/procedure.

## Rescue
Do not rely on untrained coworker entry.
Pre-plan non-entry rescue where feasible.
