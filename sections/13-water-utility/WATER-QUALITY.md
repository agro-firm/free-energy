# Water Utility — Water Quality

## Principle
Water used for milk-equipment cleaning and human handwashing must be potable-quality.

## Bangladesh parameters
DPHE publishes Bangladesh drinking-water parameters including:
- arsenic: 0.05 mg/L
- fecal coliform: 0/100 mL
- total coliform: 0/100 mL
- TDS: 1000 mg/L
- iron: 0.3–1.0 mg/L
- manganese: 0.1 mg/L
- turbidity: 10 NTU
- pH guidance around 6.5–8.5

Reference:
https://dphe.gov.bd/site/page/15fa0d7b-11f1-45c0-a684-10a543376873/

## Commissioning tests
At minimum:
- arsenic
- iron
- manganese
- pH
- TDS
- hardness
- turbidity
- total/fecal coliform

Add salinity/chloride and other parameters based on local source risk.

## Treatment
Treatment train is selected from lab results.

Examples:
- sediment filter
- iron/manganese filter
- arsenic removal
- disinfection/chlorination
- UV polish

## Monitoring
- source test before commissioning
- microbiological retest periodically
- retest after source/flood/repair event
- tank-cleaning schedule
