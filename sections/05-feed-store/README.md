# 05 — Feed Store

## Status
Planning baseline. Detailed construction design will be added later.

## Baseline
18 × 20 ft

## Purpose
Short-term dry feed, concentrate, mineral and ration-storage area.

## Inputs
Feed delivered from the service lane.

## Outputs
Measured feed to the cow-shed feed alley.

## Interfaces
Feed unloading canopy, service lane and cow shed.

## Current design notes
Designed for frequent delivery rather than months of fodder storage. Keep dry, pest controlled and organized by FIFO.
