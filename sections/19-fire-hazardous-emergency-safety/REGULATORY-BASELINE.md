# Regulatory Baseline

## Bangladesh National Building Code
The Ministry of Housing and Public Works currently publishes:
- Bangladesh National Building Code (BNBC) 2020.
- Building Construction Rules.

Official page:
https://mohpw.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/694032c235ce18e1c0561e05

## Fire Service e-NOC
Current FSCD e-NOC instructions state that the application process includes registration/application and, after initial processing, submission of:
- fire-fighting floor plan.
- layout plan.
- land documents.
- Google map.
An inspection committee then determines approval.

Portal:
https://enoc.fireservice.gov.bd/

## E-fire license
FSCD's current e-fire-license portal states that e-fire-license issuance started from 01 May 2026 and is being progressively extended.

The current application includes fields for:
- business/use type.
- combustible/flammable materials.
- fire-prevention/safety arrangements.
- access-road width.
- construction fire-fighting NOC status.

Portal:
https://elicense.fireservice.gov.bd/

## Fire risk assessment
FSCD Fire & Safety Cell currently advertises onsite fire-risk assessment/survey service.

Portal:
https://fsc.fireservice.gov.bd/

## Project action
Before IFC/construction/operation:
1. determine whether e-NOC applies to proposed construction/use.
2. prepare Fire Safety Plan and fire-fighting layout.
3. obtain required NOC/clearance.
4. obtain fire license as applicable.
5. maintain renewal/reassessment requirements.

## Rule
Do not use this document as a legal determination of occupancy category, required fire-water capacity, detector quantity, fire rating or separation distance.
