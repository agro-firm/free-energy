# Training and Drills

## Induction
All workers:
- alarm sound.
- emergency numbers.
- assembly point.
- no-smoking/hot-work.
- gas hazard.
- extinguisher awareness.
- traffic safety.

## Specialist
Selected staff:
- extinguisher practical training.
- LOTO.
- gas detector use.
- confined-space permit.
- first aid.
- emergency shutdown.
- spill response.

## Fire Service
FSCD Fire & Safety Cell currently offers:
- fire-safety training.
- drills.
- risk assessment/survey services.

## Drill program
Planning:
- fire/evacuation drill at least twice yearly or as required.
- gas-leak scenario.
- generator emergency.
- animal evacuation tabletop.
- confined-space rescue drill for rescue team only.

Final statutory frequency follows current requirements and approved plan.

## Records
- date.
- scenario.
- attendance.
- evacuation time.
- issues.
- corrective actions.
