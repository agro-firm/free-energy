# Truck / Service Lane — Utilities

## Lighting
Outdoor LED fixtures at loading nodes.
Prefer wall-mounted or poles outside strike zone.

## CCTV
Gate + feed + rear node cameras.

## Electrical crossings
Avoid overhead cables crossing lane.
Underground crossings:
- conduit/sleeve
- adequate burial
- detectable marking
- no unprotected joints under pavement

## Water crossings
Use sleeves below pavement.
Isolation valves remain outside traffic surface.

## Gas crossings
Avoid gas pipelines crossing service lane where practical.
If unavoidable:
- engineered buried/protected crossing
- no valve/manifold in wheel path
- marker/records

## Drainage
Outer-edge clean storm drain.
Dirty process drainage remains separate.

## Data
Optional:
- gate contact
- CCTV
- vehicle sensor
- node lighting control
