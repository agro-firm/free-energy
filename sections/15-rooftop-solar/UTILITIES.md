# Rooftop Solar — Utilities and Interfaces

## DC cabling
Baseline:
- 6 mm² copper PV cable.
- one home-run per string.
- MC4-compatible matched connectors only.

## AC
Baseline:
- 400/230 V three-phase.
- 5-core 6 mm² Cu concept for short run.
- final cable by length/installation/derating.

## Communications
- inverter monitoring.
- Wi-Fi/Ethernet/RS485 as available.
- plant dashboard receives kW/kWh/faults.

## Drainage
Modules do not block gutters.
No cable lies inside gutter.

## Roof ventilation
Preserve ridge vent.

## Washing
Use clean water and soft tools.
No harsh chemicals.
