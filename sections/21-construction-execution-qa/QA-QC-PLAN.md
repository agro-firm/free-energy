# QA/QC Plan

## Objectives
- build to approved design.
- prove quality before cover-up.
- maintain traceability.
- prevent rework.
- preserve records.

## Quality organization
Contractor QC:
first check.

Consultant/owner QA:
independent verification.

Vendor:
specialist quality/commissioning.

## Controlled documents
- IFC drawing.
- approved shop drawing.
- method statement.
- ITP.
- material approval.
- calibration record.
- IR.
- test.
- NCR.
- as-built.

## Calibration
Survey, pressure, electrical, torque and process test instruments require valid calibration/verification.

## Witness
Hold/witness points are scheduled early enough that inspection does not become an excuse to bypass quality.

## Records
Store digitally by section/package/date.
