# Water Utility — Safety

## Hazards
- drowning/confined-space in ground tank
- electrical shock
- contaminated water
- elevated tank/fall
- structural tank failure
- pump pressure
- chemical-treatment handling

## Ground tank
No routine entry.
Entry requires confined-space controls.

## OHT
Platform/ladder requires:
- fall protection
- engineered structure
- restricted access

## Electrical
Dry pump/control bay.
RCD/earthing as required.
Lockout before pump service.

## Water quality
Never assume clear water is potable.
Use laboratory testing.

## Chemicals
Chlorine/other treatment chemicals require labeled secure storage and SDS/PPE.
