# Construction Traffic Management

## Lane
Section 14 remains the controlled access spine.

## Rules
- delivery booking.
- one truck at a time.
- spotter.
- reverse-in/forward-out preferred.
- 5–10 km/h internal planning limit.
- emergency access priority.

## During lane construction
Provide approved temporary access strategy before closing the lane.

## Pedestrian
Use separated marked routes where possible.

## Equipment
No excavator/crane swings over uncontrolled pedestrian route.

## Mud
Clean vehicle wheels/road if construction soil is tracked onto public road.
