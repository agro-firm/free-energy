# Office + Vet + Biosecurity — Benefits

## Biosecurity
Creates one controlled entrance rather than allowing visitors/staff to enter animal areas directly.

## Animal health
Keeps medicines, vaccines, treatment records and vet tools organized and accessible.

## Vaccine protection
Dedicated 2–8°C refrigeration with alarm/backup reduces risk of losing temperature-sensitive veterinary products.

## Breeding management
A dedicated AI preparation/record area improves traceability of semen, sire and breeding dates.

## Management
Farm records, monitoring, CCTV and alerts are concentrated in one compact control room.

## Security
Locked medicines and CCTV reduce unauthorized access and loss.

## Space efficiency
All four functions fit within:
```
180 ft² = 16.72 m²
```
without requiring four separate buildings.

## Data quality
Combining records, fridge logs, treatments and CCTV makes the farm easier to audit and improve.
