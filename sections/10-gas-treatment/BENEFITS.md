# Gas Treatment — Benefits

## Engine protection
Removing H₂S and liquid water reduces corrosion risk in generator fuel-system and exhaust components.

## Reliable generator operation
Pressure stabilization and filtration give Section 11 a cleaner, more predictable fuel stream.

## Measured performance
Flow and H₂S monitoring show whether gas quality problems come from:
- digester
- media
- condensate
- holder
- generator demand

## Lower media waste
Lead/lag vessels allow fuller use of the lead bed while the lag protects the engine.

## Maintenance
Twin vessels allow one bed to be serviced while a treated path remains possible, subject to safe valve procedure.

## Energy accounting
Gas meter data allows:
```
m³ biogas → generator runtime → kWh
```
to be reconciled.

## Safety
A dedicated treatment zone concentrates H₂S monitoring, condensate handling and gas isolation away from animals and clean areas.
