# Office + Vet + Biosecurity — Architecture Report

## 1. Design intent
This 12×15 ft room is the farm's **clean control point**. It combines a compact farm office, controlled visitor/staff entry, veterinary/AI preparation and secure medicine/vaccine storage without allowing dirty farm processes to cross through it.

## 2. Geometry
```
12 × 15 = 180 ft²
180 × 0.09290304 = 16.7225 m²
```

At a 10 ft concept clear height:
```
180 × 10 = 1,800 ft³
≈ 50.97 m³
```

## 3. Architectural zones
Use local X length 0–15 ft west→east and Y width 0–12 ft south→north.

### Zone A — Biosecurity entry
```
4 × 12 = 48 ft²
```
Functions:
- visitor/staff entry
- clean/dirty footwear boundary
- bench
- PPE lockers/hooks
- handwash
- visitor log
- external/threshold footbath or disinfectant mat

### Zone B — Office + monitoring
```
5 × 12 = 60 ft²
```
Functions:
- farm records
- computer/monitor
- printer
- CCTV/NVR
- communications/router
- desk for 2 staff
- lockable records

### Zone C — Vet / AI workspace
```
6 × 8 = 48 ft²
```
Functions:
- stainless/workable examination-prep bench
- small sink
- vet instruments
- AI supplies
- sharps container
- biological waste container
- restrained liquid-nitrogen semen tank if used

### Zone D — Medicine + vaccine store
```
6 × 4 = 24 ft²
```
Functions:
- lockable medicine cabinet
- dedicated 2–8°C refrigerator
- temperature logger
- min/max alarm
- stock records
- no pesticides/feed chemicals

Area check:
```
48 + 60 + 48 + 24 = 180 ft²
```

## 4. Entry hierarchy
Concept:
- **west/public door:** visitor/staff entry into biosecurity zone
- **east/farm door:** controlled exit toward clean staff circulation/cow area
- no direct door to manure/digester/fertilizer zone

## 5. Biosecurity threshold
Recommended sequence:
```
outside → footwear/footbath → bench/change → handwash → clean PPE → office/farm side
```

The room is not a shower-in/shower-out facility; the project is too small for that level of compartmentation unless later required.

## 6. Materials
### Biosecurity/vet zones
- non-slip washable floor
- coved skirting where wet cleaning occurs
- washable walls
- moisture-resistant ceiling
- SS sink/workbench where practical
- corrosion-resistant hooks/lockers

### Office
- durable easy-clean floor
- painted washable wall
- compact laminated/metal office furniture
- concealed or organized data/power cabling

### Medicine store
- no direct sun on refrigerator/medicines
- lockable solid door/cabinet
- thermally stable location
- shelf load appropriate for bottles/boxes
- no floor storage

## 7. Ventilation and thermal control
The office/medicine room should stay substantially cleaner and cooler than the cow/manure areas.

Concept:
- 1-ton-class inverter AC for the combined room, ASSUMPTION
- controlled exhaust/ventilation from vet/biosecurity wet area
- medicine store kept away from condenser/heat source
- vaccine refrigerator ventilation clearances per manufacturer

Room volume ≈ 50.97 m³.

At 6 ACH general ventilation:
```
50.97 × 6 = 305.8 m³/h
≈ 180 cfm
```

A 200–250 cfm clean exhaust/ventilation concept is reasonable in addition to AC, subject to final HVAC design.

## 8. Security
Provide:
- lockable public and farm doors
- locked medicine store
- CCTV
- NVR/router in office
- controlled key/access log
- exterior security light if needed

## 9. Liquid nitrogen / artificial insemination
If frozen semen is stored:
- LN₂ tank belongs in the ventilated vet/AI zone
- secure tank against tipping
- do not put it in a sealed unventilated medicine closet
- provide handling PPE
- follow supplier/veterinary guidance
- oxygen-deficiency risk must be reviewed if room ventilation is inadequate

## 10. Adjacency
This section should be near the main entrance and clean circulation, not the waste-energy zone.

## 11. Professional verification
Before construction/operation verify current Bangladesh requirements for veterinary medicine/vaccine handling, occupational safety, electrical work, fire safety and any controlled drug requirements with qualified professionals/authorities.
