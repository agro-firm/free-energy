# 09 — Gas Storage

## Status
Planning baseline. Detailed construction design will be added later.

## Baseline
8 m³ raw gas holder in a 10 × 12 ft zone

## Purpose
Buffers daily biogas production so the generator can run in controlled cycles instead of continuously.

## Inputs
Raw biogas from the digester through primary condensate protection.

## Outputs
Stored raw biogas to the gas-treatment section.

## Interfaces
Digester, gas treatment, controls and gas-safety systems.

## Current design notes
Storage level/pressure should be monitored. Final holder construction, pressure range and safety hardware must follow vendor and engineering requirements.
