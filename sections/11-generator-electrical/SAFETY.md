# Generator + Electrical — Safety Report

## Main hazards
- methane leak
- CO/exhaust
- H₂S residual exposure
- hot exhaust surfaces
- rotating machinery
- electrical shock/arc
- battery short/acid
- fire
- noise
- hot oil
- accidental grid backfeed

## No backfeed
ATS/changeover must be interlocked.
Generator must never energize utility lines.

## Gas
- treated gas only
- NC solenoid fail-closed
- CH₄/LEL detection
- ventilation interlock
- no gas storage in room
- no smoking/open flame

## CO/exhaust
- CO detector
- gas-tight exhaust joints
- outdoor discharge
- insulated hot surfaces
- never operate with exhaust disconnected indoors

## Electrical
- lockable isolators
- breaker coordination
- earthing
- RCD/RCBO where applicable
- safe panel clearances
- arc/shock PPE as appropriate
- licensed electrician

## Battery
- fused leads
- insulated terminals
- secure restraint
- ventilated location
- no tools across terminals

## Fire
Risk review determines extinguisher type/quantity.
Do not store fuel cans, feed or chemicals in room.

## Maintenance
LOTO for:
- generator breaker
- ATS
- gas valve
- starter battery
- ventilation
before service.

## Noise
Use hearing protection during close maintenance if measured noise warrants.

## Hot surfaces
Guard/insulate:
- exhaust
- muffler
- manifold

## Emergency stop
Accessible from outside room and near service position.
