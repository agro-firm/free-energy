# Milk Revenue

## Engineering baseline
140 L/day.

Annual gross:
140 × 365 = 51,100 L/year.

At 97% saleable:
49,567 L/year.

At Tk53/L:
49,567 × 53 = Tk2,627,051/year.

## Market context
Farmgate reference: ~Tk53/L.
Open-market reference: ~Tk55/L.

## Direct sale
Higher price requires real buyer, cold chain, food-safety compliance, delivery/packaging and spoilage cost.

Packaged retail Tk110–115/L is not comparable to farmgate revenue.

## Formula
Revenue =
lactating cows × L/cow/day × saleable % × days × realized price.
