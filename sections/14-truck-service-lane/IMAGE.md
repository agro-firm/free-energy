# Truck / Service Lane — Canonical IMAGE.md

## Fixed geometry
- 14 ft wide ×95 ft long
- one continuous flush lane
- outer-edge drain
- loading side adjacent farm sections
- no U-turn
- one truck at a time

## Design truck
Tata LPT 709-class:
- ~22.6 ft long maximum reference
- ~7.07 ft wide
- 7.5 t GVW

## Stationing
0–10 entry
10–35 milk
35–60 feed
60–70 clear
70–95 rear utility/fertilizer

## Required images
- true top full lane
- gate/entry
- reverse-in sequence
- centered-truck clearance
- milk pickup
- feed unloading
- fertilizer loading
- rear maintenance
- cross-slope/drain
- pavement cutaway
- night lighting
- emergency-clear lane
- full-site context

## Never show
- two trucks passing
- U-turn inside lane
- pallets permanently stored
- blocked drain
- truck driving through cow areas
- fertilizer spill into storm drain
- overhead obstruction below planned clear envelope

## QA
Lane must remain 14 ft ×95 ft and route must be physically coherent with Sections 03, 06 and 12.
