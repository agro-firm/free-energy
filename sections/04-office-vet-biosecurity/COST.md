## High-case drivers
- premium pharmacy refrigerator
- higher-grade interior finishes
- larger IT/CCTV system
- premium UPS/battery
- imported AI/vet equipment
- custom SS cabinetry
- HVAC/ventilation upgrade

## Exclusions
Farm-wide server/IT platform, cows, medicine stock, vaccines, recurring LN₂ supply, service lane, cow shed, main water system, professional/regulatory fees unless quoted.

## Procurement
Obtain separate quotes for:
- building/civil
- office fitout
- vaccine refrigerator
- CCTV/network
- UPS/electrical
- AC/ventilation
- vet/AI equipment

Do not choose a domestic refrigerator for vaccines solely because it is cheaper; use equipment appropriate to the required temperature control and monitoring.
