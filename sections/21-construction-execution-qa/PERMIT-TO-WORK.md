# Permit-to-Work

## Permit types
- excavation.
- hot work.
- confined space.
- electrical energization/LOTO.
- work at height.
- lifting/critical lift.
- gas commissioning.
- pressure test.
- line breaking.

## Permit contents
- location.
- scope.
- hazards.
- isolation.
- test/monitoring.
- PPE.
- competent persons.
- validity.
- issuer/receiver.
- closeout.

## Rule
Permit expiry or changed conditions stop work.
