# Warranty and Spares

## Warranty register
Record:
- start event.
- duration.
- exclusions.
- response time.
- parts/labor.
- travel.
- service center.

## Mandatory critical spares
Vendor-specific for:
- scraper.
- slurry pumps.
- solenoid/level sensors.
- gas valves.
- H₂S media.
- generator ignition/service parts.
- screw press wear parts.
- water pump seals.
- solar inverter/SPD if justified.

## Consumables
Separate OPEX:
- H₂S media.
- filters.
- oil.
- bags.
- calibration gas.
- chemicals.

## Rule
A cheap imported unit with no replacement part/service path may have higher evaluated lifecycle cost.
