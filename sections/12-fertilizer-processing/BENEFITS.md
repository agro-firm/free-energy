# Fertilizer Processing — Benefits

## Volume reduction
Screw pressing converts a continuously wet digestate stream into:
- concentrated solids
- pumpable liquid

## Easier handling
Solid cake can be dried/bagged rather than transporting all digestate as liquid.

## Nutrient recovery
Both fractions retain plant nutrients, subject to testing.

## Site efficiency
A 16×22 ft roofed area can process the farm's entire ~0.54 m³/day digestate flow.

## Business
Bagged product may become a saleable output only after:
- lab analysis
- compliant specification
- current registration/approval
- realistic market validation

## Agronomy
Liquid digestate can reduce purchased fertilizer needs when applied according to crop nutrient requirements.

## Data
Mass balance allows:
```
digestate in → cake + liquid → product + field use
```
tracking instead of guessing fertilizer yield.
