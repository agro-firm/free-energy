# Training and Competency

## Induction
All:
- site zones.
- hygiene.
- animal behavior.
- PPE.
- alarms.
- emergency.
- lane safety.

## Livestock operator
Competent in:
- milking hygiene.
- health observation.
- treatment-withdrawal identification.
- feeding/records.
- biosecurity.

## Utility technician
Competent in:
- pumps.
- PLC/alarm basics.
- water.
- slurry.
- digester.
- gas detector.
- generator.
- solar monitoring.
- LOTO.

Specialist work remains licensed/vendor scope.

## Manager
- records.
- inventory.
- OPEX/KPI.
- veterinary coordination.
- compliance.
- emergency leadership.

## Refresh
- after incident.
- after major equipment change.
- annual core refresh.
- vendor refresh per manual.
