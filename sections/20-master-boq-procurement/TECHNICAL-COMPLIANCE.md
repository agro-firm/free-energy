# Technical Compliance Evaluation

## Step 1 — mandatory pass/fail
Examples:
- capacity.
- material.
- voltage/phase.
- hazardous rating.
- pressure/flow.
- environmental rating.
- dimensions.
- warranty.
- local service.

A failed mandatory requirement requires clarification/rejection; price cannot cure a critical technical failure.

## Step 2 — deviations
Classify:
- ACCEPTABLE.
- CLARIFICATION.
- COMMERCIAL ADDER.
- TECHNICAL REJECT.
- DESIGN CHANGE.

## Step 3 — interface compliance
Vendor must state:
- foundation load.
- footprint.
- service clearance.
- pipe/nozzle size.
- power kW.
- start current.
- control protocol.
- drain.
- ventilation.
- consumables.

## Step 4 — lifecycle
Check:
- spare availability.
- media/consumables.
- calibration.
- service capability.
- expected replacement interval.

## Output
Technical evaluation is signed before commercial recommendation.
