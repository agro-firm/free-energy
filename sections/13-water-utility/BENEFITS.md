# Water Utility — Benefits

## Resilience
10 m³ storage provides roughly 2.6 design days.

## Animal welfare
Drinking water is prioritized even during outages.

## Milk hygiene
Dedicated clean-water supply supports sanitary milk handling.

## Process control
Metered digester water improves slurry consistency.

## Efficiency
Main meter + branch meters allow leak and overuse detection.

## Power resilience
2 m³ OHT gives roughly 12.7 hours of design-demand volume if noncritical use is reduced.
