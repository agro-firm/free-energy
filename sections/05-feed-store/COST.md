# Feed Store — Cost and Budget Report

Bangladesh planning estimate, October 2026. Section 05 only.

  https://www.billalsteel.com/blogs/steel-structure-building-cost-in-bangladesh-2026

  https://www.bdstall.com/display-rack/

  https://www.bdstall.com/weight-scale-machine/

  https://www.startech.com.bd/dehumidifier

## Major cost drivers
- building envelope/weatherproofing
- floor/DPM/flood level
- rack specification
- dehumidification requirement
- optional mixer/automation
- pest/fire upgrades
- current steel price

## Exclusions
Section 06 canopy, fresh forage inventory, feed delivery vehicle, cows, nutritionist services, feed mill/grinder, silage bunker, land, taxes/duties unless quoted.
