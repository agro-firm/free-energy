## Important
The gross total contains:
- section-level contingencies.
- allowances.
- vendor assumptions.
- some shared-scope candidates.

It does not mean every line will become a separate PO.

## No double contingency
Section 20 adds no extra blanket percentage.

A management reserve may only be added later if the owner approves a separately defined risk reserve.

## Cost status hierarchy
1. ASSUMPTION.
2. BUDGET ALLOWANCE.
3. BUDGETARY QUOTE.
4. FIRM QUOTE.
5. PURCHASE ORDER / CONTRACT.
6. COMMITTED.
7. INVOICED.
8. PAID.
9. FORECAST AT COMPLETION.

## Currency
Base reporting currency:
BDT.

Foreign quotes record:
- original currency.
- quote date.
- owner-approved FX conversion date/rate.
- validity.
- hedging/LC impact if applicable.

## Exclusions
Never assume:
- VAT.
- import duty.
- AIT/AT.
- SD/RD.
- freight.
- clearing.
are included unless explicitly stated in the quote.

## Budget updates
When a firm quote replaces a section allowance:
- preserve original budget.
- record latest forecast.
- record variance.
- record reason.
