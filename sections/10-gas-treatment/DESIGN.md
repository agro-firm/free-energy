# Gas Treatment — Process Design

## 1. Gas-quality problem
Raw biogas contains:
- methane
- CO₂
- water vapor
- H₂S
- possible aerosols/particles

Water + H₂S can create highly corrosive conditions in downstream engine/exhaust equipment.

## 2. Treatment sequence
1. bulk condensate knockout
2. lead H₂S adsorption/oxidation vessel
3. lag polishing H₂S vessel
4. fine coalescing moisture separator
5. particulate filter
6. flow meter
7. booster/regulator if required
8. generator

## 3. H₂S media
Baseline planning:
- impregnated activated carbon or iron-oxide/metal-oxide media
- supplier guarantees H₂S capacity and outlet concentration
- two 25 kg beds

Do not use ordinary drinking-water activated carbon as the design media simply because it is inexpensive.

## 4. Inlet H₂S
Until measured:
- base sizing = 2,000 ppm
- sensitivity = 1,000–3,000 ppm

Actual Section 08 gas must be sampled during commissioning.

## 5. Outlet target
Planning:
- ≤100 ppm H₂S maximum
- preferred ≤50 ppm if Section 11 generator vendor requires

Generator vendor specification always controls.

## 6. Lead/lag operation
Normal:
```
A lead → B lag
```

When A approaches breakthrough:
- B protects outlet
- replace/regenerate A
- return A as lag
- switch B to lead

This uses media more efficiently and prevents immediate untreated breakthrough.

## 7. Bulk moisture
KO-401 removes liquid water upstream.
Gas piping slopes to KO/drain points.

## 8. Fine moisture
MS-401 removes entrained droplets after H₂S treatment.

Optional desiccant:
- silica gel/alumina/molecular sieve only if generator dewpoint requires
- installed after H₂S vessels
- sized from actual humidity/dewpoint

## 9. Media dust
PF-401 after scrubbers protects:
- flow meter
- booster
- generator regulator

Planning filtration around 5 µm, vendor dependent.

## 10. Pressure drop
Because Section 09 is a very-low-pressure holder, the passive skid must have low pressure drop.

Design goal:
- oversized vessels
- DN40 line
- low face velocity
- clean filters
- differential-pressure monitoring

Exact allowable total pressure drop follows holder and booster/vendor data.

## 11. Booster
A gas-compatible low-pressure blower is installed downstream of cleanup only if required.

Benefits:
- blower sees cleaner/drier gas
- less corrosion
- controlled generator inlet pressure

Protection:
- Section 09 low-level/vacuum inhibit
- suction pressure low trip
- outlet pressure high trip
- gas detector interlock

## 12. Flow meter
Do not use electromagnetic liquid meters for gas.
Use:
- thermal mass
- positive-displacement
- rotary/other low-flow gas meter
compatible with biogas.

## 13. No bypass
There is no normal untreated bypass to Section 11.
