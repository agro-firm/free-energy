# Major Vendor Data Requirements

## Milk chiller
- capacity.
- pull-down time.
- ambient rating.
- compressor power/start current.
- SS grade.
- agitator.
- service.

## Scraper/slurry
- solids size.
- pump curve.
- materials.
- seal.
- motor.
- controls.

## Digester
- total/working volume.
- pressure.
- gas tightness.
- civil loads.
- P/V protection.
- startup support.

## Gas holder
- membrane material.
- allowable pressure/vacuum.
- connector details.
- volume sensor.
- warranty/permeability.

## H₂S system
- media capacity.
- inlet H₂S.
- outlet guarantee.
- pressure drop.
- media cost.

## Generator
- biogas composition.
- H₂S max.
- gas pressure/flow.
- output derating.
- exhaust backpressure.
- cooling air.
- service intervals.

## Screw press
- feed capacity.
- cake solids.
- capture.
- power.
- wear parts.

## Solar
- approved model.
- dimensions.
- electrical characteristics.
- structural loads.
- warranty.
- inverter compatibility.
