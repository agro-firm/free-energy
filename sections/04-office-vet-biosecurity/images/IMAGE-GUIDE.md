## Photoreal prompt
Create a realistic compact 12×15 ft farm office + veterinary + biosecurity room in Bangladesh for PLANT-V1.0. West entrance is a 4×12 ft biosecurity change zone with bench, boots, PPE and handwash. Center is a 5×12 ft office/monitoring zone with desk, computer, CCTV monitor/NVR and records. East side contains a 6×8 ft vet/artificial-insemination work area with sink/workbench and a 6×4 ft locked medicine/vaccine store with a professional 2–8°C refrigerator. Clean, practical, washable farm-management interior. No manure, feed, fertilizer, generator or biogas equipment.

## Blueprint prompt
Orthographic 15×12 ft plan with exact four zones and dimensions. Show public west door, farm east door, internal medicine-store door, sinks, fridge, desk, CCTV/NVR, LN₂ tank position, utilities and XYZ axes.

## Biosecurity image
Show outside shoes/footbath, change bench, clean boots/PPE, handwash and clean-side transition. Do not show a decorative reception lobby.

## Vet/AI image
Show workbench, sink, sealed instruments, sharps container, AI supplies and a restrained cryogenic semen tank with ventilation awareness. Do not show major surgery.

## Medicine image
Show lockable cabinet, labeled shelves, 2–8°C refrigerator, independent logger and alarm. No food or pesticide storage.

## Utility overlay

## Negative prompt
