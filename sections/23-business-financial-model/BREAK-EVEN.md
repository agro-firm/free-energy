# Break-Even Analysis

## Formula
Break-even milk price =
(OPEX - realized energy value - fertilizer value - other nonmilk cash revenue)
/
saleable milk litres.

## Conservative
≈ Tk170.41/L.

## Base
≈ Tk102.72/L.

## Upside / target
≈ Tk56.47/L.

## Interpretation
The base break-even price is far above current farmgate references because milk volume is low relative to purchased feed and fixed operating scope.

## Biggest levers
1. saleable L/day.
2. feed/forage cost.
3. realized milk price.
4. labor/fixed OPEX.
5. capital/phasing.
6. energy value.
7. fertilizer value.

Never reduce welfare, nutrition, hygiene or maintenance just to force a spreadsheet break-even.
