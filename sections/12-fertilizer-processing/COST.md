## Operating costs
Main recurring:
- separator electricity
- bags
- sewing thread
- lab testing
- media/PPE
- pump/fan maintenance
- transport
- registration/compliance
- labor

## Revenue caution
Do not use a fertilizer selling price in the business plan until:
- product yield is measured
- product is tested
- registration/commercial compliance is clear
- actual buyers/prices are validated
