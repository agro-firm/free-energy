# Water Utility — Construction

## Sequence
1. survey/flood level
2. soil/groundwater check
3. excavate ground tank
4. RCC base/walls/roof
5. waterproof/potable coating
6. hydrotest
7. backfill
8. construct pump bay
9. construct OHT tower
10. install OHT
11. install pumps/manifold
12. pipe branches
13. electrical/control
14. treatment equipment
15. flushing/disinfection
16. water-quality testing
17. commissioning

## Hold points
- tank rebar/waterstop
- waterproofing
- hydrotest
- tower structural inspection
- overflow/vent
- pump alignment
- backflow protection
- electrical/earthing
- final disinfection/test
