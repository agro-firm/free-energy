# Feed Cost Model

## Base purchased-feed assumption
Concentrate:
60 kg/day × Tk72.5 ×365 = Tk1,587,750/year.

Dry roughage:
80 × Tk20 ×365 = Tk584,000/year.

Fresh forage/silage:
300 × Tk8.5 ×365 = Tk930,750/year.

## Total
Tk3,102,500/year.

## Feed cost relative to engineering milk
51,100 L/year gross engineering milk.

Tk3,102,500 / 51,100 = Tk60.71 per planning litre before labor, medicine, maintenance, transport or finance.

## Business implication
Current purchased-feed references can exceed current farmgate milk price on a per-liter basis at the 140 L/day production baseline.

## Levers
- higher milk yield with animal-health protection.
- own/contract lower-cost forage.
- lower feed waste.
- professional ration balancing.
- improved lactation rate.

Never reduce nutrition just to improve spreadsheet cost.
