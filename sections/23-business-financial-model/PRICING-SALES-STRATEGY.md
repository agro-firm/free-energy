# Pricing and Sales Strategy

## Milk channels
### Processor/cooperative
Pros:
- stable offtake.
- simpler logistics.

Risks:
- lower price.
- quality/fat conditions.
- payment terms.

### Local bulk buyer
Potentially higher price.
Verify reliability, cold chain and payment.

### Direct retail/value added
Potentially higher realized price but adds:
- food-safety compliance.
- packaging.
- delivery.
- marketing.
- labor.
- spoilage.
- regulatory requirements.

Do not compare packaged retail Tk110–115/L with farmgate Tk53/L without adding all downstream costs.

## Fertilizer
Start with farm use/trials or verified wholesale buyer after product testing/approval.

## Energy
Prioritize self-consumption where it creates higher real avoided cost, subject to technical operating constraints.
