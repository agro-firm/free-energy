# 01 — Cow Shed

## Status
Planning baseline. Detailed construction design will be added later.

## Baseline
26 × 48 ft; capacity 20 adult cows

## Purpose
Main animal housing and production area.

## Inputs
Feed, drinking water, ventilation air, electricity, cows.

## Outputs
Milk to the clean milk process and manure to the automatic waste process.

## Interfaces
Feed store, milk room, cow yard, manure automation, water system, rooftop solar.

## Current design notes
Includes feed alley/manger, water access, washable non-slip floor, manure gutters/scrapers, ventilation/fans and temporary treatment/calving use within the stall system.
