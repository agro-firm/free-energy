# Section 18 — Canonical IMAGE.md

## Purpose
Visualize levels/drainage without inventing surveyed data.

## Required labels
Every pre-survey level image must say:
**PROVISIONAL DESIGN LEVEL FRAMEWORK — FIELD RL PENDING**

## Required image categories
- topographic survey point plan.
- benchmark diagram.
- DCF-1 with spot-level placeholders.
- finished-floor freeboard diagram.
- service-lane crossfall.
- stormwater catchment map.
- clean/dirty drainage map.
- outfall/HGL section.
- earthwork cut/fill concept.
- road/gate level section.
- utility crossing section.
- IFC gate diagram.
- as-built survey diagram.

## Never show
- fake measured contours.
- fake flood RL.
- fake road/outfall invert.
- arbitrary final longitudinal slope.
- clean and dirty drains combined.
