## Basis

## Why installed cost is much higher than the membrane
The holder itself is only one component. A safe installed system also needs:
- protected enclosure
- slab/anchors
- gas-rated valves
- flexible connectors
- condensate management
- pressure/vacuum safety
- gas detection
- level sensing
- PLC integration
- bonding/lightning
- leak testing
- hazardous-area design/commissioning

## Double-membrane upgrade
A custom industrial double-membrane solution may cost substantially more due to:
- support-air blower
- outer weather membrane
- pressure-control equipment
- engineered anchors
- proprietary level sensor
- larger minimum standard sizes

Treat this as a vendor alternative, not the base estimate.
