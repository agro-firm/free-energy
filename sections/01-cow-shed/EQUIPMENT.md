## 3. Stall hardware requirements

Final stall hardware should consider:
- actual breed and body weight
- neck-rail or restraint type
- safe head access to manger
- no sharp projections
- corrosion resistance
- removable or replaceable sections
- compatibility with temporary treatment/calving use at selected end stalls

## 4. Manure scraper requirements

Vendor package should define:
- scraper blade width
- cable/chain/rope drive
- motor power
- travel speed
- end switches
- emergency stop
- overload/jam detection
- corrosion-resistant components
- cleaning/inspection access
- allowable floor slope

Do not buy scraper equipment before the floor and gutter detail is coordinated.

## 5. Fans

Vendor must provide:
- airflow
- diameter
- motor power
- mounting height
- guard
- noise
- ingress protection
- corrosion resistance

Do not select fans by wattage only.

## 6. Troughs

Vendor must provide:
- bowl/linear dimensions
- refill valve capacity
- flow rate
- drain/cleaning method
- material
- mounting
- animal access

## 7. Lighting

Recommended characteristics:
- efficient LED
- moisture-resistant
- dust-resistant
- protected against impact
- accessible for cleaning
- neutral/white inspection light

Final lux target should be selected for animal welfare and work task.

## 8. Optional cow brush

A powered cow brush is optional, not included in the core process.

Public Bangladesh listings in 2026 show automatic cow brushes around Tk 65,000–85,000; verify current vendor price before purchase.

## 9. Spare parts

Keep planned spares for:
- scraper wear blades
- chain/cable parts
- limit switches
- contactors/overloads
- fan belts/bearings if applicable
- trough valves
- LED drivers
- water valves
- sensor fuses

## 10. Equipment approval rule

Every equipment submittal must be checked against:
- available footprint
- electrical supply
- water supply
- corrosion environment
- washdown exposure
- animal safety
- maintenance/removal path
- integration with Section 07 manure system
