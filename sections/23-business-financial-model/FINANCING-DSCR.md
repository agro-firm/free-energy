# Financing and DSCR

## Inputs
- debt share.
- equity share.
- annual interest.
- tenor.
- grace.
- repayment style.
- fees.
- security/collateral.
- working-capital facility.
- interest during construction.

All remain INPUT until a lender term sheet exists.

## Debt service
Use actual bank amortization.

## DSCR
DSCR = CFADS / annual debt service.

CFADS =
EBITDA
- cash tax
- sustaining CAPEX
- required working-capital increase.

## Debt capacity
Do not size debt from asset value alone.

If base EBITDA is negative:
project operating cash flow cannot service debt without other revenue/support.

## Covenant
Target DSCR is lender-specific; no generic covenant is hard-coded.

## Decision
Obtain real financing terms before final investment approval.
