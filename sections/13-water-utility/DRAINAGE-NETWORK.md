# Water Utility — Drainage Network

## Clean
- roof water
- clean tank overflow
- clean external stormwater

## Controlled utility drain
- tank cleaning
- filter backwash
- pump-bay wash
- treatment reject water

Destination depends on water-treatment chemistry.

## Dirty networks remain separate
Section 13 must not accept:
- manure drain
- milk-room chemical wastewater
- digestate
- fertilizer leachate

## Tank overflows
Both GT-701 and OHT-701:
- visible
- screened
- routed away from foundations
- sized to exceed fill rate

## Tank drain
Provide low-point drain for maintenance.
Do not discharge where it floods service lane or process zones.
