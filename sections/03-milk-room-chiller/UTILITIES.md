# Milk Room + Chiller — Utilities and MEP

## Potable cold water
Supply from Section 13.
Concept:
- 25 mm class cold-water header
- branches to handwash, sink/CIP, bulk-tank wash and hose
- backflow prevention
- isolation valves
- water meter preferred

All milk-contact cleaning water must be potable-quality.

## Hot water
Planning target: ~125 L/day at high cleaning temperature.
Concept system:
- 150 L insulated storage heater
- ~5 kW element
- thermostatic control
- scald protection
- hot-water loop to wash/CIP area
- energy scheduling during solar-rich period where practical

## Warm water
Provide controlled mixing for ~40°C cleaning steps where required.

## Milk piping
Use food-grade sanitary SS304 pipe/hose with sanitary fittings.
Planning:
- 1–1.5 inch sanitary transfer size, VENDOR
- tri-clamp or equivalent hygienic connections
- no threaded internal milk-contact joints
- pipe slope/drainability
- capped ends
- no dead legs where possible

## Refrigeration
Vendor-specific:
- compressor
- condenser
- evaporator
- refrigerant lines
- condensate drain

If condenser is outdoors, route through sealed wall sleeves to shaded service location.

## Wastewater
Milk-room wastewater contains:
- milk residue
- detergents
- sanitizers
- hot water

Do **not** automatically route this stream into the anaerobic digester.

Route to:
- sanitary/process wastewater collection
- neutralization/controlled disposal or treatment
- only to digester if chemistry/process review explicitly approves

## Floor drains
Concept:
- 2 trapped, cleanable drains
- 75 mm class local drains ASSUMPTION
- 100 mm class main wastewater line ASSUMPTION
- removable stainless grates
- rod/flush access

## Electrical
Dedicated subpanel.
Separate:
- chiller
- hot-water heater
- milk pump
- CIP pump
- lighting
- ventilation
- outlets/testing
- controls/data

Provide critical-load contactor/load shedding so hot water cannot overload backup generation.

## Backup power
Priority backup circuit:
chiller + controller + essential pump + lights + ventilation.

Compressor starting current must be verified against generator/inverter capability. Soft-start may be required.

## Data
Recommended:
- milk temperature sensor
- tank temperature logger
- high-temperature alarm
- door contact
- power-fail alarm
- energy meter
- water meter
- optional milk-volume/flow meter
- CCTV at dispatch door

## Gas
**No raw or treated biogas pipe, gas holder or combustion equipment inside this room.**
