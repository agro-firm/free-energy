# Cow Yard — Functional Design

## Operating concept
The yard is a compact exercise/holding space for **one 10-cow group at a time**.

Two groups:
- Group A: cows 01–10
- Group B: cows 11–20

## Functional zoning
### Shaded zone — 12 × 18 ft
- 216 ft²
- 50% of active area
- resting/heat-relief area

### Open zone — 12 × 18 ft
- 216 ft²
- movement/exercise
- direct sunlight when weather permits

### Service/drain zone — 4 × 18 ft
- water trough
- hose point
- dirty drain
- maintenance access

## Floor
Use reinforced concrete with:
- non-slip broom/grooved texture
- rounded transitions
- no sharp drain edges
- slope toward dirty drain
- sufficient strength for cow hoof loading and washdown

## Fencing
Concept:
- 5 ft high GI/galvanized fence
- smooth internal face
- no exposed wire ends
- posts outside animal-impact zone where possible
- lower rail/curb if required to protect mesh

## Gates
Cattle gate:
- concept width 8 ft
- strong latch
- no pinch points
- direct relation to shed

Service gate:
- concept width 4 ft
- staff/equipment access

## Water
One 150–200 L class trough is a planning concept. Final refill rate and trough count must be selected for actual herd behavior.

## Shade
The shade structure should not trap heat. Keep sides fully open and use a light/reflective roof or livestock shade material.

## Lighting
Only low-intensity operational/security lighting is needed:
- two protected LED fixtures/floodlights concept
- no decorative lighting

## Interior/yard image character
Images should show:
- realistic cattle movement
- 10 cows maximum in the yard at one time
- clean shaded and open halves
- visible trough
- safe fence/gates
- drainage strip
- connection to cow shed where camera permits
