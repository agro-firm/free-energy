# Gas Storage — Architecture and Site Concept

## 1. Design intent
Section 09 stores one near-day's worth of raw biogas in a **very-low-pressure flexible membrane holder** protected from sun, animals, tools, rodents and accidental contact by a ventilated galvanized frame.

It is not a pressure tank and it is not a sealed building.

## 2. Zone geometry
```
10 × 12 = 120 ft²
120 × 0.09290304 = 11.1484 m²
```

Local:
- X=0–12 ft west→east
- Y=0–10 ft south→north
- Section 08 inlet from west
- Section 10 outlet to east
- emergency vent/flare interface toward north/safe direction
- service/control access south/east

## 3. Membrane envelope
Nominal 8 m³.

Planning equivalent inflated envelope:
```
2.0 × 2.0 × 2.0
=8.0 m³
```

Real flexible-bag geometry will be rounded/pillow/cylindrical and vendor-specific. The 2×2×2 m box is a **maximum planning envelope**, not a claim that the membrane forms a rigid cube.

## 4. Protective frame
Concept:
- galvanized steel cage/weather frame
- external footprint about 2.4×2.4 m
- height about 2.4 m
- removable/lockable mesh panels
- roof/sunshade above membrane without trapping gas
- rodent-resistant lower protection
- no sharp edges touching membrane
- no worker normally enters cage while gas-filled

## 5. Fit
Zone:
```
3.658 m × 3.048 m
```

Protective frame:
```
2.4 m × 2.4 m
```

Remaining length:
```
3.658-2.4
=1.258 m
```

This becomes the primary gas-manifold/control/service strip.

Remaining width:
```
3.048-2.4
=0.648 m
```

The enclosure is therefore not intended for full walk-around access on both narrow sides; frame panels must be removable and all routine service points should face the accessible manifold side.

## 6. Foundation
Concept:
- reinforced concrete slab approximately 10×12 ft
- raised above surrounding grade
- anchor points for protective frame
- smooth membrane-safe interior
- no sharp drain grates under bag
- perimeter stormwater diverted away

Final slab thickness and anchoring depend on wind/cyclone, frame loads and site soil.

## 7. Weather
The frame/shade protects membrane from:
- UV
- direct rain impact
- falling tools/debris
- animal contact

The enclosure remains naturally ventilated.

## 8. Ventilation
Do not construct solid walls around the gas bag.
Use:
- mesh/expanded-metal security panels
- open high/low air paths
- no enclosed ceiling pocket
- no storage around holder

## 9. Separation
No:
- feed
- milk
- fertilizer bags
- fuel
- electrical battery storage
- hot work
- smoking
inside the gas-storage zone.

## 10. Hazardous-area design
Final hazardous-area extent around membrane, valves, vent and relief discharge is not defined by this conceptual document. A qualified gas/electrical engineer must establish classification and equipment requirements.
