# Fire Safety Plan Content

The formal submission/fire plan should include:

## Site
- 65×95 boundary.
- road/gate and road width.
- Section 14 emergency approach.
- north arrow.
- adjacent properties/uses.
- assembly point.
- Fire Service access.

## Buildings/process
- all section numbers/names.
- exits.
- doors.
- occupancy/workers.
- hazardous materials.
- gas equipment.
- electrical rooms.
- solar shutdown equipment.

## Fire protection
- alarm panel.
- detectors.
- call points.
- horns/strobes.
- extinguishers.
- emergency/exit lights.
- gas detectors.
- E-stops.
- isolation valves.
- fire-water/hydrant system if required.

## Emergency
- escape arrows.
- assembly area.
- emergency contacts.
- fire-service access.
- shutoff locations.

## Supporting
- layout plan.
- fire-fighting floor plan.
- land documents.
- location/Google map.
- equipment datasheets/certificates.
