# Business Risk Register

| Risk | Financial effect | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Milk price below plan | revenue down | buyer diversification/contracts |
| Milk yield below plan | revenue down sharply | genetics/nutrition/herd health |
| Feed inflation | OPEX up | forage production/contracts/ration efficiency |
| Monsoon fodder loss | milk down/feed up | silage reserve/supply plan |
| Disease | milk down/vet up | DLS/vet biosecurity |
| Chiller failure | milk loss | PM/rapid backup response |
| Gas underproduction | energy value down | process monitoring |
| H2S high | media OPEX up | measurement/media sizing |
| Fertilizer approval delay | revenue delayed | value at zero until approved |
| Tariff/net-meter change | energy value changes | self-consumption sensitivity |
| CAPEX overrun | return worsens | Section 20 controls |
| Debt cost high | DSCR/cash flow worsens | term sheet before FID |
| Working-capital shortage | operational disruption | reserve/credit line |
| Buyer late payment | WC increases | payment terms/credit control |
