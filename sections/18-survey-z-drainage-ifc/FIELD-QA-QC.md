# Field QA / QC

## Before survey
- verify instrument calibration.
- inspect control points.
- agree coordinate/datum convention.

## During survey
- close leveling loop.
- repeat TBM observations.
- record backsight/foresight or digital-level report.
- code features consistently.
- photograph outfall/flood evidence.

## Independent checks
- boundary diagonal.
- P0–P3 dimensions.
- D0 repeat level.
- random 10% point resurvey.
- road/gate cross-section repeat.

## Construction
At each stage verify:
- excavation formation.
- blinding.
- foundation top.
- drain invert.
- slab/FFL.
- lane subgrade/pavement.
- final as-built.

## Nonconformance
Any deviation affecting:
- drainage.
- service lane.
- hygiene.
- gas safety.
- equipment fit.
must go through change control before covering work.
