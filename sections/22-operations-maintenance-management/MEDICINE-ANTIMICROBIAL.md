# Medicine / Vaccine / Antimicrobial SOP

## Storage
- locked medicine store.
- vaccine fridge 2–8°C.
- temperature log/alarm.
- FEFO.
- segregate expired/damaged stock.

## Administration record
- animal ID.
- drug/vaccine.
- dose.
- route.
- lot/batch.
- expiry.
- date/time.
- administrator.
- veterinarian instruction.
- withdrawal period.

## Antimicrobials
Follow current DLS antimicrobial-stewardship guidance.

Rules:
- veterinary indication.
- correct diagnosis/dose/duration.
- avoid unnecessary antimicrobial use.
- observe milk/meat withdrawal.
- do not sell withdrawal milk.

## Expired medicine
Quarantine and dispose through approved route.
