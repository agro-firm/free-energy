# Emergency Response Plan

## Emergency contacts
- Bangladesh Fire Service hotline: **102**.
- additional local station/contact details: insert after survey/licensing.
- site manager.
- electrician.
- gas/process engineer.
- veterinarian.
- ambulance/medical contacts.

## Scenario 1 — gas leak
1. alarm.
2. stop ignition.
3. no phone/switching inside hazard unless rated.
4. isolate if safe.
5. evacuate.
6. call Fire Service if significant/uncontrolled.
7. test atmosphere.
8. re-entry only by authorized decision.

## Scenario 2 — gas fire
1. alarm/evacuate.
2. isolate gas source if safely possible.
3. call Fire Service.
4. protect exposures.
5. do not extinguish flowing-gas flame unless gas can be stopped/controlled.

## Scenario 3 — H₂S
1. immediate evacuation.
2. move cross/upwind.
3. call emergency help.
4. rescue only trained/equipped personnel.
5. ventilate/test.

## Scenario 4 — generator/electrical
- E-stop.
- isolate gas/electric if safe.
- appropriate extinguisher.
- no water on live electrical.

## Scenario 5 — feed/solid fire
- alarm.
- isolate electrical.
- portable first aid only if incipient and safe.
- Fire Service.

## Scenario 6 — person injured
- first aid.
- emergency medical response.
- preserve scene.
