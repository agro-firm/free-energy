# Fire Water Review

## Existing Section 13 water
- 8 m³ ground.
- 2 m³ overhead.
- total 10 m³.

This is designed for farm drinking/process resilience.

## Rule
**Do not count any of the 10 m³ as dedicated fire-water reserve unless the approved fire engineering design explicitly allocates it and confirms that farm consumption cannot deplete the required reserve.**

## Authority decision required
Fire plan must determine whether this project needs:
- portable extinguishers only.
- hose reel.
- hydrant.
- dedicated fire-water tank.
- fire pump.
- jockey pump.
- ring main.
- sprinkler/other fixed system.

## If dedicated fire water is required
Create Section 13A / future package with:
- fire-flow demand.
- duration.
- usable reserve.
- pump duty/standby.
- emergency power.
- hydrant/hose locations.
- test header.
- fire-service inlet if required.

## No invented capacity
No hydrant flow or reserve is frozen in Section 19.
