# Feed Unloading Canopy — Functional Design

## 1. Logistics philosophy
The canopy is a **transfer space**, not storage.

The design minimizes:
- feed exposure to rain
- worker carrying distance
- truck dwell time
- conflict with Section 14 traffic
- wet feed entering Section 05

## 2. Main unloading zone
10×10 ft central zone.

Use for:
- bagged concentrate
- palletized feed after safe lowering to ground
- dry roughage transfer
- hand trolley / pallet truck movement

Keep this zone empty between deliveries.

## 3. Fresh-forage staging
4×10 ft west zone.

Use for:
- same-day chopped/green forage
- max ~600 kg / 2-day emergency staging
- tarpaulin/bin/rack isolation from wet ground
- no permanent silage fermentation

After staging:
```
canopy → feed preparation → cows
```

Do not carry wet forage into dry Section 05 unless it is packaged/approved dry storage material.

## 4. Inspection/safety zone
4×10 ft east zone.

Contains:
- wheel chocks
- folding trolley
- PPE/high-vis storage
- delivery paperwork/checklist
- broom
- fire/safety item
- temporary damaged-bag isolation bin

## 5. Truck parking
Truck stays in the 14-ft lane, parallel to canopy.

Preferred worker zone is the ~4-ft gap between truck and canopy.

No worker is allowed in this gap while truck is moving.

## 6. Manual bag unloading
For 25–50 kg bags:
- use two-person lift where required
- platform trolley
- avoid twisting
- stack immediately onto trolley/pallet
- transfer to Section 05 receiving area

## 7. Palletized unloading
Hand pallet truck is only for level movement.

If pallet arrives on truck bed:
- truck tail lift, forklift or approved lift equipment must lower it
- then pallet truck moves it across apron

## 8. Dry roughage unloading
- keep bales covered during rain
- stage only long enough to inspect
- move to dry Section 05 zone
- reject wet/moldy bales

## 9. Rain operation
- truck positions close to canopy
- roof shelters transfer path
- clean roof water to gutter
- apron water to outer trench
- stop unloading if wind-driven rain wets feed significantly

## 10. Surface markings
Recommended:
- yellow unloading box
- red no-storage strip around feed-store door
- wheel-chock position marks
- pedestrian safe side
- keep-clear drain marking

## 11. Night lighting
Provide two LED/floodlights:
- one toward unloading face
- one at feed-store door
- avoid blinding driver

## 12. Security
Optional CCTV covers:
- truck plate/arrival
- unloading face
- feed-store receiving door
