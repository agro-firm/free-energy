# CBS / WBS / Procurement-Package Mapping

## CBS
Cost Breakdown Structure answers:
**Where was money budgeted?**

CBS currently follows Sections 01–19.

## WBS
Work Breakdown Structure answers:
**What work is physically executed?**

Examples:
- earthworks.
- RCC.
- steel.
- plumbing.
- process equipment.
- electrical.
- controls.

## PPK
Procurement Package answers:
**Who is contracted to deliver it?**

One PPK can contain several section budgets.

## Example
Section 09 gas holder:
- slab/civil may be PPK-01/02.
- membrane holder/valves may be PPK-07.
- common earthing may be PPK-08.
- area detector/fire system may be PPK-12.

## Rule
Do not force every section into one vendor if it creates poor technical interfaces or duplicated preliminaries.

## Traceability
Every PO line must carry:
- section.
- BOQ code.
- PPK code.
- cost code.
- drawing/spec reference.
