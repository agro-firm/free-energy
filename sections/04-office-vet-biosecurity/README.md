# 04 — Office + Vet + Biosecurity

## Documentation status
**Detailed engineering concept package — Section 04 complete for planning.**

## Canonical baseline
- **Plant version:** PLANT-V1.0
- **Footprint:** 12 ft × 15 ft
- **Area:** 180 ft² ≈ 16.72 m²
- **Clear-height concept:** 10 ft ASSUMPTION
- **Primary roles:** biosecurity entry, farm office/records, CCTV/monitoring, veterinary/AI workspace, secure medicine/vaccine storage
- **Public/front side:** west in the local section coordinate system
- **Farm/clean staff side:** east in the local section coordinate system
- **No manure, feed, fertilizer, fuel or biogas equipment inside**
- **Medicine/vaccine refrigeration:** dedicated 2–8°C medical/pharmaceutical refrigerator with temperature logging and critical backup

> In this section, **AI** means **artificial insemination**, not artificial intelligence.
