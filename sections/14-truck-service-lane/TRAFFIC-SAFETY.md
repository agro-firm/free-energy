# Truck / Service Lane — Traffic and Worker Safety

## Rules
- 5–10 km/h internal maximum concept
- one truck at a time
- reversing spotter
- high-vis vest
- wheel chocks at loading
- parking brake
- no phone distraction during maneuvering
- no children/animals in lane during truck movement

## Pedestrians
Painted worker strip at loading nodes.
No continuous raised curb that reduces full lane emergency width.

## Bollards
Use only:
- around vulnerable doors/equipment
- outside vehicle swept path
- at generator/gas/process equipment where impact protection is required

## Mirrors
Convex mirror may be placed at blind rear/loading points.

## Lighting
Provide even lighting at:
- gate
- milk node
- feed canopy
- rear node

Avoid glare toward driver.

## Reversing
Spotter stays visible in driver's mirror.
If driver loses spotter:
STOP.

## Truck engine
Engine off during manual loading/unloading where practical.

## Emergency
Lane must be cleared immediately for emergency use.
No pallets or parked vehicles left unattended.
