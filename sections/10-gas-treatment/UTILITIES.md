# Gas Treatment — Utilities and Interfaces

## Gas inlet
From Section 09:
- DN40 / 1.5-inch concept
- low-pressure raw gas
- inlet isolation
- sample point
- pressure transmitter
- knockout pot

## Gas outlet
To Section 11:
- DN40 / 1.5-inch concept
- treated gas
- outlet H₂S verification
- flow meter
- booster/regulator
- automatic shutoff valve
- generator-ready interface

## Condensate
KO-401 and MS-401 drains:
- closed condensate container/dirty process drain
- no open drain
- no stormwater connection
- manual/automatic drain only if gas-tight design prevents gas release

## H₂S media
Spent media kept dry/contained.
Disposal/regeneration follows supplier SDS and environmental requirements.

## Electrical
Loads:
- B-401 booster ~0.37 kW concept
- H₂S analyzer/sensors
- gas detector
- PLC/I/O
- valves
- lighting outside hazardous zone

Control panel located outside final classified zone unless appropriately rated.

## Data
Signals:
- inlet pressure
- outlet pressure
- gas flow
- H₂S outlet ppm
- optional H₂S inlet ppm
- filter differential pressure
- scrubber differential pressure
- condensate high level
- booster run/fault
- gas detector alarm
- Section 09 holder level/pressure
- Section 11 generator-ready/demand

## Earthing/bonding
Bond:
- vessels
- gas piping
- skid frame
- blower
- filter housings

## Ventilation
Skid remains naturally ventilated/open-sided.

## Fire/gas
No ignition source.
Any booster motor/electrical device in classified area must meet final hazardous-area requirements.

## Stormwater
Clean roof runoff remains separate from condensate/process drainage.
