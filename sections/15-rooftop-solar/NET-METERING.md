# Rooftop Solar — Bangladesh Net Metering

## Current framework
Bangladesh Power Division currently lists:
- Net Metering Guideline 2025.
- National Rooftop Solar Program implementation guidance.
- national rooftop-solar incentives/clarifications.

References:
- https://powerdivision.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/694032be35ce18e1c0561a07
- https://powerdivision.gov.bd/pages/files/6948fac3559d93a15090eea0
- https://nem.powerdivision.gov.bd/

## Application workflow
Current NEM portal describes:
1. online application.
2. utility review.
3. site inspection.
4. approval/interconnection process.

## Approved equipment
Use the current SREDA approved lists at procurement:
- modules.
- inverters.

References:
- https://nsrra.sreda.gov.bd/solar/nem/smodule/
- https://nsrra.sreda.gov.bd/solar/nem/gti3ph/

## Current approved examples
Module:
- Canadian Solar CS6.2-66TB-625, 625 W.

Inverter:
- Sungrow SG15RT-P2, 15 kW.
- Solis S5-GR3P15K, 15 kW.

## Important
Eligibility/capacity/export and meter/interconnection conditions depend on the actual utility account, sanctioned load/service type and the current guideline.

Verify these before procurement.

## Meter
Final net meter:
- utility-approved bidirectional meter.
- installed/interconnected according to current utility process.
