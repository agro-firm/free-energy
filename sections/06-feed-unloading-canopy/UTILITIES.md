# Feed Unloading Canopy — Utilities and Drainage

## 1. Roof stormwater
Clean roof runoff:
```
canopy roof → outer gutter → downpipe → clean stormwater
```

Do not discharge roof water across the unloading apron.

## 2. Apron drainage
Apron floor slopes outward toward an 18-ft trench drain.

Apron runoff may contain:
- soil/mud from footwear
- feed dust
- forage residue

Route to approved site surface/process drainage after screening/housekeeping.

Do not automatically send this flow to the biogas digester.

## 3. Feed-store threshold
The Section 05 door threshold must remain dry.

Provide:
- outward floor fall
- trench before lane
- slight raised feed-store floor
- door weather seal

## 4. Water
No permanent washdown hose is required in base design.

Preferred cleaning:
- broom
- shovel
- dry collection

Optional nearby hose connection may be used for occasional apron cleaning after feed is removed.

## 5. Electrical
Base:
- 2 × 30 W LED/floodlights
- one weatherproof service outlet
- optional CCTV
- optional arrival/door sensor

Use exterior-rated enclosures/conduit.

## 6. Data
Optional:
- CCTV
- feed-delivery QR/barcode scan
- weather/rain sensor
- door contact to Section 05

## 7. Gas
**No biogas, fuel gas, gas storage, flare or combustion equipment in Section 06.**

## 8. Fire
Feed is combustible.
- no smoking
- no hot work during unloading
- extinguisher location coordinated with Section 05
- keep delivery vehicle exhaust away from stored dry feed where practical

## 9. Lighting
Fixtures mounted under canopy at ~10–11 ft:
- weather resistant
- impact protected
- aimed down/away from driver eyes

## 10. Earthing
If canopy is steel:
- bond/earth structure per electrical/lightning design
- coordinate with site grounding system
