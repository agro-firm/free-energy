# Biogas Digester — Benefits

## Waste stabilization
The digester biologically stabilizes the controlled manure slurry instead of storing untreated manure.

## Renewable gas
Planning gas:
```
8.10–9.18 m³/day
```
which supports the farm's biogas electricity system.

## Hydraulic stability
Selected 20 m³ liquid volume gives:
```
~37 days HRT
```
compared with the 30-day minimum design basis.

## Separate gas storage
The digester is not oversized to act as a gas tank; Section 09 provides dedicated gas buffering.

## Fertilizer
Digestate remains a nutrient-containing stream for Section 12 fertilizer processing.

## Odor/environment
Contained digestion can reduce uncontrolled decomposition/odor compared with unmanaged dung piles, when feed, gas and digestate systems are operated properly.

## Data
The digester becomes a measurable link between:
- slurry in
- gas out
- digestate out
- electricity produced

## Bangladesh fit
IDCOL reports tens of thousands of financed biogas plants in Bangladesh and supports brick-cement and prefabricated technologies, demonstrating strong local experience with manure-based biogas systems.
