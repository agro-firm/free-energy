# Cow Yard — Architecture Report

## Design intent
A compact, open-air, non-pasture exercise/holding yard for controlled cow movement. It is designed for **10 cows at a time** in two rotating groups.

## Geometry
- 18 × 28 ft = 504 ft²
- 46.82 m²
- active zone: 18 × 24 ft = 432 ft² = 40.13 m²
- service/drain strip: 18 × 4 ft = 72 ft²
- concept shade: 12 × 18 ft = 216 ft²

Shade coverage:
```
216 / 504 × 100 = 42.86% of full yard
216 / 432 × 100 = 50% of active animal zone
```

## Capacity
20 cows simultaneously:
```
46.82 / 20 = 2.34 m²/cow
```
Recommended 10-cow group in active zone:
```
40.13 / 10 = 4.01 m²/cow
```

## Architectural form
- open fenced yard
- no enclosed room
- paved non-slip floor
- partial steel shade canopy
- 5 ft concept fence height
- direct cattle gate to shed
- separate service/personnel access where possible
- east-end drainage/service strip

## Shade canopy
Concept:
- footprint 12 × 18 ft
- open sides
- galvanized steel posts/frame
- metal or high-quality livestock shade roof
- conceptual eave 9.5–10 ft, high side 11 ft
- slope away from cow shed
- clean gutter/downpipe to stormwater

Final frame sizes and wind loading are structural-engineer items.

## Material palette
- reinforced concrete pavement
- broom/grooved non-slip finish
- galvanized pipe/rail/chain-link fence
- galvanized steel gates
- corrosion-protected shade frame
- durable trough
- grated concrete/HDPE/steel drain

## Climate strategy
The yard is primarily open to natural air. Partial shade creates a cooler refuge while retaining open sun area.

## Adjacency
For section-local drawings, assume the cow shed connection is on the west side. This is a **local documentation assumption**; the whole yard may be rotated in the future global site coordinate plan without changing its internal geometry.

## Safety
- no sharp fence wire inside animal contact zone
- gate latches operable from safe side
- no standing water
- protected drain grates
- no electrical hardware within cow reach
- shade columns protected from animal impact
