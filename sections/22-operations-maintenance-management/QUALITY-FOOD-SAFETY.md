# Quality and Food Safety

## Milk
Control:
- treated/withdrawal animals.
- udder/teat hygiene.
- sanitary contact surfaces.
- cooling.
- dispatch.
- buyer/lab requirements.

## Water
Potable-quality for:
- milk cleaning.
- handwash.
- relevant vet/clean uses.

## Feed
Reject:
- mold.
- water damage.
- pest contamination.
- wrong/expired lot.

## Medicines
- cold chain.
- FEFO.
- withdrawal.
- record.

## Fertilizer
Commercial claims only after current lab/regulatory approval.

## Traceability
Milk/feed/medicine/fertilizer records can be traced by:
- date.
- lot.
- operator.
- animal/batch.
