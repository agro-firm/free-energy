# Truck / Service Lane — Operations and Maintenance

## Daily
- keep clear
- remove feed/fertilizer debris
- inspect spills
- inspect drain grates

## Weekly
- clean drain
- check lights
- inspect gate
- inspect markings/chocks

## Monthly
- inspect cracks/joints
- inspect bollards
- review CCTV
- vegetation clearance

## Monsoon
- drain cleaning increased
- inspect ponding/settlement
- repair edge erosion

## Annual
- pavement condition survey
- joint reseal
- repaint markings
- gate service
- update vehicle swept path if supplier truck changes

## KPIs
- truck dwell time
- reversing incidents
- blocked-lane hours
- drain blockages
- pavement repairs
- vehicle strikes
