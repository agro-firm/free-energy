# Section 19 — Fire / Safety / Regulatory Cost

## Purpose
Incremental/consolidated plant-wide safety budget.

It must be reconciled against safety allowances already included in Sections 03–15 to avoid double counting.

## Current Bangladesh hardware benchmarks — October 2026
Retail examples:
- 5 kg ABC/ABCE extinguisher ~Tk1,380–1,500.
- 5 kg CO₂ extinguisher ~Tk2,700–3,000.
- smoke/heat detector ~Tk770–2,200.
- manual call point ~Tk1,100–2,500.
- 8-zone conventional panel ~Tk17,000–38,000.
- emergency exit light ~Tk600–1,730.

FSCD currently lists its onsite fire-risk assessment/survey service at:
```
Tk5,000 + online fee
```

15%:
```
850,000×0.15
=Tk127,500
```

Base:
```
Tk977,500
≈Tk9.78 lakh
```

## Scenarios
- Low: ~Tk6.5 lakh.
- Base: ~Tk9.78 lakh.
- High: ~Tk17.0 lakh.

## Exclusions
Not included until required:
- dedicated fire-water tank.
- fire pump.
- hydrant/hose reel.
- sprinkler.
- major DCF relocation.
- major fire-rated building reconstruction.

Those can materially increase project cost.
