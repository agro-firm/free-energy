# Preventive Maintenance Master

## PM levels
### D — Daily/operator care
Clean, inspect, record.

### W — Weekly
Minor adjustment/functional check.

### M — Monthly
Planned technical inspection.

### Q — Quarterly
Calibration/service/trend review.

### A — Annual
Major professional/vendor service.

### H — Runtime/condition
Based on hours, gas volume, breakthrough, pressure drop, measured wear or alarm trend.

## Key philosophy
Vendor manual and statutory inspection override this master.

## No calendar-only replacement
Condition/runtime controls:
- H₂S media.
- filters.
- generator service items.
- pump seals.
- solar cleaning.

## Backlog
Class:
- P1 safety/process critical.
- P2 production risk.
- P3 planned.
- P4 improvement.

## KPI
- PM compliance %.
- overdue PM.
- breakdown hours.
- MTBF.
- MTTR.
- maintenance BDT/L milk.
