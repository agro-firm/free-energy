# Survey Validation Requirements

## Boundary
Verify:
- 65 ft width.
- 95 ft depth.
- corner monument positions.
- fence/wall thickness.

## Road/front
Measure:
- road edge.
- road width.
- ditch.
- gate offset.
- turning obstruction.
- neighboring boundary.

## Topography
Survey grid and all section corners at ≤10-ft planning spacing plus break points.

## Levels
Capture:
- front road crown/edge.
- all four plot corners.
- low points.
- existing drains.
- flood marks.
- groundwater observations.

## Deliverables
- signed CAD/DWG.
- PDF topo.
- coordinate CSV.
- benchmark description.
- photos.
- north reference.

## Tolerance
Construction setting-out tolerances are set by licensed survey/structural/civil team, not this concept document.
