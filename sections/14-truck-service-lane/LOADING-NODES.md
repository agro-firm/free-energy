# Truck / Service Lane — Loading Nodes

## STA 10–35 — Milk
Length:
```
25 ft
```

Functions:
- milk tanker/pickup
- sanitary hose transfer
- clean worker approach

Rules:
- no feed/fertilizer activity during milk transfer if conflict exists
- no dirty runoff
- milk hose does not cross traffic path unnecessarily

## STA 35–60 — Feed
Length:
```
25 ft
```

Interfaces:
- Section 06 18-ft canopy
- Section 05 feed store

Truck may shift outward to create roughly 4 ft worker space beside canopy.

## STA 60–70 — Clear service node
Keep open for:
- vet vehicle
- pedestrian crossing
- maintenance maneuvering
- temporary pull-forward positioning

No storage.

## STA 70–95 — Rear utility/fertilizer
Functions:
- Section 12 bag loading
- liquid-fertilizer transport
- Section 11 generator service
- digester/gas utility maintenance

Dirty product must stay contained within Section 12 transfer system.

## Node markings
Each stop:
- yellow stop box
- wheel-chock marks
- KEEP CLEAR text at doors
- pedestrian/worker strip
- node ID signage
