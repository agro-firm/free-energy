# Generator + Electrical — Architecture Report

## 1. Room geometry
```
12 × 14 = 168 ft²
168 ×0.09290304 =15.6077 m²
```

At 10 ft clear:
```
168×10 =1,680 ft³
≈47.57 m³
```

## 2. Local zoning
Use X=0–14 ft west→east and Y=0–12 ft south→north.

### West gas/service strip
```
3×12=36 ft²
```
Includes treated-gas entry, manual isolation, normally-closed solenoid, pressure points, flexible engine connection and maintenance route.

### Central genset/mechanical zone
```
6×12=72 ft²
```
Includes generator, anti-vibration base, service clearance, intake/hot-air path and exhaust connection.

### East electrical zone
```
5×12=60 ft²
```
Includes generator breaker, ATS, Essential Load Board, meters, controller, battery charger and protected worker access.

Area:
```
36+72+60=168 ft²
```

## 3. Building form
- noncombustible masonry/steel utility room
- washable non-slip concrete floor
- 10 ft clear height
- weatherproof roof
- wide outward-opening service door
- low south intake louver
- high north exhaust fan/louver
- engine exhaust exits north/high and terminates safely away from air intakes

## 4. Generator footprint
Planning envelope:
- ~5.5 ft × 3 ft
- exact vendor footprint controls
- mounted around X3.25–8.75 ft and Y4.5–7.5 ft

West/east strips provide side service clearance; north/south room depth provides engine/exhaust access.

## 5. Acoustic concept
Use:
- anti-vibration mounts
- flexible exhaust section
- industrial muffler
- noncombustible acoustic treatment where needed
- acoustic door seals that do not compromise ventilation

No numerical noise limit is fixed until site/regulatory criteria are verified.

## 6. Ventilation architecture
South:
- low-level intake louver around 24×24 in gross planning minimum

North:
- high-level mechanical exhaust
- target total ventilation 1,500–2,000 m³/h

Final generator vendor cooling-air demand overrides this concept.

## 7. Electrical separation
Panels are mounted on the east wall away from gas-entry manifold and hot exhaust.

No gas pipe is routed above electrical panels.

## 8. Fire/egress
- outward-opening door
- clear path from genset to exit
- no storage in room
- extinguishing equipment selected by fire review
- external emergency stop near door

## 9. Weather and drainage
Room floor is raised/dry.
No floor drain is required under engine; use drip tray/bund for oil.
Roof water to clean stormwater.

## 10. Adjacent interfaces
- west: Section 10 treated gas
- east/south: farm electrical distribution
- exhaust terminates away from Sections 09/10 gas equipment and occupied areas
