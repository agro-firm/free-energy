# Cow Shed — Operations and Maintenance Plan

## 1. Daily inspection

### Animals and floor
- count cows
- inspect lameness/slipping risks
- remove obstacles
- inspect wet spots
- inspect manger cleanliness
- inspect trough cleanliness

### Manure system
- verify scraper cycles
- inspect drive/cable/chain
- listen for abnormal noise
- confirm discharge to Section 07
- record jams/faults

### Ventilation
- confirm side openings clear
- verify fan operation
- check temperature/humidity

### Electrical
- inspect visible damage
- keep enclosures closed
- verify lights/alarms

---

## 2. Cleaning schedule

### After feeding / as required
- remove spoiled feed
- sweep feed alley
- clean manger

### Manure lane
Use scraper as primary solids-removal method before adding wash water.

### Troughs
Clean on a scheduled basis and whenever contamination is visible.

### Washdown
Use measured water. Excessive wash water:
- increases pumping
- dilutes digester feed
- increases wastewater volume

---

## 3. Weekly

- check scraper wear
- inspect anchor/guard bolts
- test emergency stop
- inspect trough valves
- inspect fan guards
- inspect gutter/downpipe blockages
- inspect roof leaks from ground
- review water and electricity meters

---

## 4. Monthly

- lubricate scraper parts per vendor
- inspect motor/gearbox
- check motor current trend
- clean fan blades/guards
- inspect corrosion
- inspect stall hardware
- tighten loose non-structural fasteners
- review sensor calibration status
- clean electrical panel exterior
- review CCTV views

---

## 5. Quarterly / seasonal

Before monsoon:
- clean roof gutters
- inspect downpipes
- inspect roof fasteners
- inspect drainage outlet
- repair corrosion

Before hottest season:
- service all fans
- confirm water capacity
- clean open ventilation paths
- verify emergency power strategy

---

## 6. Annual

- structural visual inspection
- professional electrical test
- earthing check
- scraper major service
- roof and solar-interface inspection
- repaint/recoat damaged steel
- review stall dimensions against herd changes
- update risk assessment

---

## 7. Lockout / maintenance safety

Powered scraper, fans and electrical equipment must be isolated before hands-on maintenance.

Maintenance workers should use:
- lockout/tagout
- appropriate PPE
- safe ladder/roof procedure
- animal separation where required

---

## 8. KPI dashboard

Track:
- liters water/cow/day
- shed kWh/day
- scraper cycles/day
- scraper faults/month
- fan runtime
- temperature/RH
- injury/slip events
- wash-water use
- manure-transfer interruptions

These data should be used to improve future design revisions.

---

## 9. Spare-parts minimum

Maintain:
- scraper wear components
- limit switch
- motor overload/contactors
- trough valves
- fan service parts
- LED drivers/fixtures
- isolation valves
- fuses/breakers specified by electrician

---

## 10. Documentation rule

Every significant equipment replacement should update:
- EQUIPMENT.md
- COST.md if cost model changes
- images if appearance changes materially
- master skill only if canonical capacity/dimensions change
