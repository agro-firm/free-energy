## Digestate outlet
East outlet/overflow chamber concept:
- X≈15.5–19.5 ft
- Y≈4.5–10.5 ft

Gravity digestate line:
- nominal 100 mm / 4-inch-class concept
- overflow centerline near normal liquid level Z≈+0.25 m
- routes to Section 12 buffer/separation process

## Gas outlet
North gas manifold zone:
- X≈7–13 ft
- Y≈13–16 ft

Gas outlet:
- nominal DN40 / 1.5-inch-class concept
- rises from gas-space high point
- goes to condensate protection and Section 09
- P/V relief has an independent safe path and is not isolated by normal process valves

## Recirculation loop
Planning external loop:
- suction from lower side around Z≈-2.5 m
- solids-capable pump P-201
- return around Z≈-0.5 m, preferably tangential
- isolation/cleanout points
- final diameter vendor-selected

## Bottom drain
Maintenance drain/pump-out at low point around Z≈-2.8 to -3.0 m.
Normal operation does not depend on bottom draining.

## Manway
Gas-tight manway on roof with safe external access.
Final size, bolts, gasket and lifting arrangement by engineer/vendor.
