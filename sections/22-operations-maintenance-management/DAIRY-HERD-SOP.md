# Dairy Herd SOP

## Daily observation
Before each milking/feeding:
- appetite.
- posture/behavior.
- lameness.
- respiration.
- visible injury.
- udder/teat condition.
- manure consistency.
- water intake abnormality.
- heat signs.

## Cow identification
Each cow has unique ID linked to:
- age/breed.
- calving.
- lactation.
- milk.
- treatment.
- breeding.
- vaccination.
- withdrawal period.

## Abnormal cow
- identify/isolate as appropriate.
- contact veterinarian based on severity.
- record.
- protect milk/feed chain.

## Monthly
- body-condition review.
- weight/heart-girth trend if available.
- production trend.
- reproductive status.

## Hoof/lameness
Inspect routinely.
Professional trimming/treatment based on need, not arbitrary calendar.

## Welfare
Always provide:
- drinking water.
- feed.
- dry/resting area.
- ventilation/heat mitigation.
