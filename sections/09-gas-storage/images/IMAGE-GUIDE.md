## Master photoreal prompt
Create Section 09 Gas Storage for PLANT-V1.0 in Bangladesh. Exact zone 12×10 ft. Show a custom 8 m³ very-low-pressure flexible biogas membrane bag inside a ventilated galvanized mesh protective frame about 2.4 m square and 2.4 m high. The bag itself has an approximate maximum 2×2×2 m inflated envelope but should look rounded/flexible, not rigid. West raw-gas inlet comes from Section 08 through condensate protection; east raw-gas outlet goes to Section 10 treatment. Show low-range pressure transmitter, membrane level/position sensor, condensate pot, independent pressure/vacuum safety connection, gas detector, earthing/bonding and safe vent/flare interface. No flame near holder.

## Cutaway
Show membrane, protective cage, gas manifold, flexible connectors and sensor locations. Do not make it a pressure tank.
