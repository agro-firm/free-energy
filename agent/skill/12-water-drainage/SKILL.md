# Water + Drainage Skill

## Canonical baseline
- Utility area: **8 × 12 ft**.
- Ground storage: **8 m³ RCC tank**.
- Overhead storage: **2 m³ food-grade tank**.
- Total storage: **10 m³**.
- Normal demand: **~3.30 m³/day**.
- Design demand +15%: **~3.79 m³/day**.
- Hot-weather design: **~4.25 m³/day**.

## Demand components
- cow drinking: ~1,600 L/day baseline
- cow shed/yard wash: ~700 L/day planning
- milk room cleaning/CIP: ~397 L/day
- office/vet: ~80 L/day
- digester dilution: ~270 L/day
- fertilizer/process wash: ~100 L/day
- staff/general: ~150 L/day

## Network
Source → ground tank → duty/standby pumps → pressure manifold → overhead tank + distribution branches.

Gravity bypass from overhead tank provides emergency drinking/service water if power is lost.

## Water quality
Test source water before commissioning and periodically for relevant Bangladesh drinking-water parameters including arsenic, iron, manganese, pH, TDS, turbidity and microbiology.

## Drainage
- clean roof/stormwater → clean drainage
- tank overflows → visible safe drain
- tank-cleaning water → controlled drain
- dirty manure/process water → separate dirty/process network

## Rule
Final pump size, pipe diameter, pressure setpoints and tank structure require engineer/vendor confirmation.
