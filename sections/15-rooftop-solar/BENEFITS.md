# Rooftop Solar — Benefits

## No ground use
15 kWp is placed on existing cow-shed roof.

## Energy
Planning:
```
~21.3 MWh/year
```

## Daytime load support
Solar offsets:
- pumps.
- fans.
- milk cooling.
- fertilizer processing.
- office/process loads.

## Complement with biogas
Solar is daytime/weather-dependent.
Biogas is stored and dispatchable.

## Net metering
Approved grid interconnection can credit surplus energy according to current Bangladesh rules.

## Monitoring
Solar, generator and grid can be compared on one energy dashboard.
