# Generator + Electrical — Utilities

## AC electrical
- 230 V 50 Hz single-phase concept
- generator breaker 32 A 2P
- ATS 40–63 A 2P
- Essential Load Board
- SPD
- meter
- branch MCB/RCBO/RCD
- cable tray/conduit

## DC
- 12 V electric-start battery
- charger/maintainer
- fused DC control wiring
- battery isolation

## Gas
Only treated gas from Section 10.
Normally-closed automatic shutoff.
No gas storage in room.

## Exhaust
High-temperature engine exhaust to outdoors.
No connection to general room air duct.

## Ventilation
Mechanical exhaust plus low intake.
Fans interlocked with generator start.

## Drainage
No open drain below engine.
Use drip tray/bund for oil/coolant if applicable.
Roof/stormwater remains clean.

## Data
Log:
- kW
- kWh
- V
- A
- Hz
- runtime
- gas flow
- gas pressure
- oil pressure
- engine temperature
- room temperature
- CH₄/CO alarms
- ATS position
- breaker status

## Earthing
Common coordinated farm earthing system.
Bond:
- generator frame
- alternator frame
- electrical panels
- gas pipe
- exhaust metalwork where required
- room steelwork

## Lighting
Normal and emergency lighting.
Do not rely on one generator-fed lamp for safe maintenance.
