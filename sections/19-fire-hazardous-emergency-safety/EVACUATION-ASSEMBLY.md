# Evacuation and Assembly

## Human evacuation
Primary evacuation direction:
rear/process areas → service lane/front → designated assembly point.

## Assembly point AP-01
Candidate:
**outside the plot near the front-west side, away from the gate approach and away from smoke/gas plume path.**

Exact location:
TBD after road/boundary survey and fire-plan approval.

## Rule
Assembly point must not:
- block the 14-ft emergency lane.
- block Fire Service gate approach.
- sit downwind of likely gas release without alternative.
- sit beside gas/electrical equipment.

## Secondary escape
Where safe, west/perimeter pedestrian escape gates may be considered if local security/site boundaries allow.

## Animal emergency
Section 01/02 requires a separate livestock-release plan:
- open designated non-fire-side gates.
- move cattle away from gas/fire area.
- never direct animals through the fire-engine approach lane during responder entry unless unavoidable.
