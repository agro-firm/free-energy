# Payment Milestones

## Principle
Pay for verified progress, not calendar promises.

## Equipment example
Commercial negotiation example only:
- advance: only against approved security/terms.
- approved drawings.
- FAT/manufacturing completion.
- delivery.
- installation.
- SAT/performance.
- retention/final acceptance.

## Civil works
Payments against:
- measured quantities.
- approved material.
- completed work.
- test results.

## No payment trigger
Do not pay solely because:
- invoice date arrived.
- equipment is "ready" without evidence.
- vendor submitted unapproved drawing.

## Final payment
Requires:
- as-built.
- O&M.
- warranty.
- training.
- spares.
- punch-list closure.
