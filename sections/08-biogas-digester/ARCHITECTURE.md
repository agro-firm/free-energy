# Biogas Digester — Architecture and Civil Concept

## 1. Design intent
Section 08 is a **compact, semi-buried, low-pressure anaerobic process vessel** inside a 16×20 ft compound. The architecture keeps most liquid volume below grade for thermal stability and land efficiency while exposing the upper ring, gas-tight roof, manway, gas outlet and service points for inspection.

## 2. Compound
```
16 × 20 = 320 ft²
320 × 0.09290304 = 29.72897 m²
```

Local coordinates:
- X=0–20 ft west→east
- Y=0–16 ft south→north
- Section 07 feed approaches from west
- Section 12 digestate route leaves east
- Section 09 gas route leaves north/northeast

## 3. Selected vessel geometry
Planning internal cylinder:
```
D = 2.8 m
r = 1.4 m
cross-sectional area = πr²
≈ 6.1575 m²
```

For 25 m³ total:
```
H_total = 25 / 6.1575
≈ 4.060 m
```

Selected working volume:
```
20 m³
```

Working liquid depth:
```
20 / 6.1575
≈ 3.248 m
```

Headspace/freeboard:
```
25 - 20 = 5 m³
```

Equivalent headspace depth:
```
5 / 6.1575
≈ 0.812 m
```

## 4. Semi-buried elevation
Planning internal levels:
- vessel internal bottom: Z ≈ -3.00 m
- normal liquid level: Z ≈ +0.25 m
- internal gas roof: Z ≈ +1.06 m
- external cover top: determined by structural thickness

This keeps most digestion volume underground and leaves roughly 1 m of process structure visible above grade.

## 5. External RCC planning geometry
For quantity planning only:
- internal diameter: 2.8 m
- conceptual wall thickness: 0.20 m ASSUMPTION
- external cylinder diameter: ~3.20 m
- conceptual base slab diameter: ~3.40 m
- conceptual base thickness: 0.25 m
- conceptual roof slab thickness: 0.15 m

These are quantity assumptions only. Structural engineer must redesign them from soil, groundwater, gas tightness, loading and BNBC/local criteria.

## 6. Compound fit
External diameter ~3.2 m ≈10.50 ft.

Within 16-ft compound width:
```
16 - 10.50 = 5.50 ft
```

Approximate side allowance:
```
5.50 / 2
≈2.75 ft each side
```

Within 20-ft length:
```
20 - 10.50 = 9.50 ft
```

allowing feed, outlet and maintenance pits at opposite sides.

## 7. Roof / manway
Gas-tight roof must include:
- maintenance manway
- gas outlet
- P/V relief connection
- temperature/level instrument penetrations
- lifting/handling strategy
- seals compatible with moist biogas/H₂S

Routine human entry is prohibited.

## 8. Compound architecture
Recommended:
- paved/non-slip service ring
- low fence/controlled-access boundary
- west feed valve/recirculation pit
- east digestate outlet chamber
- north gas/safety manifold
- south clear maintenance access
- clean stormwater diverted around compound
- no unrelated storage

## 9. Groundwater and uplift
A partly empty buried digester can be subject to buoyancy. Foundation design must consider:
- highest groundwater level
- empty-vessel uplift
- soil bearing
- differential settlement
- hydrostatic pressure
- watertight construction joints

## 10. Thermal strategy
Semi-burial reduces daily temperature swings. Base concept is **unheated ambient/mesophilic digestion**. No heater is included unless monitoring later shows insufficient temperature.

## 11. Materials
- RCC vessel with gas/water-resistant internal coating/lining as designed
- chemical-resistant joint waterstops
- corrosion-resistant gas pipe/fittings
- HDPE/uPVC/appropriate slurry pipe where compatible
- stainless or protected fasteners
- lockable instrument enclosure

## 12. Public Bangladesh context
IDCOL states that Bangladesh biogas programs use both brick-cement and prefabricated biodigesters and finances dairy/poultry farm systems. The selected RCC cylinder is a project-specific engineering concept, not an IDCOL standard drawing.
