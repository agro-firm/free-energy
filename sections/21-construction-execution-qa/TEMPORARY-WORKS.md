# Temporary Works

## Categories
- excavation support/shoring.
- dewatering.
- access platforms.
- scaffolds.
- temporary roof/weather protection.
- formwork.
- lifting beams/supports.
- temporary electrical.
- temporary drainage.
- protection to completed work.

## Design
Temporary works with structural/safety significance require:
- responsible designer.
- drawing/calculation.
- approval.
- inspection.
- load limit.

## Excavation
Digester/tanks/sumps:
- safe slopes/shoring.
- access/egress.
- groundwater control.
- edge protection.
- spoil setback.

## Formwork
Check:
- line/level.
- support.
- release agent.
- embedded items.
- pour rate.
