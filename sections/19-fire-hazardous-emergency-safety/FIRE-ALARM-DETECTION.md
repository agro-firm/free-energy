# Fire Alarm and Detection

## Baseline planning system
Central conventional **8-zone fire-alarm panel** located in Section 04 clean/admin area.

Final panel/loop count is authority/fire-consultant controlled.

## Proposed alarm zoning
1. Office/water/front clean zone.
2. Feed store/canopy.
3. Milk room.
4. Cow shed/yard.
5. Waste/digester.
6. Gas storage/treatment.
7. Generator/electrical.
8. Fertilizer/rear service.

## Detection philosophy
### Smoke/multisensor
Suitable clean/dry rooms:
- office.
- feed store where dust false alarm is manageable.
- milk/electrical clean areas.

### Heat detection
Consider:
- generator room.
- dusty/steamy locations where smoke detection is unsuitable.

### Gas detection
CH₄/H₂S/CO is separate process-safety detection and interfaces into the emergency cause/effect.

## Manual call points
Planning locations:
- front/office exit.
- feed/milk lane node.
- cow-shed accessible exit.
- rear process entrance.
- generator/fertilizer node.

## Audible/visual
Provide sitewide audibility/visibility based on final noise survey.
Use horn/strobe/beacon where animal/process noise may mask bells.

## Power
Fire alarm:
- normal mains.
- compliant standby battery.
- fault monitoring.

## Current market context
2026 Bangladesh retail examples:
- conventional detectors ~Tk770–2,200.
- manual call point ~Tk1,100–2,500.
- 8-zone panel roughly Tk17,000–38,000.
These are hardware benchmarks only; certified design/installation/testing costs are higher.
