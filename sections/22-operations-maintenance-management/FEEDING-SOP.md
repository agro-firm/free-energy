# Feeding SOP

## Planning daily quantities
- concentrate ~60 kg/day.
- dry roughage ~80 kg/day.
- fresh forage ~300 kg/day as-fed.

These are design assumptions; nutritionist/vet ration controls actual feeding.

## Receiving
- supplier.
- lot.
- weight.
- bag/bale condition.
- moisture/mold/pest.
- reject wet/contaminated feed.

## Storage
- pallets/racks.
- FIFO/FEFO.
- dry.
- sealed minerals.
- label lots.

## Daily issue
1. approved ration sheet.
2. pick oldest acceptable lot.
3. weigh.
4. record.
5. distribute.
6. remove refusals/spoilage.

## Feed change
Do not abruptly change ration without nutrition/animal-health review.

## KPI
- kg concentrate/cow.
- kg DM/cow.
- feed cost/L milk.
- refusal/waste %.
- stock days.
