# Fertilizer Processing — Utilities and Drainage

## Digestate inlet
From Section 08:
- 100 mm / 4-inch gravity/controlled outlet concept to BT-601
- isolation/cleanout
- covered transfer
- no open splash channel where avoidable

## Separator feed
BT-601 → P-601 → SP-601.
Pump selected for digestate solids.

## Liquid line
SP-601 liquid outlet → LT-601:
- 50–75 mm concept
- gravity preferred
- closed line
- sample point

## Solid cake
Gravity/chute onto cake trolley or curing bay.

## Water
Small wash/cleanup water only.
Avoid diluting product unnecessarily.

## Dirty drainage
All:
- screw-press wash
- cake leachate
- floor wash
flows to:
- liquid digestate tank or dedicated dirty collection

No process water to clean stormwater.

## Roof stormwater
Roof → gutter → clean stormwater.

## Electrical
Planning:
- screw press ~2.2 kW
- feed pump ~0.75 kW
- two fans ~0.50 kW total
- bag closer/scale/control ~0.2 kW
- lighting

Electrical supply is vendor-specific. Separator may be single- or three-phase.

## Data
Recommended:
- BT-601 level
- LT-601 level
- separator run/fault
- motor current
- liquid flow total if justified
- cake batch weight
- bag count
- moisture test results

## Gas
No biogas piping/equipment inside Section 12.

## Odor/ventilation
Natural/open-sided ventilation plus fans.
Tank vents terminate safely away from workers.
