# Monsoon / Weather Plan

## Risks
- excavation flooding.
- soft subgrade.
- concrete quality.
- steel/roof work in wind.
- electrical equipment wetting.
- material damage.
- access mud.

## Controls
- temporary diversion drains.
- sump/dewatering pumps.
- covered cement/electrical storage.
- weather forecast check.
- stabilize exposed soil.
- postpone unsafe lifting/roof work.
- protect fresh concrete.

## Schedule
Weather float remains in forecast; do not compress critical safety/curing activities to recover delay.

## Water
No pumped muddy construction water is discharged directly into clean storm drains without approved sediment control.
