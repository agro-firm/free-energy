# Lockout / Tagout

## Energy sources
- grid AC.
- solar DC/AC.
- generator.
- batteries.
- pumps/motors.
- gas pressure.
- water/process pressure.
- stored mechanical/gravity energy.

## Minimum LOTO
1. identify.
2. stop.
3. isolate.
4. lock/tag.
5. dissipate stored energy.
6. verify zero-energy state.
7. perform work.
8. inspect/clear.
9. remove locks by authorized process.
10. controlled restart.

## Solar
PV DC conductors can remain energized in daylight.
Use PV-specific isolation/verification.

## Gas
Closing a valve does not automatically make a line gas-free.
Depressurize/purge/test as required.

## Records
Maintain authorized-person list and annual procedure review.
