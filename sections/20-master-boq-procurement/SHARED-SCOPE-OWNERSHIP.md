# Shared Scope Ownership

## Site drainage
Owner:
PPK-01.

Section vendors terminate at agreed interface points.

## Earthing
Owner:
PPK-08 coordinates common earth system.

Solar/gas/generator vendors provide local bonding tails and equipment requirements.

## Fire alarm
Owner:
PPK-12.

Process vendors provide dry contacts/status signals.

## Gas detectors
Technical specification:
process/safety engineer.

Commercial supply can be PPK-12 if one compatible platform is chosen.

## CCTV
Owner:
PPK-13.

Section files define camera locations, not separate NVR systems.

## SCADA
Owner:
PPK-13.

Local PLCs:
process packages.

## Integrated commissioning
Owner:
PPK-14.

Vendor SAT/startup remains within individual packages.

## Safety signage
Sitewide common signs:
PPK-12.

Equipment/process labels:
equipment vendor.
