# Gas Treatment — Gas Quality Specification

## Raw biogas
Expected:
- methane ~60% planning
- CO₂ balance plus minor gases
- saturated/high water vapor
- H₂S unknown until measured

## H₂S design basis
ASSUMPTION:
- nominal inlet 2,000 ppm
- sensitivity 1,000–3,000 ppm

The system must be re-sized if commissioning samples are materially higher.

## Treated H₂S
Planning acceptance:
- maximum ≤100 ppm
- preferred ≤50 ppm if generator vendor requires

Generator vendor specification overrides this planning target.

## Moisture
Acceptance:
- no free liquid carryover
- downstream separator clear
- dewpoint/humidity only specified after generator vendor requirement is known

## Particles
Final filter:
- planning ~5 µm
- vendor/generator requirement controls

## Pressure
Treated-gas pressure:
- stable
- above generator minimum
- below generator maximum
- does not cause Section 09 vacuum

No project pressure setpoint is fixed until holder/booster/generator vendors are selected.

## Flow
Design:
```
3 m³/h peak
```

Expected generator:
```
~2.23 m³/h
```

## Sample points
- SP-401A raw inlet
- SP-401B between beds where practical
- SP-401C final treated gas

## Measurement
Use:
- H₂S detector/analyzer suitable for ppm range
- gas flow meter designed for gas
- pressure transmitters
- optional methane analyzer for periodic performance testing

## Acceptance before generator start
- Section 09 gas available
- H₂S within generator limit
- no liquid carryover
- filters not blocked
- outlet pressure valid
- no gas alarm
