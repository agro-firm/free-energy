# Water + Drainage Skill

## Baseline
- Utility area: 8 × 12 ft.
- Water storage concept: 8 m³ ground + 2 m³ overhead.
- Current planning demand: about 3.07 m³/day before final metering.

## Networks
- Clean roof rain: gutter → storm drainage/storage/discharge.
- Dirty manure/wash flow: cow shed → waste system/digester path where appropriate.
- Milk-room drainage: hygienic system; do not send incompatible cleaning chemicals to digester.

## Formulas
daily_water = drinking + washdown + digester dilution + staff/process.
storage_days = usable_storage ÷ daily_water.
pump_flow = peak demand ÷ available pumping hours.

## Rule
Final pipe sizes and slopes require surveyed levels and local drainage verification.
