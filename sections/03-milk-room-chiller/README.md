# 03 — Milk Room + Chiller

## Documentation status
**Detailed engineering concept package — Section 03 complete for planning.**

## Canonical baseline
- **Plant version:** PLANT-V1.0
- **Footprint:** 14 ft × 18 ft
- **Area:** 252 ft² ≈ 23.41 m²
- **Clear height concept:** 10 ft ASSUMPTION
- **Milk production basis:** ~140 L/day
- **Recommended chiller:** 300 L minimum for daily pickup; 500 L preferred for reserve / alternate-day pickup
- **Cold-chain design target:** cool to about 4°C as quickly as practical and maintain chilled storage
- **Truck interface:** direct clean dispatch to Section 14 service lane
- **Cow/milking interface:** clean ingress from Section 01
- **Wastewater:** sanitary/process wastewater route; CIP chemicals must not automatically enter the digester
