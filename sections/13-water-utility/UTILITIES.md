# Water Utility — Utilities

## Water source
Could be:
- bore/submersible source
- municipal/community supply
- approved external supply

Source-specific pump is separate from the distribution pump set.

## Distribution pumps
P-701A/B:
- 1 HP each
- duty/standby
- check/isolation valves
- common pressure header
- pressure vessel/accumulator

## Treatment
Install only as test results require.

## Electrical
- pump starters/VFD or pressure controller
- tank-level control
- dry-run protection
- overload
- manual/auto
- energy meter
- emergency isolation

## Data
- ground tank level
- overhead tank level
- main flow total
- pressure
- pump run/fault
- water-quality service alarm
- leak/high-consumption alarm

## Gas
No biogas or fuel-gas equipment in Section 13.
