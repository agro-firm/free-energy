# Alarm Priority and Response

## P1 — Emergency / immediate
Examples:
- fire.
- CH₄ high.
- H₂S dangerous alarm.
- CO high.
- digester/gas pressure critical.
- uncontrolled overflow.
- electrical major trip affecting safety.
- milk chiller critical failure with product at risk.

Response:
- acknowledge.
- execute emergency/SOP.
- protect people/animals/product.
- escalate immediately.
- no auto reset without cause review.

## P2 — Urgent production
Examples:
- water pump duty failure with standby.
- milk-room temperature deviation.
- slurry batch failure.
- gas-treatment H₂S warning.
- generator failed start.
- vaccine-fridge excursion.

Response:
- act same shift.
- assign owner.
- escalate if not restored.

## P3 — Maintenance
Examples:
- filter DP trend.
- sensor calibration due.
- solar MPPT imbalance.
- minor leak.

## P4 — Advisory
Inventory/review/reminder.

## Alarm hygiene
- no alarm permanently bypassed without approved temporary management.
- recurring alarm gets root-cause action.
