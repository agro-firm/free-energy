# 03 — Milk Room + Chiller

## Status
Planning baseline. Detailed construction design will be added later.

## Baseline
14 × 18 ft; 300–500 L chiller concept

## Purpose
Clean receiving, chilling, short-term milk storage and truck dispatch.

## Inputs
Fresh milk, potable cleaning water, electricity.

## Outputs
Chilled milk to milk truck; wash water to hygienic drainage.

## Interfaces
Cow shed/milking route, service lane, water and electrical systems.

## Current design notes
Clean milk traffic must never cross manure, digester or fertilizer handling routes.
