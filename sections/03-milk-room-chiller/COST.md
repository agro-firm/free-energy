## Major cost drivers
- chiller brand/capacity/cooling performance
- hygienic wall/floor system
- SS sanitary pipe/pump
- electrical phase/backup requirements
- hot-water/CIP system
- wastewater treatment connection
- imported refrigeration parts

## Exclusions
Cow shed/milking stall infrastructure, cows, main water tanks, generator, solar array, truck lane, land, taxes/duties unless quoted.

## Procurement rule
Do not compare chiller price only. Compare:
- rated capacity
- gross capacity
- SS304 milk-contact surface
- cooling time from warm milk to 4°C
- ambient temperature rating
- compressor power
- agitator
- CIP/drain
- warranty/service
- electrical supply
