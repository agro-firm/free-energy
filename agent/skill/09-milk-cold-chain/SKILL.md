# Milk + Cold Chain Skill

## Baseline
- Room: 14 × 18 ft.
- Chiller concept: 300–500 L.
- Clean dispatch door opens toward service lane.

## Flow
Cow → milking equipment → sanitary transfer → milk receiving/filtering → chiller → dispatch connection → milk truck.

## Must include
- Washable floor and wall finishes.
- Hygienic drainage.
- Potable cleaning water.
- Hot-water/CIP method.
- Handwash.
- Milk sampling/testing point.
- Chiller condenser clearance/ventilation.
- Cleaning chemical storage.
- Pest exclusion.

## Rule
Milk truck staff should not pass through manure, digester, fertilizer or dirty cow-yard routes.
