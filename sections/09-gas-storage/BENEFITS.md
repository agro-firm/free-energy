# Gas Storage — Benefits

## Production/consumption buffering
Digester gas production is continuous while generator use is intermittent. The 8 m³ holder separates those time patterns.

## Generator efficiency
A normal 4.4 m³ storage swing supports roughly:
```
4.4/2.23
≈1.97 h
```
of generator operation instead of many short starts.

## Gas recovery
Storage reduces avoidable venting when gas is produced before the generator is ready.

## Process stability
The digester does not need to change biological feed rate instantly to match generator demand.

## Maintenance
If the generator/treatment system is temporarily unavailable, storage provides time to respond before high-level gas action is required.

## Safety
A dedicated holder allows:
- independent P/V protection
- gas detection
- controlled vent/flare interface
- dedicated hazardous-area design
rather than relying on digester headspace.

## Small-site fit
A custom 8 m³ flexible bag can fit the existing 10×12 ft zone without enlarging the plant.

## Upgrade flexibility
Future options:
- replace bag with vendor double-membrane holder
- add larger external storage
- add gas flow metering
without changing digester biology.
