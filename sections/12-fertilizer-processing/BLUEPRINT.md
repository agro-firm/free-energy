# Fertilizer Processing — Blueprint and XYZ Specification

## Coordinates
- origin southwest
- X=0–22 ft west→east
- Y=0–16 ft south→north
- Z vertical

## Zones
| Zone | X | Y | Area |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Receiving/separation | 0–6 | 0–16 | 96 ft² |
| Curing/drying | 6–14 | 0–16 | 128 ft² |
| Bagging/finished | 14–18 | 0–16 | 64 ft² |
| Liquid/service | 18–22 | 0–16 | 64 ft² |

## Buffer tank BT-601
Planning:
- gross ~0.75 m³
- working ~0.60 m³
- located west/north of screw press
- receives gravity digestate from Section 08
- covered/vented
- no-entry

## Screw press SP-601
Planning envelope based on small 500 kg/h machine:
- ~1.5 m L ×0.8 m W ×1.2 m H
- 3 HP / ~2.2 kW concept
- X≈2–6 ft
- service clearance around screw/gearbox

## Curing bays
Four concept bays:
- each ~4×8 ft
- arranged two-by-two inside 8×16 ft zone
- removable low partitions
- leachate drains to process collection

## Bagging
- screening/work table
- 60–100 kg platform scale
- 25 kg bag stand
- bag sewing/sealing
- pallet staging

## Liquid tank LT-601
Planning:
- 2.0 m³ covered vertical
- ~1.2 m diameter
- ~1.8 m liquid height
- located in X18–22 strip
- vented
- overflow prohibited to stormwater

## Liquid outlet
Pump/hose connection to:
- field-use transfer
- transport container
- approved downstream use

## Floor drainage
Receiving/curing:
- 1–1.5% local slope to dirty leachate drain

Bagging:
- dry, slightly raised/isolated floor

## Roof
Eave Z≈10 ft
Ridge Z≈13 ft
