# Pre-Commissioning

## Mechanical completion
Verify:
- equipment installed.
- guards.
- lubrication.
- alignment.
- piping complete.
- supports.
- labels.

## Electrical
- IR.
- continuity.
- earth.
- phase/polarity.
- protection.
- panel checks.

## Instruments
- calibration.
- loop.
- PLC I/O.

## Piping/process
- flush/clean.
- leak/pressure test.
- valves aligned.
- drain/vent.

## Safety
- E-stops.
- gas/fire alarms.
- guards.
- permits.
- emergency plan.

## Turnover
Use system/subsystem turnover dossiers before startup.
