# Emergency Access

## Existing corridor
Section 14:
- 14 ft wide.
- 95 ft long.
- east-side continuous lane.
- one-truck-at-a-time.

## Current Fire Service form relevance
Current e-fire-license application asks for the width of the road providing access to the establishment.

## Required validation
Fire consultant/FSCD/site survey must check:
- external road width.
- gate clear width.
- turning/swept path.
- vertical clearance.
- pavement load.
- dead-end condition.
- approach to rear process/gas areas.
- obstruction from parked trucks.

## Operational rules
- no permanent parking.
- no pallets/feed/fertilizer stored in lane.
- gate keys/access available.
- keep turning/entry clear during operating hours.
- loading truck yields to emergency access.

## Fire-appliance status
Section 14 is an **emergency-access candidate**, not a certified fire-appliance access route until authority review.
