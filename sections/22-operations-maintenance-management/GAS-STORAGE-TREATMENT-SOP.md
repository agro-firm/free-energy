# Gas Storage and Treatment SOP

## Holder
Monitor:
- volume %.
- pressure.
- condensate.
- CH₄/H₂S detectors.

Planning controls:
- 20% low cutout.
- 30% low warning.
- 75% generator demand enable.
- 90% high warning.
- 95% high-high action.

## Treatment
Daily:
- outlet H₂S.
- inlet/outlet pressure.
- flow.
- condensate.
- filter ΔP.

## H₂S media
Replace by:
- breakthrough.
- gas volume.
- pressure drop.
- vendor capacity.

Not by calendar alone.

## No bypass
Untreated gas never goes to generator.

## Leak
Alarm → isolate if safe → stop ignition → evacuate → specialist response.
