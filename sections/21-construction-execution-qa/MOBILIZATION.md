# Mobilization Plan

## Before entry
- NTP/workfront release.
- insurance/contracts.
- HSE plan.
- organization chart.
- emergency contacts.
- method statements for first activities.
- survey benchmark handover.

## Site setup
- secure boundary/fence as needed.
- controlled gate.
- worker induction point.
- first aid.
- fire extinguishers.
- temporary lighting.
- sanitation/welfare.
- drinking water.
- waste segregation.
- temporary power distribution.

## Compact-site policy
Permanent plot is not used as a large camp.

Preferred:
- site office outside/front-adjacent.
- worker welfare outside permanent process footprint.
- offsite bulk storage.
- just-in-time deliveries.

## Mobilization acceptance
- access clear.
- lane protected.
- emergency route.
- temporary drainage.
- electrical safe.
- welfare functional.
