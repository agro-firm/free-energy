# Generator + Electrical — Blueprint and XYZ Specification

## Coordinates
- origin southwest
- X=0–14 ft west→east
- Y=0–12 ft south→north
- Z vertical

## Zones
| Zone | X | Y | Area |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Gas/service | 0–3 | 0–12 | 36 ft² |
| Genset/mechanical | 3–9 | 0–12 | 72 ft² |
| Electrical | 9–14 | 0–12 | 60 ft² |

## Generator
Planning footprint:
- X≈3.25–8.75 ft
- Y≈4.5–7.5 ft
- ~5.5×3.0 ft envelope
- vendor exact dimensions govern

## Door
South-east:
- ~3.5–4 ft clear
- outward opening
- around X9.5–13.5, Y=0

## Air intake
South wall:
- centered near generator
- gross louver ~24×24 in minimum concept
- low level

## Hot-air exhaust
North wall:
- high level
- total fan airflow 1,500–2,000 m³/h
- location aligned to generator cooling/hot-air path

## Engine exhaust
- flexible section from engine
- muffler
- insulated pipe
- passes north wall/roof
- planning 1.5–2 inch class, VENDOR
- backpressure must remain below genset limit

## Gas entry
West wall around Y≈6 ft.
Sequence:
manual valve → NC gas solenoid → pressure switch/transmitter → flexible gas connector → engine mixer/regulator.

## Electrical panels
East wall:
- GB-501 generator breaker
- ATS-501
- ELP-501 essential load panel
- MTR-501 multifunction meter
- CP-501 controller

Panels bottom around 3.5–4 ft above floor, subject to code.

## Battery
12 V starter battery/charger near generator but away from hot exhaust and gas manifold; ventilated and protected.

## Gas/CO detectors
- CH₄/LEL sensor high in room
- CO detector in breathing/room zone
- H₂S monitoring as required from Section 10 safety review

## E-stop
Outside/adjacent to exit door.

## Earthing
Generator frame, panels, gas pipe, room metalwork connected to coordinated farm earthing system.
Neutral-earth bonding and switched-neutral arrangement are ENGINEER decisions.
