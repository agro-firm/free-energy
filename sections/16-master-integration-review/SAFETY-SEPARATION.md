# Master Safety and Separation Review

## Clean zone
- milk room.
- office/vet.
- feed.
- clean water.

## Dirty biological zone
- cow dirty lanes.
- waste receiving.
- digester.
- fertilizer.

## Gas/ignition zone
- digester gas outlet.
- gas holder.
- treatment.
- generator gas train.

## Traffic zone
- service lane.

## Critical separation laws
1. Milk route never crosses manure/digestate.
2. Feed route never uses gas/fertilizer service area.
3. Raw gas remains outdoors/ventilated until treated.
4. No open flame near gas systems.
5. Potable water protected from backflow.
6. Generator exhaust away from gas equipment and air intakes.
7. Fertilizer liquid never enters storm drain.
8. Service lane remains clear.

## Unresolved safety hold points
- hazardous-area classification.
- fire-service access compliance.
- exact gas equipment separation.
- lightning protection.
- emergency flare/vent location.
- confined-space procedures.
