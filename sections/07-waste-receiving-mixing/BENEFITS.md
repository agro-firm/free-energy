# Waste Receiving + Mixing — Benefits

## Stable digester feed
The digester receives controlled ~0.135 m³ batches instead of irregular raw manure dumps.

## Automatic dilution
Measured water prevents uncontrolled slurry consistency and makes total daily feed auditable.

## Pump protection
Coarse debris/grit removal reduces blockage and wear.

## Measurement
Load cells + water meter + slurry flow meter create three independent process checks:
1. manure mass
2. water volume
3. slurry volume

## Labor
Workers no longer manually carry/mix manure for every feed cycle.

## Safety
Covered sumps, emergency containment and high-high alarms reduce uncontrolled spills.

## Digester performance
More uniform feed timing can reduce hydraulic/organic shocks compared with infrequent large feeding.

## Water efficiency
The PLC records actual dilution instead of allowing uncontrolled hose use.

## Maintenance
The 10×14 ft layout keeps all pumps, valves, screen and controls concentrated in one serviceable zone.

## Data value
The section becomes the farm's manure mass-balance checkpoint, supporting future comparisons between:
- cows
- dung collected
- slurry fed
- gas produced
- fertilizer produced
