# Office + Vet + Biosecurity — Construction Plan

## Preconstruction
- confirm main public entry orientation
- confirm clean farm-side door
- confirm water/drain route
- select vaccine fridge dimensions
- coordinate AC and refrigerator heat rejection
- confirm CCTV/network route
- confirm medicine/LN₂ storage safety

## Sequence
1. set out 12×15 ft
2. foundations/floor/walls/roof
3. sanitary/water/electrical/data sleeves
4. internal partitions
5. wet-area waterproofing/finishes
6. office finishes
7. doors/locks/windows
8. handwash and vet sink
9. electrical/data panel
10. AC/ventilation
11. office/CCTV fitout
12. medicine cabinets
13. vaccine fridge + logger
14. vet/AI equipment
15. biosecurity bench/PPE system
16. commissioning/training

## Hold points
- floor drain/sleeves before slab
- partition dimensions
- wet-wall waterproofing
- earthing/electrical
- dedicated fridge circuit
- potable water and drainage test
- AC/ventilation
- CCTV coverage
- fridge 2–8°C stability test
- temperature alarm test
- UPS/power-fail test
- medicine-store lock/access test

## Commissioning
Before stocking medicines:
- run fridge empty long enough to verify stable temperature
- verify logger independently
- test power failure
- test alarm
- label shelves
- establish inventory log
- clean/disinfect vet area
