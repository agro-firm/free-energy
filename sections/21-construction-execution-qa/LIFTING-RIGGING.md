# Lifting and Rigging

## Lift plan
Required for equipment/steel lifts.

## Data
- load weight.
- center of gravity.
- lift points.
- radius.
- crane chart.
- ground bearing.
- rigging WLL.
- wind/weather.
- exclusion zone.
- signaler/rigger.

## Critical lift
Defined by project HSE/lifting procedure based on risk, not merely weight.

## Compact site
No suspended load over:
- people.
- live milk/feed area.
- gas equipment.
- public/service traffic.

## Delivery
Pre-plan crane position so Section 14 lane can be restored quickly.
