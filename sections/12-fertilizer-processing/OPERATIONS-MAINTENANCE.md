# Fertilizer Processing — Operations and Maintenance

## Daily
- buffer level
- liquid tank level
- press inspection
- run separator
- clean screen/press exterior
- move cake to dated bay
- inspect leachate
- record batch

## Drying
- turn/aerate
- monitor moisture
- protect from rain
- avoid packaging too wet

## Weekly
- grease/service press as vendor
- inspect screw/screens
- clean drains
- inspect curing bays
- inspect fans
- inspect liquid tank vent

## Monthly
- reconcile digestate in/cake/liquid
- test product moisture
- inspect pumps
- calibrate scale
- review bag inventory
- inspect pallets

## Quarterly
- lab test representative product/liquid
- assess separator capture
- inspect tank condition
- review agronomic liquid-use plan

## Annual
- press major service
- electrical inspection
- structural/roof inspection
- tank inspection
- review regulatory registrations/approvals
- update product specification

## KPIs
- kg cake/day
- cake TS %
- finished kg/day
- bags/month
- separator runtime
- kWh/day
- liquid m³/day
- capture %
- rejected/out-of-spec batches
