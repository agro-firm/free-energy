# Safety + Regulatory Skill

## Regulatory rule
Never invent legal separation distances, permit classes, pressure limits, hazardous zones or structural requirements.

Verify current requirements from official sources as relevant:
- Department of Livestock Services.
- Department of Environment.
- Fire Service and Civil Defence.
- Local building/land-use authority.
- Electrical utility/SREDA.
- Fertilizer/agricultural-input authority.

## Safety domains
Biogas flammability, H2S toxicity, confined spaces, pressure/vacuum, generator exhaust/CO, electrical shock/earthing, rotating machinery, animal handling, slippery floors, truck traffic and milk chemical hygiene.

## Required outputs before construction
Hazard register, emergency shutdown philosophy, gas detector plan, fire plan, evacuation/access, lockout/tagout, confined-space procedure and maintenance-clearance review.

## Rule
Concept plans are not construction approval. Licensed professionals and equipment vendors must finalize structural, gas, electrical, fire and sanitary details.
