# Construction Plant / Equipment Plan

## Earth/civil
As needed:
- excavator/backhoe.
- dump truck.
- plate compactor/roller.
- dewatering pump.
- concrete vibrator.
- rebar cutter/bender.
- mixer or ready-mix delivery/pump.
- survey instrument.

## Steel
- welding machines.
- cutting/grinding tools.
- torque tools.
- manlift/scaffold where approved.
- crane/HIAB as lift plan requires.

## MEP
- pipe threading/fusion tools.
- pressure-test pumps.
- cable pulling tools.
- insulation resistance tester.
- earth tester.
- calibration tools.

## Lifting
Crane capacity is not preselected.
Every critical lift uses:
- real weight.
- radius.
- rigging.
- ground bearing.
- chart.
- competent operator/rigger.

## Inspection
All plant has:
- operator.
- service record.
- pre-use checks.
- guards.
- certification where required.
