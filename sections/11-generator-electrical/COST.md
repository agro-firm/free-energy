# Generator + Electrical — Cost and Budget Report

## Basis
Bangladesh planning estimate, October 2026. Section 11 only.

## Public market references
Current Bangladesh listings:
- Lutian 5 kW petrol generator ~Tk61,500.
- 5 kW portable diesel generator ~Tk120,000.
- 5 kW gasoline/gas-type generator ~Tk75,000.
- 63 A single-phase ATS ~Tk4,500; other ATS assemblies are listed around Tk24,000.
- industrial exhaust-fan examples range around Tk7,500–20,000+ depending size/type.

## High-cost drivers
- imported dedicated biogas genset
- acoustic enclosure
- industrial ATS/switchgear
- long cable runs
- hazardous-area gas equipment
- premium detectors
- exhaust routing
- power-quality/soft-start equipment
- future solar/generator synchronization

## Exclusions
Section 09 holder, Section 10 treatment skid, Section 15 solar array/inverter, grid connection fees and statutory/professional fees unless quoted.
