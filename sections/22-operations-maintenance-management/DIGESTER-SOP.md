# Digester SOP

## Daily
- feed volume.
- temperature.
- pH.
- gas m³.
- gas pressure.
- digestate overflow.
- leak/odor.
- alarms.

## Planning operating references
- HRT ~37 days.
- pH ~6.8–7.5 planning.
- temperature ~25–35°C desirable.
- gas ~8.10–9.18 m³/day.

These are planning bands; process specialist/vendor controls.

## Feed upset
Do not compensate for poor gas by blindly increasing manure.

Check:
- pH.
- temperature.
- feed consistency.
- H₂S.
- leaks.
- biology.

## Manway
No routine entry.

## Recirculation
Run only by approved schedule/condition.
