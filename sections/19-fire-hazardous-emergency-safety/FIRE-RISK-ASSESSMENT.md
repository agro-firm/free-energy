# Plant Fire and Emergency Risk Assessment

## Method
Use a formal risk register:
- hazard.
- initiating event.
- people/assets exposed.
- existing safeguards.
- consequence.
- likelihood.
- risk.
- action.
- owner.
- due date.

## Immediate high-risk design holds
- C02 digester/generator ~2-ft closest plan gap.
- final gas hazardous-area extents.
- flare/safe-vent location.
- fire-water requirement.
- public-road/fire-appliance access.
- emergency assembly point outside hazard/traffic path.
