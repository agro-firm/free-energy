# Road and Gate Levels

## Survey
At site frontage record:
- road crown.
- near edge.
- far edge.
- shoulder.
- ditch invert.
- culvert/inlet.
- gate centerline.

## Gate grading goals
- no abrupt truck step.
- no site runoff discharged uncontrolled across road.
- no road runoff channeled directly into farm.
- maintain Section 14 swept path.

## Longitudinal lane
Do not freeze a single Y-slope before survey.

Civil designer selects a profile that:
- drains positively.
- respects road tie-in.
- avoids excessive fill.
- keeps building thresholds protected.
- keeps rear node accessible.

## Section 14 transverse
Retain:
```
1.5% fall from X51 to X65
≈64 mm across 14 ft
```
unless final drainage design deliberately revises it.
