# Gas Treatment — Operations and Maintenance

## Daily
- outlet H₂S
- flow
- inlet/outlet pressure
- condensate
- gas detector
- booster status
- filter ΔP

## Weekly
- drain KO/MS
- inspect leaks
- inspect sample ports
- inspect media-vessel temperatures externally
- review H₂S trend
- inspect booster

## Monthly
- verify H₂S sensor with reference/calibration
- inspect filter element
- inspect valve manifold
- review gas volume through each media batch
- inspect earth/bond
- check condensate drain seals

## Media service
Change based on:
- breakthrough
- gas volume
- pressure drop
- vendor capacity

Do not change only because a calendar date arrived.

## Quarterly
- gas meter verification
- gas detector calibration
- booster maintenance
- regulator inspection
- vessel sealing inspection

## Annual
- full gas-train leak test
- pressure instruments calibration
- replace filter elements as needed
- inspect vessels internally only under gas-safe procedure
- review treatment sizing against real H₂S data

## KPIs
- inlet H₂S ppm
- outlet H₂S ppm
- removal %
- kg media used
- m³ gas treated/kg media
- pressure drop
- condensate L
- booster kWh
- generator trips due gas quality
