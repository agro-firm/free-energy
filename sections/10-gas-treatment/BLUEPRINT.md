# Gas Treatment — Blueprint and XYZ Specification

## Local coordinates
- origin southwest
- X=0–10 ft west→east
- Y=0–8 ft south→north
- Z vertical

## Equipment zones
| Zone | X | Y | Area |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Inlet/KO | 0–2 | 0–8 | 16 ft² |
| Twin H₂S beds | 2–6 | 0–8 | 32 ft² |
| Polishing/meter/booster | 6–10 | 0–8 | 32 ft² |

## Inlet KO-401
Planning center:
- X≈1 ft
- Y≈4 ft
- low point at ~0.3–0.5 m above slab
- closed condensate drain to dirty-process container

## H₂S vessels
V-401A:
- center X≈3.0 ft, Y≈3 ft

V-401B:
- center X≈5.0 ft, Y≈3 ft

Planning:
- diameter ~0.30 m / 1 ft
- total vessel height ~1.2–1.4 m
- media bed ~0.70 m

Provide ~3 ft service access in front/north side by offsetting vessels as needed.

## Moisture separator MS-401
Center around:
- X≈6.8 ft
- Y≈3 ft

## Particulate filter PF-401
Downstream of MS-401.

## Gas meter FIT-401
Near X≈8 ft, Y≈3 ft.
Thermal mass or positive-displacement type compatible with wet/treated biogas.

## Booster B-401
Near X≈9 ft, Y≈5 ft.
Gas-compatible low-pressure blower.
Planning motor: ~0.37 kW ASSUMPTION.

## Outlet regulator PCV-401
Downstream of booster as generator vendor requires.

## Sample points
SP-401A: upstream of lead bed.
SP-401B: between lead/lag if practical.
SP-401C: final treated gas.

## H₂S monitor
AIT-401 final treated gas:
- low-range ppm sensor/analyzer
- target alarm below generator maximum

## Pressure
PIT-401 inlet.
PIT-402 final outlet.

## Pipe
DN40 / 1.5-inch class concept throughout primary line.
Low points drain to closed condensate system.

## Bypass rule
No full untreated bypass line to generator.

Crossover valves may:
- swap A/B lead-lag order
- isolate one vessel for maintenance
but gas must pass at least one approved H₂S vessel before outlet.
