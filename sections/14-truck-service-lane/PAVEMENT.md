# Truck / Service Lane — Pavement Design

## Concept
Rigid concrete lane for low-frequency but loaded farm trucks.

## Baseline structure
Planning only:
1. 150 mm reinforced/dowel-jointed RCC pavement
2. engineered compacted base/sub-base
3. prepared/compacted subgrade

## Concrete
Planning strength:
- M25/M30 class depending engineer
- broom/textured finish
- saw-cut joints
- sealed joints
- proper curing

## Reinforcement
Concept mesh/dowels are included in budget allowance, but final design depends on:
- axle load
- wheel load
- slab panel geometry
- subgrade CBR
- groundwater
- traffic repetitions

## Public benchmark
LGED August 2025 Zone-D lists 30 MPa RCC pavement concrete around:
```
Tk18,120.33/m³
```
excluding reinforcement in that item.

## Joints
Engineer to define:
- transverse contraction
- construction joints
- dowels
- isolation around structures
- sealant

## Edge
Outer drain/edge requires slab thickening or stable shoulder/curb detail so truck wheels do not break slab edge.

## Quality
- compaction tests
- concrete slump
- cube/cylinder strength
- thickness checks
- joint inspection
