- higher realized milk price.
- lower forage cost.

## Main financial levers
1. saleable milk L/day.
2. feed/forage cost.
3. realized milk price.
4. lactation/productivity.
5. fixed labor/OPEX.
6. final CAPEX.
7. energy self-consumption value.
8. fertilizer sale/use.

## FID rule
No full-project investment approval based only on engineering completeness.

Require:
- buyer/offtake.
- herd production/purchase data.
- 12-month feed plan.
- OPEX quotes.
- normalized CAPEX.
- financing.
- conservative/base/upside scenarios.
