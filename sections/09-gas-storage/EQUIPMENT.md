## Material requirements
Membrane supplier must state:
- H₂S compatibility
- methane permeability
- UV rating
- temperature range
- flame-retardant class
- tensile strength
- seam/weld method
- warranty
- maximum and minimum allowable pressure

## Public product context
- 10 m³ PVC double-layer balloons are sold commercially in South Asia.
- global 10 m³ PVC/TPU gas bags are also widely available.
- industrial double-membrane holders typically include level sensing, pressure control and sometimes support-air blowers, but many standard models begin at larger capacities.
