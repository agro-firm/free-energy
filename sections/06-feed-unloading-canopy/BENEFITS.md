# Feed Unloading Canopy — Benefits

## Weather protection
The canopy protects the most vulnerable transfer moment: moving dry feed from vehicle to store during rain.

## Land efficiency
The truck remains in the existing 14-ft service lane, so the canopy does not duplicate truck-parking land.

## Feed quality
Wet bags/bales can be detected and isolated before entering Section 05.

## Labor
The transfer distance is only about 10 ft from lane edge to store door, reducing manual carrying.

## Fresh-forage flexibility
The west 40 ft² zone provides short staging for:
```
300 kg/day normal
600 kg max / 2 days emergency
```
without contaminating the dry feed store.

## Vehicle safety
Marked parking, chocks and fixed unloading side reduce uncontrolled vehicle-worker movement.

## Drainage
Clean roof water and apron runoff are separated, keeping Section 05 dry.

## Equipment efficiency
One hand pallet truck and one folding trolley can support most level-ground movement without using a forklift for routine bag deliveries.
