# Master Construction Schedule

## Basis
Week 1 starts only after the affected workfront has:
- IFC/approved-for-construction drawing.
- survey control.
- safety release.
- material/vendor readiness.
- permit.

## Critical path
A05 → A08 → A21/A22 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A27.

## External constraints
Not guaranteed inside 40 weeks:
- FSCD approval.
- utility/net-metering approval.
- imported equipment customs.
- exceptional monsoon/flood event.
- major geotechnical redesign.

## Schedule control
Update weekly:
- baseline start/finish.
- actual.
- remaining.
- forecast.
- float.
- critical path.
- procurement constraint.
