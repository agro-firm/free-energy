## Not duplicate by default
Keep:
- local gas detectors required by each hazardous process.
- local PLC/VFD panels.
- local process drains.
- local commissioning needed for warranty.
- local equipment earthing/bonding.
- local PPE specific to process hazards.

## Deduction workflow
1. highlight overlapping line.
2. mark retained owner.
3. attach retained quote scope.
4. calculate exact superseded allowance.
5. issue Budget Transfer / Dedup Credit.
6. update forecast.

No arbitrary percentage deduction.
