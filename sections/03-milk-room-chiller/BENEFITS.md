# Milk Room + Chiller — Benefits

## Milk quality
Rapid cooling slows bacterial growth and preserves milk quality. FAO guidance identifies cooling to about 4°C as the preferred preservation method.

## Revenue protection
Better cold-chain control reduces the chance of spoilage, souring and buyer rejection.

## Logistics
A dispatch door directly onto the service lane means milk can be transferred without milk-truck staff entering cow/manure areas.

## Hygiene
Closed transfer, SS contact surfaces, CIP and washable room finishes reduce contamination opportunities.

## Energy integration
Critical refrigeration can be prioritized on solar/grid/biogas-generator power while the hot-water heater is shed during backup operation.

## Data
Temperature, volume, power and water records make milk handling auditable and allow performance improvement.

## Scalability
A 500 L cooler gives reserve without enlarging the 14×18 ft room, subject to actual equipment dimensions.
