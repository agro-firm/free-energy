# Master Architecture

## Architectural concept
A compact, linear-gradient farm where public/clean functions occupy the front, livestock/dairy occupy the middle, biological waste/process equipment moves progressively rearward, and gas/energy/fertilizer are concentrated at the back while a full-length east service lane connects all logistics.

## Master architectural principles
1. Clean-to-dirty progression front→rear.
2. One continuous east service spine.
3. Short feed and milk logistics.
4. Direct manure collection.
5. Compact gas chain.
6. Rooftop solar without extra ground use.
7. No decorative empty land that prevents functional maintenance.
8. Physical Sections 01–15 preserve their local designs.

## Main forms
### Section 01
Large naturally ventilated gable-roof cow shed with ~21° conceptual roof slope and rooftop solar.

### Sections 03–05 / 07 / 10–13
Compact utility/process buildings with simple maintainable forms, washable surfaces where required and direct relation to their process function.

### Section 06
Covered truck/feed unloading canopy.

### Section 08
Contained digester compound rather than habitable building.

### Section 09
Outdoor gas-storage zone with protective/operational enclosure only as required.

### Section 14
Flush service corridor, not a parking lot.

## Material language
Project-wide:
- RCC bases/slabs/tanks where process or structural requirements demand.
- galvanized/painted structural steel for sheds/canopies.
- durable metal roofing.
- masonry/light industrial walls where appropriate.
- stainless sanitary surfaces in milk contact/cleaning zones.
- corrosion-resistant process piping/supports.
- non-slip washable floors in wet/process zones.

## Climate response
- strong natural ventilation in cow shed.
- shading.
- roof drainage.
- monsoon-resistant detailing.
- corrosion protection.
- raised/controlled clean floors after surveyed flood/level design.

## Site architecture
The whole farm is organized by:
- DCF-1 coordinates.
- operational flows.
- safety separation.
- truck access.
- clean/dirty drainage.
- equipment replacement paths.
