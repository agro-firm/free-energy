# Office + Vet + Biosecurity — Operations and Maintenance

## Daily
- visitor log
- handwash/PPE supplies
- vaccine-fridge temperature
- fridge alarm status
- medicine-store lock
- office/CCTV status
- clean vet bench after use

## Every medicine/vaccine use
- record product
- animal ID
- dose
- batch/lot
- expiry
- date
- administrator
- withdrawal period where applicable

## Weekly
- verify fridge min/max
- inspect door gasket
- clean fridge exterior
- check UPS status
- review CCTV/NVR
- inspect PPE stock
- empty controlled waste as approved

## Monthly
- reconcile medicine inventory
- check expiries/FEFO
- clean AC filter
- test power-fail notification
- inspect LN₂ tank level and restraint
- inspect sink/drain leaks
- back up digital records

## Quarterly
- review biosecurity procedure
- check CCTV recording retention
- check temperature logger accuracy
- review AI/vet consumables
- test emergency contacts

## Annual
- professional electrical inspection
- AC service
- refrigerator service/calibration check
- CCTV storage review
- deep-clean medicine room
- refresh staff biosecurity training

## KPIs
- fridge temperature excursions
- medicine expiry loss
- visitor count
- biosecurity breaches
- treatment records completeness
- breeding/AI success metrics
- CCTV uptime
- backup-power events
