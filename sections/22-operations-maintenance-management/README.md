## Core staffing
- 1 farm/operations manager.
- 2 livestock/milking operators.
- 1 utility/process technician.
- 1 feed/cleaning/logistics worker.
- security/night monitoring and leave relief as required.
- veterinarian/AI and technical specialists on-call.

Actual employment roster must comply with applicable Bangladesh employment law, contracts and rest/leave requirements.
