# Gas Treatment — Architecture and Skid Layout

## 1. Design intent
Section 10 is an **open-sided weather-protected gas-treatment skid**, not a sealed room. It must keep equipment dry while continuously ventilating any gas leak.

## 2. Zone geometry
```
8 × 10 = 80 ft²
80 ×0.09290304 = 7.4322 m²
```

Local:
- X=0–10 ft west→east
- Y=0–8 ft south→north
- Section 09 inlet west
- Section 11 outlet east

## 3. Three bands
### Inlet/knockout — X 0–2 ft
```
2×8 =16 ft²
```
Includes:
- inlet valve
- pressure/sample point
- knockout/condensate pot
- inlet H₂S sample point

### H₂S treatment — X 2–6 ft
```
4×8 =32 ft²
```
Includes:
- V-401A lead vessel
- V-401B lag vessel
- crossover valves
- differential pressure points
- media-removal clearance

### Polishing/outlet — X 6–10 ft
```
4×8 =32 ft²
```
Includes:
- coalescing/fine-moisture separator
- optional desiccant cartridge
- particulate filter
- gas meter
- outlet H₂S monitor/sample
- gas-compatible booster/regulator
- generator isolation valve

Area:
```
16+32+32=80 ft²
```

## 4. Shelter
Concept:
- galvanized steel skid/canopy
- open mesh/open sides
- weather roof around 9–10 ft
- no solid walls trapping gas
- non-sparking access hardware where appropriate
- lockable perimeter if needed

## 5. Scrubber vessels
Planning each:
- internal diameter ~300 mm
- media bed ~25 kg
- media bulk density assumption ~500 kg/m³
- media bed volume ~0.05 m³ / 50 L
- bed height ~0.70 m
- total vessel height ~1.2–1.4 m including support/headspace

Actual vessel wall, pressure rating, distributor and drain are VENDOR/ENGINEER.

## 6. Access
Maintain:
- front valve access
- overhead media fill access
- bottom media discharge/container space
- removable vessel path
- no pipe crossing worker head/foot path without protection

## 7. Floor
- reinforced concrete skid slab
- slight slope to perimeter
- no open gas/condensate drain
- bunded/controlled condensate collection
- media-change tarp/container area

## 8. Weather
Rainwater remains outside process drain.
Canopy roof → clean stormwater.

## 9. Hazardous area
Electrical panel/VFD should be outside the classified gas zone unless properly rated. Final zone extent is engineered from vents, valves and gas connections.

## 10. Separation
No:
- feed
- milk
- fertilizer
- welding storage
- batteries
- open flames
inside Section 10.
