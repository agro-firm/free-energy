# Site Logistics

## DCF constraints
- east service lane X51–65 stays open.
- no contractor container permanently in lane.
- no bulk rebar/steel pile in clean milk/feed route.
- no gas/hot-work storage near Sections 08–11.

## Delivery booking
All large deliveries:
- booked time.
- unloading point.
- spotter.
- crane/forklift plan where needed.
- route clear.

## Laydown strategy
### Offsite/front-adjacent preferred
- structural steel bundles.
- solar pallets.
- equipment crates.

### Onsite short-term
- section-specific next-use materials only.

## Dirty/clean segregation
Construction traffic is controlled so finished milk/feed areas are not contaminated by later dirty civil work.

## Sequence principle
Finish the rear underground/process civil before final lane/clean finishes where possible.
