# 23 — Business Model, Revenue, Profitability, Cash Flow & ROI

## Status
Financial-model framework complete using current 2026 reference prices plus editable scenarios.

## Main finding
At the current engineering baseline of roughly 140 L milk/day with farmgate milk around Tk53/L and purchased feed near current market references, the full integrated 20-cow project is not supported economically by milk revenue alone.

## Capital layers
- Plant/preconstruction/safety: Tk26,972,958.
- Illustrative 20-cow purchase at Tk250k each: Tk5,000,000.
- Illustrative 3-month working capital: Tk1,388,125.
- Illustrative new-project funding: Tk33,361,083.

Do not add cow capital if animals are already owned.

## Scenario headline
| Case | Annual revenue/value | Annual OPEX | EBITDA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Conservative | Tk1.85m | Tk6.02m | -Tk4.18m |
| Base | Tk3.09m | Tk5.55m | -Tk2.46m |
| Upside / target | Tk7.66m | Tk6.31m | +Tk1.35m |

The upside case requires substantially higher milk output/price and lower forage cost than the current baseline.
