# Truck / Service Lane — Emergency Access

## Purpose
The lane is designed to remain physically available for:
- ambulance/small emergency vehicle
- maintenance response
- fire-response approach where geometry permits

## Important limitation
This documentation does **not** certify the lane as code-compliant fire access.

Current local requirements must verify:
- width
- clear height
- turning radius
- gate
- fire-appliance weight
- distance to buildings/process equipment
- dead-end requirements

## Operational rule
Keep full 14 ft unobstructed outside active loading.

## Gate
Emergency opening:
- minimum lane width
- lock/key/emergency access procedure
- no inward obstruction

## Gas/process emergency
During Section 08–11 gas incident:
- keep vehicle approach outside hazardous zone as directed by emergency plan
- do not route responders through active gas release

## Water
Fire-water demand is **not** included in Section 13's 10 m³ domestic/process water storage unless separately engineered.
