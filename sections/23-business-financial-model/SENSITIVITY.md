# Sensitivity Analysis

## Variables
### Milk
- L/day.
- saleable %.
- Tk/L.

### Feed
- concentrate price.
- roughage price.
- forage sourcing cost.
- feed efficiency/waste.

### Labor
- payroll.
- relief/security.
- specialist contracts.

### Energy
- self-consumption.
- tariff.
- export settlement.
- generator availability.

### Fertilizer
- solids capture.
- approval status.
- selling price.

### Capital
- final vendor quotes.
- dedup credits.
- livestock cost.
- authority-driven additions.

## Key thresholds
Base break-even milk price:
~Tk102.72/L.

Upside break-even:
~Tk56.47/L.

## Sensitivity priority
1. milk L/day.
2. feed BDT/day.
3. milk price.
4. labor/fixed OPEX.
5. CAPEX.
6. energy value.
7. fertilizer value.

Small equipment savings do not compensate for weak milk/feed unit economics.
