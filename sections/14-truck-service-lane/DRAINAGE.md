# Truck / Service Lane — Drainage

## Crossfall
Building/loading side high.
Outer boundary/drain low.

```
1.5% across 14 ft
=64 mm fall
```

## Outer drain
Concept:
- covered/grated edge drain
- approximately 300×300 mm planning
- entire 95 ft length
- traffic-rated at crossings
- removable cleaning covers

Final hydraulic section from local rainfall/catchment.

## Clean runoff
Lane rainwater is normally stormwater if uncontaminated.

## Loading-node controls
### Feed
Dry feed debris is swept before rain.
Section 06 apron drain may connect only after debris control.

### Milk
No milk/CIP wastewater goes into lane storm drain.

### Fertilizer
No liquid digestate/leachate enters lane storm drain.
Section 12 retains dirty liquids internally.

- adjacent clean roof catchments if connected
and uses current local IDF data.

## Longitudinal grade
Surveyed site controls.
