# Survey and Setting-Out Tolerances

These are **project QA targets**, not a substitute for licensed-surveyor or structural specifications.

## Coordination targets
- permanent benchmark repeat: ±5 mm vertical.
- secondary site spot check: ±10 mm vertical.
- building corner horizontal setting-out: ±10 mm.
- drain invert: ±5 mm where practical.
- finished floor: ±10 mm.
- lane/pavement level: ±10 mm.
- equipment anchor setting: vendor/structural tolerance, often tighter.

## Boundary
Legal boundary tolerance/authority is controlled by the licensed survey and cadastral records, not these internal targets.

## Rule
Where vendor/structural/civil specification is tighter, the tighter requirement governs.
