# Master Commissioning Plan

## Stage A — civil
- dimensions.
- drainage.
- water tightness.
- access.
- door/vehicle clearance.

## Stage B — electrical
- insulation.
- earth.
- breakers.
- ATS.
- E-stop.
- no-backfeed.

## Stage C — water
- disinfection.
- quality.
- pump failover.
- tank alarms.

## Stage D — manure/digester
- water commissioning.
- flow meters.
- interlocks.
- inoculation/feed ramp.

## Stage E — gas
- leak test.
- P/V protection.
- holder level.
- H₂S cleanup.
- condensate.

## Stage F — generator
- treated-gas acceptance.
- no-load.
- 4 kW load.
- ATS/load shed.

## Stage G — fertilizer
- separator capture.
- cake moisture.
- liquid volume.
- product QA.

## Stage H — solar
- strings.
- earth/SPD.
- anti-islanding.
- utility approval.

## Integrated acceptance
Run at least a controlled multi-day operating trial and reconcile:
water + manure + slurry + gas + kWh + digestate + fertilizer.
