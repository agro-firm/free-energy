# Site Layout Skill

## Priorities
- Milk room directly faces service lane.
- Feed store and unloading canopy directly face service lane.
- Waste receiving is immediately downstream of cow-shed gutters.
- Digester is beside waste receiving.
- Gas holder follows digester.
- Gas treatment sits between gas holder and generator.
- Fertilizer zone is close to digestate outlet and service lane.
- Office/biosecurity is near public entrance and away from dirty processing.

## No-dead-space law
Any apparently open area must have a defined function: drainage, ventilation, maintenance, fire/emergency access, truck movement, washing, inspection, or buffer.

## Acceptance checks
1. All geometry inside boundary.
2. No unintended overlaps.
3. Truck reaches milk, feed, fertilizer and service points.
4. Clean and dirty routes are visibly separated.
5. Equipment replacement path exists.
6. Site area balance passes the engineering-math skill.
