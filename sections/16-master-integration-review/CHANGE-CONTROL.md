# Master Change Control

Any major change must state:
1. requested change.
2. reason.
3. sections affected.
4. geometry impact.
5. process impact.
6. water impact.
7. power impact.
8. safety/regulatory impact.
9. CAPEX impact.
10. image impact.

## Version rule
Current integrated concept:
**PLANT-V1.0**

A change affecting footprint/capacity/topology becomes:
- V1.1 for minor controlled change.
- V2.0 for major plant reconfiguration.

## No silent changes
Vendor substitutions must update:
- section equipment.
- calculations.
- interfaces.
- master integration.
