# Progress Measurement

## Principle
Progress follows measurable installed work, not subjective "almost done."

## Examples
Civil:
- excavation m³.
- concrete m³.
- rebar t.
- floor m².

Steel:
- fabricated/erected t or defined milestones.

Piping:
- installed/tested length.

Electrical:
- cable installed/terminated/tested.

Equipment:
- delivered.
- installed.
- mechanically complete.
- SAT.

## Weighted progress
Package weight based on contract/BOQ value or approved earned-value basis.

## Rule
Delivered equipment is not 100% installation progress.

## Schedule
Use:
- baseline.
- actual.
- remaining.
- forecast.
- float.
