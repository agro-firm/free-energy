# Generator + Electrical — Canonical IMAGE.md

## Fixed geometry
- room 14 ft long ×12 ft wide
- clear height ~10 ft
- west 3×12 ft gas/service strip
- center 6×12 ft genset zone
- east 5×12 ft electrical zone
- south low intake
- north high hot-air exhaust
- engine exhaust separate to outdoors

## Required equipment
- 5 kW genset
- anti-vibration base
- gas shutoff/solenoid/flex
- exhaust flex/muffler/pipe
- ventilation fans/louvers
- generator breaker
- ATS
- Essential Load Board
- meter/controller
- starter battery
- CH₄ detector
- CO detector
- E-stop
- earthing

## Image math
```
5 kW rated
4 kW target
2.23 m³/h gas @4 kW
21.7 A rated @230 V
17.4 A @4 kW
ventilation≈1500–2000 m³/h
```

## Never show
- generator connected directly to untreated gas
- grid and generator paralleled
- solar and generator paralleled without approved system
- exhaust venting into room
- gas storage inside room
- fuel cans/feed/chemicals stored in room
- panels directly above gas valves
- blocked service access
