# Truck / Service Lane — Benefits

## Land efficiency
One 14-ft corridor serves multiple logistics functions instead of separate roads.

## Milk hygiene
Direct truck access to Section 03 avoids dirty-zone crossing.

## Feed efficiency
Section 06 side-unloading minimizes carrying distance.

## Fertilizer dispatch
Rear node allows bags/liquid pickup without entering clean dairy areas.

## Maintenance
Heavy service equipment can approach generator/digester systems.

## Emergency readiness
Keeping the corridor clear provides a defined response route, subject to local compliance review.

## Drainage
RCC surface prevents mud and provides controlled stormwater flow.
