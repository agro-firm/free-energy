# Punch List / Handover

## Punch classes
A:
safety/operation critical; must close before startup/handover.

B:
functional/quality; close before final acceptance unless formally agreed.

C:
minor cosmetic/document item; controlled closeout.

## Handover dossier
- approved as-built.
- test certificates.
- calibration.
- FAT/SAT.
- O&M.
- warranties.
- spares.
- training records.
- authority approvals.
- equipment list/serials.
- software backups.
- punch closure.

## Training
Operators demonstrate:
- normal operation.
- emergency stop.
- maintenance.
- alarm response.
