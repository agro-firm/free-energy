# 12 — Fertilizer Processing

## Status
Planning baseline. Detailed construction design will be added later.

## Baseline
16 × 22 ft

## Purpose
Processes digestate into manageable solid and liquid fertilizer products.

## Inputs
Digestate from the biogas digester.

## Outputs
Dewatered/cured solid fertilizer and liquid fertilizer for dispatch or field use.

## Interfaces
Digester, service lane, water/drainage and packaging/dispatch.

## Current design notes
Concept includes separation/screw press, curing/drying, screening, bagging and covered storage. Product quality and N-P-K must be verified by testing.
