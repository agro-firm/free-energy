# Master Commissioning

## Stage A — civil / architectural
Verify:
- dimensions.
- access.
- slopes.
- drains.
- water tightness.
- hygiene finishes.
- truck clearances.

## Stage B — electrical
- insulation resistance.
- earthing.
- breaker/protection.
- ATS.
- E-stops.
- no-backfeed.

## Stage C — water
- tank clean/disinfection.
- quality test.
- pump duty/standby.
- pressure.
- levels/alarms.

## Stage D — manure/slurry
- scraper.
- screen.
- batch weighing/flow.
- pumps/mixer.
- overflow.

## Stage E — digester
- water commissioning.
- leak/gas-tight tests.
- instrument calibration.
- inoculation/startup.
- controlled feed ramp.

## Stage F — gas
- P/V safety.
- holder level.
- leak test.
- H₂S treatment.
- condensate.
- flow/pressure.

## Stage G — generator
- treated-gas readiness.
- no-load.
- loaded run.
- ATS.
- load shedding.
- exhaust/CO.

## Stage H — fertilizer
- separator throughput.
- cake/liquid mass balance.
- product quality.
- bagging.

## Stage I — solar
- strings.
- polarity.
- insulation.
- earth/SPD.
- inverter.
- anti-islanding.
- utility/net-meter process.

## Integrated acceptance
Run a controlled multi-day whole-plant trial and reconcile:
- milk.
- water.
- dung/slurry.
- gas.
- kWh.
- digestate.
- fertilizer.
- alarms.

## Handover
Require:
- O&M.
- as-built.
- warranties.
- spares.
- training.
- software backups.
- punch closure.
