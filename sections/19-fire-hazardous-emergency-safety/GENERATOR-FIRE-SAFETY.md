# Generator Fire / Exhaust / Electrical Safety

## Gas
Only Section 10 treated gas enters Section 11.
NC gas solenoid closes on stop/fire/gas alarm.

## Exhaust
- gas-tight joints.
- insulated/guarded hot surfaces.
- outdoor discharge.
- separation from gas-intake/vent and air intake verified.

## CO
Fixed CO alarm.
No operation if exhaust integrity is uncertain.

## Fire detection
Heat or appropriate multisensor detector subject to fire-plan design.

## Electrical
- 32 A generator breaker concept.
- ATS interlock.
- no grid backfeed.
- E-stop.
- earthing.
- LOTO.

## Digester-generator proximity
DCF-1 closest plan gap is ~2 ft where projections overlap.

This remains **HIGH HOLD**.

Acceptable resolution may require:
- generator relocation.
- digester/gas-equipment relocation.
- noncombustible/fire-rated barrier.
- reorientation of exhaust/openings.
- hazardous-area confirmation.
- authority acceptance.

Section 19 does not declare the 2-ft gap acceptable.
