## 5. Pump hydraulic power
Assume:
- Q=50 L/min=0.000833 m³/s
- TDH=20 m
- pump efficiency=45%

```
P_h=ρgQH
=1000×9.81×0.000833×20
≈163.5 W
```

Input:
```
163.5/0.45
≈363 W
≈0.49 HP
```

A 0.75–1 HP pump provides practical margin. Baseline uses 1 HP duty/standby.
