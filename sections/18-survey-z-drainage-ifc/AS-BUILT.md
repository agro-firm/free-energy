# As-Built Survey Requirements

## Underground
Survey before backfill:
- drain pipes.
- water.
- gas.
- electrical ducts.
- sleeves.
- sumps.
- foundations.

## Finished
Survey:
- building corners.
- FFL.
- lane edges/center.
- drain inverts/covers.
- tank/manhole levels.
- equipment pads.
- gate/road tie-in.

## Deliverables
- as-built DWG.
- PDF.
- CSV.
- photos.
- utility crossing register.
- deviations from IFC.

## Rule
Do not accept hidden utility work without coordinate/elevation record.
