# Gas Treatment — Safety and Hazard Report

## Hazards
- methane flammability
- H₂S toxicity
- low-pressure vacuum/overpressure
- spent-media sulfur/self-heating
- condensate exposure
- electrical ignition
- pressurized filter/vessel opening
- booster rotating machinery

## H₂S
OSU notes H₂S is corrosive in wet biogas and toxic at elevated concentrations.

Controls:
- area gas detection
- outlet ppm monitoring
- sample ports
- no smell-based testing
- ventilation
- PPE

## Methane
- open-sided skid
- no flame/smoking
- leak test
- hazardous-area electrical review
- bonding/earthing

## Vessel opening
Before opening media vessel:
- isolate both sides
- depressurize/purge
- gas-test
- verify no trapped pressure
- PPE
- controlled media container

## Spent media
Some sulfur-loaded media may heat on air exposure.
Follow supplier SDS.
Do not pile spent media in open air.

## Booster
- guarded
- gas-compatible
- suction low-pressure trip
- discharge high-pressure trip
- emergency stop

## Condensate
Treat as contaminated process liquid.
No open cup/bucket under live gas drain.

## Bypass
Untreated bypass to generator is prohibited.

## Fire
If gas leak/fire:
- stop ignition
- isolate gas if safe
- evacuate
- fire response
- do not open scrubber vessel

## Electrical
Panel outside classified area where feasible.
All in-zone equipment selected by hazardous-area engineer.

## Pressure
This remains low-pressure biogas service.
Do not apply compressed-natural-gas practices/setpoints.
