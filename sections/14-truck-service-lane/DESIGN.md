# Truck / Service Lane — Functional Design

## One-truck-at-a-time rule
The 14-ft corridor is not two-way.
No passing is expected.

## Preferred vehicle operation
If front-road geometry allows:
1. spotter stops pedestrian activity
2. truck reverses into lane
3. truck proceeds to assigned loading node
4. loading/unloading
5. truck exits forward

This improves visibility on departure.

If reverse-in from the public road is unsafe/impractical, use forward-in and controlled reverse-out with spotter.

## Speed
Internal operational target:
- walking speed / very low speed
- signage concept 5–10 km/h maximum
Final site policy may select lower.

## Milk pickup
- clean truck stop
- engine off where practical
- hose connection from Section 03
- no fertilizer/feed operation simultaneously beside milk truck

## Feed delivery
- truck stops parallel to Section 06
- wheel chocks
- covered transfer
- lane reopened immediately after unloading

## Fertilizer loading
- pallets/bags staged inside Section 12
- load truck from rear node
- no wet digestate spill into lane drain

## Generator/digester maintenance
Lane allows service technicians and equipment delivery.
Large crane/boom operations require separate lift plan and may temporarily close lane.

## Veterinary/emergency
Small vehicles can use corridor without special loading setup.

## Pedestrian safety
At loading nodes:
- painted 4-ft worker side
- high-visibility PPE
- spotter
- engine/brake/chocks
- no worker between moving truck and wall

## Parking
No overnight truck parking.
No pallets, fertilizer bags, feed sacks or waste stored on lane.
