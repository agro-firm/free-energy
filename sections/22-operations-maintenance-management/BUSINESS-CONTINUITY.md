# Business Continuity

## Priority 1 — animal water
If main pumps fail:
- standby pump.
- OHT gravity.
- emergency water supply.

## Priority 2 — milk cooling
If chiller fails:
- immediate technician.
- backup power if electrical.
- transfer to approved alternative cold storage if available.
- evaluate/reject temperature-abused milk.

## Grid outage
- essential board via biogas generator if gas ready.
- shed noncritical loads.
- solar baseline anti-islands.

## Feed delay
Maintain planned short stock.
Emergency:
- approved alternate supplier/ration only with nutrition/vet review.

## Waste/digester outage
Use Section 07 emergency capacity only temporarily.
Reduce wash water.
Arrange recovery.

## Gas system fault
Hold gas use/feed as safe.
Preserve P/V protection.
No raw-gas vent improvisation.

## Disease event
Restrict movement/visitors.
Veterinarian/DLS guidance.
Separate affected animals.
Increase sanitation/vector control.

## Flood/monsoon
Protect:
- feed.
- electrical.
- medicines.
- milk.
- gas/electrical areas.
Follow Section 18/19 emergency plan.

## Staff shortage
Relief roster and cross-trained backup.
Critical milking/water tasks always covered.
