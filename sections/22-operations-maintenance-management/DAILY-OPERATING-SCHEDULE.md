# Daily Operating Schedule

## Automated background
Slurry feed planning windows:
```
00:00 / 06:00 / 12:00 / 18:00
```
subject to Section 07/08 permissives.

Generator:
level/demand controlled; no fixed clock schedule.

Solar:
daylight automatic.

## 04:30–05:00
- night handover.
- cow count/health check.
- water/trough check.
- milk room/chiller status.
- alarms.

## 05:00–06:30
- pre-milking hygiene.
- morning milking.
- milk volume/temperature.
- start rapid cooling.

## 06:30–07:30
- milk equipment cleaning/CIP.
- fresh ration/feed.
- scraper/manure inspection.
- cow-yard group movement as scheduled.

## 07:30–09:00
- manager/utility handover.
- dashboard review.
- water/gas/digester/solar check.
- feed inventory.
- biosecurity/visitor readiness.

## 09:00–12:00
- preventive maintenance.
- feed deliveries.
- vet/breeding work if scheduled.
- cleaning.
- records/admin.

## 12:00–13:00
- slurry batch review.
- animal/water/feed check.
- shift handover.

## 13:00–15:00
- fertilizer separator daily run if buffer/capacity ready.
- solar/daytime high-load work.
- maintenance.
- dispatch/deliveries.

## 15:00–16:30
- evening ration preparation.
- cow observation.
- milk-room preparation.

## 16:30–18:00
- evening milking.
- chilling.
- milk recording.

## 18:00–19:00
- slurry batch.
- cleaning/CIP.
- evening feeding/water.
- gas/process checks.

## 19:00–20:00
- herd final check.
- doors/locks.
- alarms.
- inventory exception.
- handover/night monitoring.
