# Cow Shed Skill

## Baseline
- Footprint: 26 × 48 ft = 1,248 ft².
- Capacity: 20 adult cows.
- Prefer two rows of 10 where practical.
- Roof supports the 15 kWp solar concept subject to structural verification.

## Must include
- Feed alley/manger.
- Drinking-water access.
- Non-slip washable floor.
- Manure gutters and automatic scraper interfaces.
- Natural cross-ventilation and fans as required.
- Inspection lighting.
- Safe animal restraint/handling.
- Two stalls capable of temporary use for treatment or calving.

## Interface points
- Manure discharge to waste zone.
- Feed route from feed store.
- Water supply.
- Power for scraper, fans and lights.
- Milk route to clean milk room.

## Rule
Do not reduce animal working/resting space merely to fit utility equipment. Move utilities first.
