# Emergency Shutdown Philosophy

## ESD levels
### ESD-0 Local stop
Single equipment fault.

### ESD-1 Process area emergency
Gas/H₂S/CO alarm in one rear zone.

### ESD-2 Site emergency
Confirmed major gas release/fire or multiple-zone alarm.

## General fail-safe
- gas automatic valves fail closed where specified.
- P/V safety relief remains mechanically available.
- fire alarm remains powered.
- essential alarm/communications stay active.

## Gas emergency
- stop generator/booster.
- close downstream gas isolation where safe.
- hold/reduce upstream feed.
- preserve digester/holder pressure-vacuum protection.
- evacuate gas zone.

## Generator fire
- gas solenoid closes.
- generator breaker trips.
- ATS returns normal source if safe.
- engine stops.
- fire alarm.
- ventilation response follows approved fire cause/effect.

## Electrical fire
- isolate affected board/source.
- solar DC remains live when illuminated until properly isolated.

## Manure/digester emergency
Never close a required relief path merely to "isolate" the plant.
