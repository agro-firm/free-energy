# Waste Receiving + Mixing — Safety and Hazard Report

## 1. Main hazards
- H₂S from decomposing manure
- methane from stagnant organic material
- oxygen-deficient atmosphere in sumps
- biological exposure
- slips/splashes
- rotating mixer
- pump/electrical hazards
- confined spaces
- manual screen cleaning
- pressure from blocked slurry line

## 2. Confined-space rule
RS-101 and ES-101 are **no-entry process sumps** during normal operation.

If entry is ever required:
- formal confined-space procedure
- isolation
- atmospheric testing
- ventilation
- rescue plan
- trained personnel
- permit

Never enter to retrieve tools or clear blockage casually.

## 3. H₂S / methane
Controls:
- covers
- venting
- semi-open architecture
- avoid manure stagnation
- optional fixed gas detector after risk assessment
- no smoking/open flame
- electrical equipment appropriate to environment

## 4. Mixer
- guarded drive/coupling
- interlocked cover if practical
- lockout/tagout before maintenance
- no hand/tool insertion while energized

## 5. Pumps
- isolation before removal
- lifting point for submersible/heavy pump
- no pulling by cable
- motor overload
- dry-run/no-flow detection

## 6. Screen cleaning
- long-handled tool or removable basket
- gloves/face protection
- segregated foreign material disposal
- handwashing afterward

## 7. Biological hygiene
PPE:
- rubber boots
- gloves
- eye/face protection for splash tasks
- washable clothing

No eating/drinking in process area.

## 8. Spill/overflow
All process spills contained within curbed dirty area. No discharge to stormwater.

## 9. Electrical
Panel above splash level, lockable, IP-rated, earthed. E-stop clearly marked.

## 10. Chemicals
No strong disinfectant/acid/alkali should be dumped into the process without confirming digester compatibility.

## 11. Fire/gas
This section is not the biogas gas-handling zone, but methane may still be generated locally. Final hazardous-area classification must be completed by qualified engineer.
