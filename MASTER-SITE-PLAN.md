# Master Site Plan — DCF-1

## Functional master zoning

### Front band — Y0–18
Public, clean and incoming logistics:
- 04 Office/vet/biosecurity.
- 13 Water utility.
- 05 Feed store.
- 06 Feed unloading canopy.
- east Section 14 lane.

### Dairy band — Y18–36
- 01 Cow shed west.
- B01 clean cow-to-milk transfer.
- 03 Milk room on lane.
- milk truck pickup from Section 14.

### Waste band — Y37–47
- 01 cow shed west.
- 07 waste receiving/mixing.
- B03 service/maintenance strip.

### Digester band — Y47–63
- 01 cow shed west.
- 08 digester compound.
- B04 service strip.

### Energy transition — Y63–79
- 02 cow yard west/rear.
- 09 gas holder.
- 10 gas treatment.
- 11 generator/electrical.
- direct Section 14 service access.

### Rear product band — Y79–95
- Section 02 yard continues to Y94 west.
- Section 12 fertilizer processing east/central.
- direct rear-lane pickup.

## Master adjacency laws
- 06 must touch 05 and 14.
- 03 must touch/serve 14 and connect to 01 via clean transfer.
- 07 must directly receive from 01.
- 08 must directly follow 07.
- gas path must follow 08→09→10→11.
- 12 must remain accessible from rear Section 14 lane.
- Section 15 stays on Section 01 roof.

## No-build meaning
Open residual zones are functional:
- animal transfer.
- hygiene separation.
- process maintenance.
- drainage.
- valves/pipes.
- emergency access.
- equipment removal.
