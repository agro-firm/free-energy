# Generator + Electrical Skill

## Baseline
- Generator: 5 kW rated.
- Normal planning load: about 4 kW.
- Room: 12 × 14 ft.

## Energy check
At 60% methane, planning biogas thermal energy ≈ 0.60 × 9.97 = 5.98 kWh thermal per m³.
At 30% electrical efficiency, electricity ≈ 1.79 kWh per m³.
Daily gross at 8.10–9.18 m³/day ≈ 14.5–16.4 kWh/day.
At about 4 kW operating load, runtime ≈ 3.6–4.1 h/day.

## Must include
- Engine protections.
- Exhaust and ventilation.
- Automatic gas isolation.
- Emergency stop.
- Electrical distribution/ATS strategy.
- Earthing and overcurrent protection.
- Metering of kWh and runtime.

## Rule
Generator size follows available fuel and operating load, not room size.
