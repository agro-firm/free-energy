# Records and As-Built

## Construction records
- survey.
- IR.
- MAR.
- test.
- concrete reports.
- weld/bolt.
- pressure tests.
- cable tests.
- calibration.
- photos.
- NCR/RFI.

## As-built capture
Before cover:
- underground route.
- invert.
- sleeve.
- foundation.
- cable duct.

At completion:
- all section corners/FFLs.
- equipment.
- utilities.
- panel schedules.
- pipe tags.
- valve tags.

## Format
- editable source.
- PDF.
- CSV/register.
- photo archive.

## Rule
No hidden service is closed without record.
