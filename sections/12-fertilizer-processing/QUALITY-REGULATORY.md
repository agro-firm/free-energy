# Fertilizer Processing — Quality and Regulatory Report

## Farm-use vs commercial product
### Farm-use digestate
May be applied internally only after:
- suitable agronomic analysis
- crop/soil planning
- environmental controls

### Commercial bagged product
Requires current Bangladesh legal/regulatory verification, registration/approval, product specification, labeling and quality testing before sale.

## Current Bangladesh context
The Department of Agricultural Extension maintains fertilizer registration services for production/distribution, and the Ministry of Agriculture publishes specifications/notifications for organic fertilizer products.

DAE citizen-charter information currently lists fertilizer/fertilizer-product registration services.

References:
- https://dae.gov.bd/
- https://moa.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6940329b35ce18e1c055f3f1

## 2025 draft-law caution
A 2025 **preliminary draft** Fertilizer Management Act explicitly includes organic fertilizer in the definition of fertilizer.

That draft is not treated here as proof of the currently effective law.

Reference:
https://dae.gov.bd/sites/default/files/files/dae.portal.gov.bd/notices/07a241ce_15ce_4ba7_8d55_74ea37636de1/2025-03-17-08-38-e76aee7f5dc14d55c389b834d55725c8.pdf

## Laboratory tests
Before any nutrient claim:
- moisture
- total solids
- organic matter / TOC
- total N
- available/total P
- K
- pH
- EC
- C:N ratio where relevant
- pathogens where required
- heavy metals where required

## Batch records
Each commercial batch should link:
- production date
- separator run
- curing period
- moisture result
- lab certificate
- net bag weight
- lot number
- source digestate batch

## Claims
Never print:
- guaranteed N-P-K
- “pathogen-free”
- organic certification
- registration number
until actually supported.

## Commercial packaging
Final label must follow current DAE/MoA requirements and approved specification.

## Distributor registration
Current DAE citizen-charter information lists registration services for fertilizer/fertilizer-product distributors. Confirm current fee, documents and timelines at application.
