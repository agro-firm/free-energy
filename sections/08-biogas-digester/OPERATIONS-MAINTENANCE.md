# Biogas Digester — Operations and Maintenance

## Daily
- feed volume
- gas production
- digester temperature
- pH
- gas pressure
- digestate overflow
- leak/odor walkaround
- gas alarm status
- recirculation status

## Weekly
- inspect gas pipe/condensate
- inspect outlet chamber
- inspect P/V relief externally
- check recirculation pump
- sample digestate
- check compound drainage

## Monthly
- trend HRT/feed/gas
- inspect manway/seals externally
- check pressure instrument
- verify level sensor
- inspect corrosion/coatings visible above grade
- review condensate volumes

## Quarterly
- process review: pH, TS/VS if available
- gas methane test if available
- verify relief/valve maintenance schedule
- inspect settlement/cracks
- check groundwater/drainage condition

## Annual
- structural engineer visual review if required
- gas system inspection
- instrument calibration
- recirculation pump service
- electrical/hazardous-area inspection
- review sludge/scum accumulation

## Internal cleaning
Do not schedule routine entry.
Only clean internally when performance/inspection justifies shutdown and full confined-space controls are available.

## KPIs
- m³ gas/day
- m³ gas/kg collected dung
- HRT
- pH
- temperature
- feed volume
- digestate volume
- pressure alarms
- gas leaks
- downtime
