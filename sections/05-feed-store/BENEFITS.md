# Feed Store — Benefits

## Feed-quality protection
A dry, ventilated, pest-controlled room reduces losses from rain, humidity, mold and rodents.

## Working-capital control
The 3–7 day inventory strategy avoids tying up cash in months of feed stock.

## Price/usage visibility
Weighing every delivery and daily issue creates accurate feed-cost-per-cow data.

## Labor
Direct flow:
```
truck → receive → rack/pallet → weigh → cow shed
```
minimizes carrying distance.

## Space efficiency
The 360 ft² store holds:
- ~420 kg 7-day concentrate stock
- ~560 kg 7-day dry-roughage stock
- ~90 kg 30-day mineral/premix stock
with dedicated aisle, receiving and preparation space.

## Waste reduction
Quarantine prevents wet/moldy deliveries from contaminating good stock.

## Biosecurity/pest benefit
Feed is separated from veterinary medicine, fertilizer, manure and waste zones.

## Farm-performance value
Good inventory and ration records support milk-cost analysis and future ration optimization by a nutrition professional.
