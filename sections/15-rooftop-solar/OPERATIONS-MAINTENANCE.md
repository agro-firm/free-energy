# Rooftop Solar — Operations and Maintenance

## Daily remote
- power.
- energy.
- inverter alarms.

## Monthly
- compare two MPPTs.
- visual roof inspection from safe point.
- check inverter ventilation.

## Cleaning
Frequency based on soiling.
Use:
- clean water.
- soft brush/squeegee.
- safe fall protection.

## Quarterly
- inspect cable clips.
- connector/rail condition.
- corrosion.
- gutter clearance.

## Annual
- torque/thermal/electrical inspection.
- earth continuity.
- SPD status.
- insulation test.
- roof waterproofing.
- structural fastener inspection.

## KPIs
- kWh/kWp.
- PR/model variance.
- downtime.
- MPPT imbalance.
- annual degradation.
