# Office + Vet + Biosecurity — Utilities and Systems

## Potable water
Supply from Section 13.
Branches:
- biosecurity handwash
- vet sink
- cleaning tap if required

Concept branch: 20–25 mm class, ENGINEER to size.

## Sanitary drainage
This room generates normal handwash/vet-sink wastewater, not manure slurry.

Route:
```
handwash + vet sink + floor drain
→ trapped sanitary/process drain
→ approved wastewater system
```

Do not route medicine spills, disinfectant concentrate, blood/biological waste or sharps into drains.

## Vaccine cold chain
Dedicated circuit to 2–8°C medical refrigerator.
Required:
- internal digital display
- independent temperature logger
- min/max alarm
- power-fail alarm
- critical backup supply
- door kept locked/controlled
- fridge kept out of direct sun
- no food/drinks

## Electrical
Dedicated small subpanel or controlled circuits:
- AC
- vaccine refrigerator
- office sockets
- CCTV/NVR/router
- lights
- printer
- vet equipment
- UPS/backup

Fridge critical circuit should not share a switch that is routinely turned off.

## UPS / backup
Recommended hierarchy:
1. plant solar/grid/biogas backup
2. local UPS/battery bridge for fridge + logger + network
3. alarm on power loss

## CCTV/data
Concept:
- 4-camera package covering public entrance, farm-side door and nearby approach
- NVR in locked office cabinet/rack
- router/network
- remote alert capability if supported

## Ventilation/HVAC
- 1-ton-class inverter AC concept
- 200–250 cfm exhaust/fresh-air provision as needed
- separate ventilation attention for LN₂ storage area

## Liquid nitrogen
No piped gas.
If AI semen tank is used:
- portable cryogenic vessel only
- restrained upright
- ventilated vet zone
- PPE
- manufacturer handling procedure
- oxygen-deficiency assessment if ventilation is limited

## Gas
**No biogas pipe, gas holder, gas-treatment equipment or combustion appliance in Section 04.**

## Fire/safety
- extinguisher as risk assessment requires
- no medicine refrigerator plugged into unreliable extension lead
- no overloaded multi-plug
- separate clean electrical route from wet sinks
