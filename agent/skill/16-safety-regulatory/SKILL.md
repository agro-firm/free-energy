# Safety + Regulatory Skill

## Current Bangladesh fire-regulatory workflow
- Bangladesh National Building Code (BNBC) 2020 remains published by the Ministry of Housing and Public Works.
- Fire Service e-NOC is an online process; current instructions require registration/application and later submission of fire-fighting floor plan, layout plan, land documents and Google map, followed by inspection.
- FSCD e-fire licensing was launched from 01 May 2026 and is being progressively applied across fire-license categories.
- Current e-fire-license application asks for, among other items, access-road width, combustible/flammable materials and fire-prevention/safety arrangements.
- FSCD Fire & Safety Cell offers fire-risk assessment/survey services.

Official current process must be re-checked at application time.

## Hazardous-area rule
Never invent Zone 0/1/2 distances.

For Sections 08–11:
- perform a formal gas-release-source inventory.
- classify hazardous areas using IEC 60079-10-1 or the current authority-accepted equivalent.
- select electrical installation to IEC 60079-14/current equivalent.
- create inspection/maintenance plan to IEC 60079-17/current equivalent.
- final Bangladesh authority/FSCD requirements control.

## Fire-water rule
Section 13's 10 m³ process/domestic storage is **not automatically fire water**.
If the approved fire plan requires a hydrant/hose-reel/fire-water system:
- add a dedicated fire-water demand calculation.
- dedicated usable reserve.
- dedicated fire pump/jockey pump as required.
- ring/main/hydrant arrangement.
- emergency-power philosophy.

## Required outputs before IFC
- fire/hazard register.
- fire-safety plan.
- hazardous-area dossier.
- emergency shutdown matrix.
- gas detector plan.
- evacuation/assembly plan.
- emergency access.
- extinguisher/alarm schedule.
- hot-work permit.
- LOTO.
- confined-space permit/rescue.
- training/drill program.
- FSCD/e-NOC/e-fire-license document register.

## Rule
Concept plans are not approval. Licensed local fire, gas, electrical and civil professionals plus FSCD/other authorities must finalize compliance.
