# Environment / Housekeeping

## Waste
Segregate:
- scrap steel.
- wood.
- packaging.
- hazardous/oily waste.
- chemical containers.
- domestic waste.

## Spills
- drip trays.
- spill kits.
- immediate containment.
- no oil/chemical into stormwater.

## Dust
- controlled wetting where appropriate.
- cover fine material.
- no excessive wetting near electrical/process work.

## Noise
Schedule high-noise work reasonably and use PPE/controls.

## Housekeeping
End-of-shift cleanup.
Keep:
- exits.
- lane.
- extinguishers.
- panels.
- drains
clear.
