# Commercial Evaluation

## Sequence
1. technically compliant shortlist.
2. normalize commercial scope.
3. evaluate landed/installed cost.
4. review payment terms.
5. delivery.
6. warranty/service.
7. financial/commercial risk.
8. approval.

## Suggested evaluation headings
- evaluated BDT cost.
- delivery date.
- quote validity.
- advance requirement.
- warranty.
- liquidated-damage terms if used.
- performance guarantees.
- exclusions.
- after-sales support.
- spare pricing.

## Public procurement reference
Bangladesh's e-GP portal currently publishes Revised e-GP Guidelines 2025 and Electronic Standard Tender Documents.

The project is private and is **not automatically governed by BPPA/PPR rules unless the funding/procuring context makes them applicable**.

However, BPPA structures are useful examples for:
- priced schedules.
- compliance sheets.
- tenderer information.
- contract conditions.
- documented evaluation.
