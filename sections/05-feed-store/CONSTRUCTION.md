# Feed Store — Construction Plan

## Preconstruction
- confirm Section 06 door alignment
- confirm cow-shed dispatch route
- survey flood/rain levels
- design raised dry floor
- confirm rack/pallet loads
- confirm ventilation/dehumidifier drain
- coordinate fire/safety review

## Sequence
1. set out 18×20 ft
2. foundations/subgrade
3. damp-proof membrane and slab
4. raised threshold/plinth
5. structural frame/walls/roof
6. gutters/downpipes
7. screened high-level vents
8. weather-sealed doors
9. electrical/data
10. ventilation/dehumidifier
11. racks/pallets/bins
12. scale/mixing bench
13. pest-control setup
14. fire/safety setup
15. dry commissioning
16. first controlled feed delivery

## Floor
Final engineer to specify:
- slab thickness
- reinforcement
- DPM
- rack point-load capacity
- joints
- raised edge/plinth

Do not use a continuously wet-washdown finish strategy; keep feed storage dry.

## Hold points
- subgrade/DPM before slab
- floor level versus outside
- roof/cladding weatherproofing
- door pest seals
- rack anchoring
- ventilation airflow
- RH logger
- dehumidifier drainage
- electrical panel clearance
- fire equipment placement
- first-delivery inspection

## Commissioning tests
- hose/rain simulation outside doors to confirm no water ingress
- ventilation fan operation
- RH/temperature logger
- dehumidifier
- scale calibration
- door sealing
- rack stability/load labels
- pest trap map
