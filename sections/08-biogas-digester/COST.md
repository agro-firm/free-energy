# Biogas Digester — Cost and Budget Report

## Basis
Bangladesh planning estimate, October 2026. Section 08 only.

This is an engineering estimate, not a vendor quotation.

## Public references
### IDCOL
IDCOL reports:
- biogas program active since 2006
- more than 68,000 plants financed through December 2023
- brick-cement and prefabricated biodigester models
- financed daily gas-production capacities from about 1.2 to 25 m³/day
- commercial biogas electricity financing for dairy/poultry farms

Source:
https://www.idcol.org/idcol_new/public/renewable/biogas-and-bio-fertilizer

### Bangladesh PWD Schedule of Rates
Official civil-work chapters include excavation, RCC, steel and related works:
https://ss.pwd.gov.bd/sor/sordownload/1

Public PWD schedule examples show RCC concrete-only rates roughly in the mid-teen-thousand Tk/m³ range depending grade/region, before various reinforcement/formwork/watertight/process requirements. A gas-tight buried digester therefore needs a much higher installed all-in allowance.

## Base estimate
| Item | Base |
|---|---:|
| Excavation, shoring, dewatering, backfill | Tk 180,000 |
| Gas/water-tight RCC digester structure (~11.1 m³ concept) | Tk 620,000 |
| Compound slab/curbs/drainage | Tk 160,000 |
| Internal gas/water-resistant coating, waterstops, seals | Tk 150,000 |
| Gas-tight roof/manway/penetrations | Tk 180,000 |
| Feed inlet, digestate overflow chamber and piping | Tk 140,000 |
| External recirculation pump/loop/valves | Tk 120,000 |
| Raw-gas outlet, condensate trap and gas valves | Tk 100,000 |
| P/V relief and gas-safety hardware | Tk 100,000 |
| Temperature/level/pressure/pH instrumentation | Tk 110,000 |
| Electrical/control integration | Tk 85,000 |
| Gas detector/safety/PPE/signage | Tk 70,000 |
| Inoculum/startup/process testing allowance | Tk 80,000 |
| Engineering QA/commissioning/training allowance | Tk 50,000 |
| **Subtotal** | **Tk 2,145,000** |

20% contingency:
```
2,145,000×0.20
=Tk 429,000
```

Base total:
```
2,145,000+429,000
=Tk 2,574,000
≈Tk 25.74 lakh
```

## High-cost drivers
- groundwater/uplift
- deep excavation/shoring
- heavy reinforcement
- gas-tight roof complexity
- premium coatings
- hazardous-area electrical
- redundant recirculation
- instrumentation
- difficult soil
- imported gas-safety hardware

## Exclusions
Section 07 pretreatment, Section 09 gas holder, Section 10 gas treatment, Section 11 generator, Section 12 fertilizer equipment, land and statutory/professional fees unless quoted.

## Procurement strategy
Get at least:
- one experienced biogas EPC/vendor design
- independent structural/civil review
- process/hydraulic review
- gas-safety review

Compare:
- total vs working volume
- guaranteed gas tightness
- foundation/uplift design
- P/V protection
- warranty
- biological startup support
- after-sales service
