# Cow Yard — Cost and Budget Report

## Basis
Bangladesh planning estimate, October 2026. Section 02 only.

Public references used:
- Bangladesh PWD Schedule of Rates portal: https://ss.pwd.gov.bd/sor
- LGED Schedule of Rates 2025 public reproductions show Zone-D RCC pavement rates around Tk 15,958/m³ for a 20 MPa crushed-stone pavement item.
- 2026 Bangladesh GI chain-link mesh public supplier guides show mesh roughly Tk 8–45/ft² depending gauge/mesh; installed fencing also needs posts, rails, gates, transport and labor.
- 2026 Bangladesh basic steel shed guides commonly quote about Tk 400–600/ft² for basic industrial shed shells; the shade canopy uses this only as a planning benchmark.

- pavement/subbase thickness

- canopy steel/roof
