# Material Staging and Storage

## Cement/chemicals
- dry.
- raised.
- FIFO.
- labeled.
- SDS for chemicals.

## Rebar
- raised off soil.
- tagged by diameter/heat/batch.
- segregated.

## Structural steel
- dunnage.
- piece marks visible.
- coating protected.

## Electrical
- dry locked storage.
- cable drums supported.
- instruments indoors.

## Process equipment
- vendor storage instructions.
- corrosion/desiccant protection.
- battery maintenance.

## Solar
- upright pallet rules per supplier.
- protected from impact/water.

## Gas membrane/media
- UV/heat/chemical protection.
- no sharp-object contact.
- media SDS storage.

## Traceability
GRN/material tag links material to:
- PO.
- MTC/certificate.
- inspection.
- location.
