# Biogas Digester Skill

## Baseline
- Nominal digester: 25 m³.
- Compound: 16 × 20 ft.
- Daily slurry planning flow: about 0.54 m³/day.

## Sizing
working_volume = daily_slurry_volume × HRT.
At 30 days: 0.54 × 30 = 16.2 m³ working liquid volume.
The 25 m³ concept provides additional headspace/process margin.

## Must define before procurement
- Digester type.
- Working volume versus total vessel volume.
- Pressure/vacuum limits.
- Mixing method.
- Feed and discharge elevations.
- Manway/access.
- Overpressure and underpressure protection.
- Drain/cleanout.
- Gas and digestate outlets.

## Rule
Do not invent vessel wall thickness, foundation reinforcement or pressure ratings. Those are VENDOR/engineer values.
