# Service Lane / Logistics SOP

## Booking
Schedule:
- milk pickup.
- feed delivery.
- fertilizer pickup.
- major maintenance.

## Rule
One truck at a time.
No parking/storage.

## Reversing
Spotter.
Preferred reverse-in/forward-out if external road geometry supports.

## Loading
- chocks.
- parking brake.
- worker clear zone.
- engine off where practical.

## Emergency
Lane clears immediately for emergency access.
