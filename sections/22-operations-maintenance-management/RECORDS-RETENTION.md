# Records Retention

## Permanent / asset life
- as-built.
- equipment manuals.
- commissioning.
- calibration baseline.
- major modifications.
- warranty/serial records.

## Animal lifetime + required period
- identity.
- breeding.
- treatment.
- vaccination.
- withdrawal.

## Operational
Retain according to buyer/regulatory/business needs:
- milk.
- feed.
- water.
- gas.
- energy.
- fertilizer.
- cleaning.
- pest.
- maintenance.

## Financial
Invoices/payroll/taxes/contracts per applicable legal/accounting retention.

## Digital
- controlled permissions.
- backups.
- export.
- audit trail.

## Rule
Final retention periods are set from current Bangladesh legal, veterinary, buyer and tax requirements.
