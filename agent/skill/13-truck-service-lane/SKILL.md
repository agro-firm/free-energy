# Truck / Service Lane Skill

## Canonical baseline
- Width: **14 ft / 4.267 m**.
- Length: **95 ft / 28.956 m**.
- Area: **1,330 ft² / 123.56 m²**.
- One-way-at-a-time operational corridor.
- No passing and no internal U-turn.
- No permanent storage.

## Planning design vehicle
Tata LPT 709-class rigid truck:
- overall width ≈2.155 m.
- overall length ≈6.225–6.875 m.
- GVW ≈7.5 t.
- turning-circle diameter ≈12.3–13.5 m.

Use current Tata Motors Bangladesh data as reference; actual farm supplier truck controls final swept path.

## Lane-width check
Centered truck clearance:
```
14 ft - 7.07 ft = 6.93 ft total
≈3.46 ft per side
```

At loading nodes the truck can shift outward to create about 4 ft worker clearance on the building side while retaining roughly 2.9 ft on the outer side.

## Operating strategy
Preferred:
- reverse into lane under trained spotter
- perform loading/unloading
- exit forward

Alternative:
- forward in
- guided reverse out

Final method depends on public-road entrance geometry.

## Pavement
Concept:
- 150 mm RCC.
- engineered sub-base.
- 1.5% crossfall.
- 7.5-t GVW design vehicle minimum.
- final design based on axle load, repetitions, subgrade/CBR and drainage.

## Must serve
- Milk pickup.
- Feed unloading.
- Fertilizer pickup.
- Generator/digester maintenance.
- Veterinary vehicle.
- Emergency/service access.

## Rule
Do not describe the lane as a fire-service-approved access route until current local Fire Service/building requirements and actual turning/access are verified.
