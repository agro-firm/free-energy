# Organization and Roles

## Farm / Operations Manager — 1
Accountable for:
- animal/product/process performance.
- staffing/roster.
- procurement/inventory approval.
- veterinary coordination.
- KPI/OPEX review.
- buyer/supplier interface.
- compliance/records.
- emergency command until specialist/emergency service arrives.

## Livestock / Milking Operator A — 1
Primary:
- morning herd/milking.
- animal observation.
- trough/feed support.
- milk records.
- sanitation.

Backup:
- Operator B duties.

## Livestock / Milking Operator B — 1
Primary:
- midday/evening herd/milking.
- evening records.
- yard rotation.
- sanitation.

Backup:
- Operator A duties.

## Utility / Process Technician — 1
Primary:
- Sections 07–15 process/equipment.
- water.
- gas.
- generator.
- solar.
- daily PM.
- alarms.
- meter reconciliation.

Must not perform hazardous gas/electrical work beyond competency/authorization.

## Feed / Cleaning / Logistics Worker — 1
- feed receiving.
- feed issue.
- cleaning.
- fertilizer movement.
- delivery support.
- grounds/housekeeping.

## Contract/on-call
- registered veterinarian / AI technician.
- refrigeration technician.
- electrician.
- gas/biogas specialist.
- generator service.
- solar service.
- water-treatment/lab.
- fire/HSE specialist.

## Coverage rule
Every critical task has:
- primary.
- trained backup.
- escalation contact.
