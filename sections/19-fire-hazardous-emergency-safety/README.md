# 19 — Fire, Hazardous-Area, Emergency Access & Regulatory Safety Validation

## Status
**Plant-wide safety engineering package complete for planning / authority submission preparation.**

This section does not declare the project fire-approved. It defines the fire/gas/electrical emergency strategy and the exact evidence required to close IFC Gate 8.

## Current Bangladesh regulatory context
- BNBC 2020 is published by the Ministry of Housing and Public Works.
- FSCD e-NOC is online and current instructions call for fire-fighting floor plans, layout plans, land documents and Google map before inspection/decision.
- FSCD e-fire licensing was launched from **01 May 2026**.
- Current e-fire-license forms ask for access-road width, combustible/flammable materials and fire-prevention arrangements.
- FSCD Fire & Safety Cell currently offers fire-risk assessment/survey service.

## Plant safety conclusion
- The DCF-1 geometry is usable for safety planning.
- Exact hazardous-area Zone 0/1/2 boundaries are **not yet frozen**.
- The **2-ft digester/generator plan gap remains a HIGH safety hold**.
- The 14-ft service lane remains an emergency approach candidate, but not yet a certified fire-appliance route.
- The existing 10 m³ water utility is not counted as fire reserve.
- A site fire alarm/emergency-notification system is recommended and documented.
- Portable extinguishers are planned provisionally; final quantity/type/location follows the approved fire plan.
