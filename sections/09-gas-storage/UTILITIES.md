# Gas Storage — Utilities and Gas Interfaces

## Raw-gas inlet
From Section 08:
- DN40 / 1.5-inch class concept
- upstream primary condensate trap
- low-point secondary condensate pot if required
- manual full-port isolation
- flexible/strain-relieved membrane connector
- pressure-rated only for very-low-pressure biogas service

## Raw-gas outlet
To Section 10:
- DN40 / 1.5-inch concept
- isolation valve
- pressure transmitter
- flexible connector
- downstream treatment/booster package

## Condensate
Use closed condensate pot with:
- level visibility/sensor as needed
- manual/automatic drain to dirty-process collection
- no open drain
- no stormwater connection

## Pressure/vacuum safety
Independent mechanical protection:
- P/V relief device
- safe vent/flare connection
- no software dependency
- no normal valve arrangement that can isolate all safety relief

## Emergency vent/flare
Section 09 provides connection/permissive to the project safe vent/flare system.
Final flare location, flame arrestor requirements and separation distances belong to gas-system/fire engineering.

## Electrical
Minimal normal load:
- sensors
- gas detector
- PLC/I/O
- lighting outside hazardous zone
- optional double-membrane support blower only if alternative technology chosen

No electrical heater or battery inside membrane enclosure.

## Data
Signals:
- holder volume %
- gas pressure
- CH₄/H₂S alarm
- condensate high level
- inlet/outlet valve status
- downstream Section 10 ready
- flare/vent available
- generator demand request

## Earthing/bonding
Bond:
- protective metal frame
- metallic gas pipe
- valve manifold
- lightning system where required

Membrane anti-static/fire properties must be vendor documented.

## Stormwater
Slope slab/ground away from enclosure.
No open drain inside gas cage.

## Ventilation
Natural ventilation continuously available. Do not enclose holder in solid walls.

## Gas treatment
No H₂S scrubber belongs in Section 09 baseline. Treatment begins in Section 10.
