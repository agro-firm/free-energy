# Feed Store — Utilities and Environmental Systems

## 1. Water
This is a **dry store**.

Preferred:
- no internal hose washdown
- no water line above racks
- small handwash at/near receiving threshold if required
- spot-cleaning only
- any handwash drain routed to sanitary/process wastewater, not feed floor

## 2. Internal drainage
Primary storage floor should not depend on floor drains.

Design principle:
- prevent water from entering
- raised threshold/plinth
- exterior drainage outside doors
- dry floor inside

If a local handwash is installed, use a small trapped drain isolated from storage floor.

## 3. Roof stormwater
```
roof → gutters → downpipes → clean stormwater
```

No roof water is allowed to discharge at door thresholds.

## 4. Electrical
Dedicated circuits for:
- ventilation
- lighting
- dehumidifier
- weighing scale/outlets
- optional mixer
- CCTV/data

Keep panel and switches away from roughage stacks.

## 5. Ventilation
Concept:
- screened high-level intake louvers
- high-level exhaust
- approximately 300–500 cfm
- avoid pulling rain/dust directly into feed

## 6. Humidity control
Install:
- RH/temperature logger
- optional 24 L/day class dehumidifier
- drain condensate outside or to controlled drain

Current public 2026 dehumidifier pricing starts around Tk31,500 for a 24 L/day unit and rises with capacity.

## 7. Data/monitoring
Recommended:
- RH/temperature logger
- feed moisture meter
- door contact
- CCTV
- optional stock barcode/QR system
- power meter if dehumidifier/mixer energy is significant

## 8. Gas
**No raw/treated biogas, gas holder, flare or combustion appliance in the feed store.**

## 9. Fire
Dry feed/roughage adds combustible load.
Requirements to be finalized by fire/safety professional:
- extinguisher type/quantity
- emergency egress
- electrical separation
- hot-work restrictions
- no smoking signage

## 10. Dust
No open grain grinding inside the compact store.
If a mixer is installed:
- use enclosed/low-dust equipment
- local dust capture if necessary
- housekeeping to prevent dust accumulation
