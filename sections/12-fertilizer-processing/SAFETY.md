# Fertilizer Processing — Safety Report

## Main hazards
- biological exposure
- screw/press pinch
- rotating machinery
- wet slippery floor
- confined liquid tanks
- ammonia/odor
- heavy bags
- electrical washdown risk
- dust during dry screening/bagging

## Screw press
- guards
- E-stop
- lockout before opening
- no hand/tool into feed screw while energized

## Tanks
BT-601/LT-601 are no-entry under normal operation.
Confined-space procedure required for entry.

## Biological PPE
- gloves
- rubber boots
- eye/face protection for splash
- washable clothing
- handwashing

## Dust
If dry product creates dust:
- respiratory protection as risk assessment requires
- local ventilation
- avoid compressed-air cleaning

## Lifting
25 kg bags:
- proper technique
- trolley/pallet
- avoid repetitive twisting

## Liquid
No uncontrolled hose discharge.
Use spill kit/contained drain.

## Commercial product
Do not expose workers to untested dried material without basic hygiene/dust controls.

## Electrical
IP-rated equipment in wet zone.
Lockout before washdown/maintenance.
