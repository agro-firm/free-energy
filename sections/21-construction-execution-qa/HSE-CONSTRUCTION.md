# Construction HSE

## Current Bangladesh context
DIFE publishes construction-safety inspection guidance and broader machinery/chemical/accident-prevention inspection guidance.

Reference:
https://dife.gov.bd/site/page/de808375-64af-4416-9035-86503d0f2109/

## Site controls
- induction.
- daily toolbox.
- JSA/task risk.
- permit-to-work.
- PPE.
- fall protection.
- electrical temporary-power safety.
- lifting.
- excavation.
- hot work.
- confined space.
- traffic.
- housekeeping.

## Stop-work
Any person may stop unsafe work without penalty under project policy.

## High-risk work
- deep excavation.
- roof work.
- crane/lifts.
- energization.
- gas commissioning.
- confined space.
- hot work near gas systems.

## Incident
Immediate care, isolate hazard, report, investigate, corrective action.
