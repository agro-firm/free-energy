# Livestock Capital

## Plant budget exclusion
Section 20 gross control budget excludes livestock purchase.

## Current public examples
2026 Holstein Friesian listings include roughly Tk220k–300k for several animals.

## Illustrative base
20 × Tk250,000
= Tk5,000,000.

## Animal-by-animal due diligence
Record:
- purchase price.
- milk yield.
- lactation stage.
- pregnancy.
- age.
- health/vet exam.
- breed.
- insurance if used.

## Existing herd
If cows are already owned, new project cash requirement excludes purchase cost, although management accounts may still recognize the livestock asset.
