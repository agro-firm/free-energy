# Feed Logistics Skill

## Baseline
- Feed store: 18 × 20 ft.
- Unloading canopy: 10 × 18 ft.
- Direct service-lane access.

## Small-site rule
Use frequent feed delivery. Do not consume land storing months of fodder.

## Capacity formula
storage_mass = daily_use × storage_days × reserve_factor.
storage_volume = storage_mass ÷ actual bulk density.

## Must include
Dry floor, pest control, FIFO inventory, feed weighing/mixing point, bag/bin storage and shortest route to cow-shed feed alley.
