# 10 — Gas Treatment

## Status
Planning baseline. Detailed construction design will be added later.

## Baseline
8 × 10 ft zone

## Purpose
Conditions stored biogas so it is suitable for the generator.

## Inputs
Raw biogas from gas storage.

## Outputs
Treated, pressure-regulated biogas to the generator.

## Interfaces
Gas storage, generator/electrical and control/safety system.

## Current design notes
Concept functions include H2S reduction, moisture separation, metering, pressure regulation and automatic/manual isolation.
