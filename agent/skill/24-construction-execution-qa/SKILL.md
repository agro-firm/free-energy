# Construction Execution + QA Skill

## Baseline schedule
- Start point: approved IFC/NTP for applicable workfront.
- Base: 40 weeks.
- Fast-track: 36 weeks only with early procurement and closed holds.
- Risk case: 48+ weeks where monsoon, approvals or imports delay work.

## Compact-site rule
- Permanent plot is 65×95 ft.
- Only ~845 ft² remains outside permanent ground footprints and lane.
- Site camp/large laydown should be outside/front-adjacent where possible.
- Section 14 lane must remain available.
- Planning peak workforce: ~30–35 onsite.
- Higher peak requires explicit congestion/egress/HSE review.

## QA
Use:
- approved method statement.
- approved material.
- approved shop drawing.
- ITP.
- inspection request.
- test records.
- NCR/CAR.
- as-built capture.

## HSE
Use Section 19 permits and DIFE construction-safety guidance.
No work starts without task risk/JSA and required permit.

## Progress
Report:
- planned vs actual.
- critical path.
- manpower.
- plant.
- quantities.
- inspections.
- RFIs/NCRs.
- procurement constraints.
- safety.
- forecast.

## Handover
Complete:
- punch list.
- as-builts.
- O&M.
- warranties.
- training.
- spare parts.
- SAT/commissioning.
- integrated trial.
