# Cow Yard — Operations and Maintenance

## Daily
- inspect gate/latch
- remove manure solids
- clean trough
- inspect drain grate
- check slippery areas
- inspect fence damage

## Weekly
- flush dirty drain
- inspect shade roof/gutter
- test hose and isolation valve
- inspect light/CCTV
- inspect post movement

## Monthly
- tighten gate/fence hardware
- inspect corrosion
- inspect canopy connections
- review drainage performance
- check concrete cracking/spalling

## Monsoon
- clean gutter/downpipe frequently
- keep dirty drain clear
- verify heavy-rain bypass/wastewater plan
- stop yard use if flooding creates hoof/slip risk

## Hot season
- inspect shade coverage
- confirm water flow
- schedule shorter sessions in extreme heat

## Annual
- structural canopy inspection
- repaint/repair corrosion
- repair pavement joints/cracks
- check gate alignment
- review cow-rotation density

## KPI
- cows/session
- session duration
- water use
- cleaning water
- drain blockages
- slip/injury events
- gate faults
