# Shift Handover

## Mandatory handover
- cow count.
- sick/injured/treated animals.
- milk volume/temp/quality exception.
- feed/water exceptions.
- alarms.
- equipment out of service.
- gas/digester status.
- generator/solar status.
- deliveries/visitors.
- open work orders.
- medicine withdrawal animals.
- safety permits.

## Handover rule
Critical verbal handover is also written/digital.

## Unresolved issue
Each open issue has:
- owner.
- priority.
- due time.
- escalation.
