# Cow Yard — Benefits

## Movement
Provides a controlled place for cows to move outside the stall area without consuming pasture-scale land.

## Compact-land benefit
The rotational 10-cow model lets a 504 ft² yard support a 20-cow herd in two groups.

## Heat relief
Half of the active zone is conceptually shaded:
```
216 / 432 = 50%
```

## Hygiene
Paved floor and controlled dirty drainage make manure easier to collect than an unpaved mud yard.

## Water control
Separate canopy stormwater avoids unnecessary dilution of the manure/digester system.

## Labor
Direct shed-to-yard gate reduces walking/handling distance.

## Health/observation
Workers can observe gait, hoof condition and general mobility while cows are outside stalls.

## Business integration
The yard is not a direct revenue center; it supports animal management and manure capture and can reduce uncontrolled mud/manure problems.
