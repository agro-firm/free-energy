# Fertilizer Processing — Construction and Commissioning

## Preconstruction
- confirm Section 08 outlet elevation
- select separator
- confirm 500 kg/h machine footprint/power
- confirm service-lane loading side
- confirm liquid-use/transport plan
- design dirty drainage
- check regulatory/product plan

## Sequence
1. set out 16×22 ft
2. foundations/slab
3. curbs/process drains
4. roof/steel/open sides
5. buffer tank
6. screw press foundation
7. liquid tank foundation
8. digestate/liquid piping
9. curing bays
10. bagging area/pallet zone
11. electrical/fans/control
12. leak/drain test
13. dry run
14. water test
15. live digestate test
16. solids/liquid sample
17. tune press
18. commissioning

## Hold points
- drain slope
- clean/dirty drainage separation
- screw-press anchor/service clearance
- tank vent/overflow
- electrical washdown rating
- curing-bay leachate
- service-lane access
- safety guards

## Commissioning
Measure actual:
- digestate TS
- feed rate
- separator capture
- cake moisture
- liquid volume
- motor current
- bagged yield

Then update CALCULATIONS.md with measured values.
