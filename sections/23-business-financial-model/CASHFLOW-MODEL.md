# Cash Flow Model

## Operating cash proxy
EBITDA =
cash revenue
+ realized avoided cost
- operating expense.

## Unlevered free cash flow
FCF =
EBITDA
- cash taxes
- sustaining CAPEX
- working-capital increase.

## Levered cash flow
Cash to Equity =
FCF
- debt principal
- interest
+ new debt.

## Year 0
Normally includes:
- plant CAPEX.
- livestock.
- initial working capital.
- finance fees.
- owner/project-development costs.

## Year 1+
Use actual ramp/production assumptions.

## Terminal value
Only include explicitly justified:
- asset residual.
- livestock value.
- land value.
- working-capital release.

## Warning
EBITDA is not owner cash return.
