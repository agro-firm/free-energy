# Construction + Review Skill

## Construction sequence
1. Survey and soil/site verification.
2. Approved layout and drainage levels.
3. Foundations, civil utilities and underground sleeves.
4. Truck/service lane.
5. Cow shed and roof.
6. Water and drainage.
7. Manure gutters, scraper and sump.
8. Digester and digestate system.
9. Gas holder, treatment and flare/vent protection.
10. Generator and electrical.
11. Solar.
12. Milk room and chiller.
13. Feed and fertilizer fit-out.
14. Controls and instrumentation.
15. Dry commissioning.
16. Water commissioning.
17. Digester biological startup.
18. Gas-quality verification.
19. Generator load test.
20. Integrated operating trial.

## Mandatory change-control checks
- Area balance.
- No unintended geometric overlap.
- Truck/service access.
- Clean/dirty route separation.
- Manure mass balance.
- Digester hydraulic load.
- Gas production and storage balance.
- Generator fuel/runtime balance.
- Solar/electrical impact.
- Water demand/storage.
- Milk cold-chain access.
- Fertilizer handling/dispatch.
- Safety/regulatory impact.
- Cost impact.
- Image geometry consistency.

## Version law
Every approved major plan receives a version such as PLANT-V1.0. Images and calculations must state the plan version.

## Acceptance rule
No major design change becomes canonical until math, layout, process, safety and review checks pass.
