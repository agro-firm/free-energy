# Master Utilities

## Water
Source → Section 13 → ground tank → duty/standby pumps → pressure header → users + overhead tank.

Planning:
- normal demand ~3.30 m³/day.
- design demand ~3.79 m³/day.
- hot-weather design ~4.25 m³/day.
- total storage 10 m³.

Priority:
1. cow drinking.
2. milk hygiene.
3. handwash/vet.
4. digester minimum.
5. process.
6. washdown.

## Electrical
Normal:
Grid/utility + Section 15 solar → main plant electrical system.

Backup:
Section 11 biogas generator → ATS → Essential Load Board.

Baseline rule:
on-grid solar does not energize an isolated generator island.

## Gas
Section 08 → 09 → 10 → 11.

Required:
- condensate management.
- pressure/vacuum protection.
- H₂S treatment.
- moisture removal.
- gas metering.
- regulation.
- automatic isolation.

## Drainage
Clean:
- roofs.
- clean lane stormwater.
- clean tank overflow.

Dirty/process:
- cow/manure.
- digestate/leachate.
- gas condensate.
- milk/process wastewater as separately approved.

Never combine dirty biological/process flow into clean storm drainage.

## Data / controls
Central monitoring should collect:
- milk L.
- water m³.
- slurry m³.
- gas m³.
- H₂S.
- holder %.
- generator kWh.
- solar kWh.
- fertilizer kg.
- alarms.
- maintenance state.

## Authority
Detailed pipe sizes, pump duties, cable sizes, protection, detector locations and final routes remain in the applicable Section 01–15 files and approved vendor/IFC documents.
