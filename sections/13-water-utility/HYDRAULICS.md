# Water Utility — Hydraulics

## Design flow
Main pressure manifold:
```
40–50 L/min
```

This covers:
- trough refill
- wash hose
- digester water dose
- milk-room cleaning when sequenced

## Pump selection
Baseline:
- 2×1 HP
- duty/standby
- target duty point ~40–50 L/min at 20–25 m TDH

Final selection requires:
- source/tank suction level
- longest pipe run
- elevation
- fittings
- simultaneous demand
- actual pump curve

## Pipe hierarchy
- 32 mm main
- 25 mm high-demand branches
- 20 mm local branches
- 15 mm fixture branches where suitable

## Pressure
Gravity tank at 12–15 ft platform provides only modest static pressure.
Therefore boosted pressure is used for wash/process loads.

## Backflow
Use:
- non-return valves
- air gaps
- vacuum breakers/backflow devices where contaminated hoses can contact manure/process liquids

## Water hammer
Slow-closing valves and pump controls should limit pressure shock.
