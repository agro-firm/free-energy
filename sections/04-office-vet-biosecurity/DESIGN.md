# Office + Vet + Biosecurity — Functional Interior Design

## 1. Biosecurity zone
Functions:
- separates external shoes/clothes from clean farm circulation
- holds visitor PPE
- hand hygiene
- visitor/contractor log
- emergency farm rules display

Recommended:
- divided dirty/clean shoe rack
- seated change bench
- color-coded boots
- disposable visitor coveralls where needed
- handwash with soap/paper towel
- closed PPE storage
- small exterior footbath/mat under cover

## 2. Office/monitoring zone
Designed for 1–2 people working normally, with short-term visitor seating.

Include:
- 1200–1500 mm work desk
- 2 work chairs
- wall monitor for CCTV/dashboard
- computer
- printer/scanner
- NVR/router
- lockable paper records
- whiteboard/task board
- charging station

Avoid oversized executive furniture.

## 3. Vet/AI workspace
This is a preparation/records/workbench zone, not a surgery room.

Include:
- durable workbench
- SS or chemical-resistant sink
- lockable instrument drawer
- disposable gloves/PPE
- sharps container
- medical-waste container
- sample tubes/basic diagnostic supplies
- artificial-insemination supplies
- restrained LN₂ semen tank if used

No animal carcass handling or major invasive procedures should be performed here.

## 4. Medicine/vaccine store
Include:
- dedicated 2–8°C refrigerator
- independent temperature logger
- high/low temperature alarm
- lockable medication cabinet
- labeled shelves
- expiry/FEFO system
- no food/drinks
- no pesticides, herbicides or cleaning chemicals stored with veterinary medicines

## 5. Vaccine refrigerator
Current Bangladesh market examples show 2–8°C pharmaceutical refrigerators around:
- Sunny Med 218 L: ~Tk 100,000
- premium Scitek 226 L: ~Tk 265,000

The project should select capacity based on actual vaccine inventory, not room size.

## 6. Lighting
Concept:
- 4 × 18 W LED ceiling fixtures
- task light over vet bench
- exterior entry light
- high CRI where medicine labels/animal samples are read

## 7. HVAC
One small inverter AC may serve the room if envelope/load calculations confirm.
Wet/biosecurity area should still receive fresh-air/exhaust provision.

## 8. Furniture and storage
Storage priority:
1. medicines/vaccines
2. veterinary/AI instruments
3. PPE
4. records
5. IT/CCTV

No floor clutter.

## 9. Safety
- sharps never go into normal waste
- chemicals separately labeled
- emergency contacts visible
- first-aid kit
- extinguisher as safety review requires
- LN₂ PPE
- medicine fridge on critical backup power
