# First 90 Days of Operations

## Day 0
Integrated handover accepted.
Baseline instruments calibrated.

## Days 1–7 — stabilize
Daily manager review:
- cow behavior/milk.
- feed/water.
- slurry.
- digester/gas.
- generator.
- solar.
- fertilizer.
- alarms.

Do not optimize aggressively.

## Days 8–30 — establish baseline
Calculate:
- average milk.
- feed/L milk.
- water/cow.
- dung collection.
- gas/kg dung.
- generator kWh/m³.
- solar kWh/kWp.
- fertilizer capture.
- alarm frequency.

## Days 31–60 — tune
- adjust PM.
- refine reorder levels.
- correct nuisance alarms.
- optimize scheduled loads.
- retrain weak competencies.

No process tuning outside vendor/vet/safety limits.

## Days 61–90 — management baseline
Freeze first operating budget and real KPI targets from measured data.

Compare:
```
Design assumption vs 90-day measured
```

Update Sections 01–22 where canonical assumptions materially changed.
