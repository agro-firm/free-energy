# Contracting Strategy

## Private-project baseline
Use competitive private RFQs with transparent evaluation.

The project is not automatically a Bangladesh public procurement entity.

## Good practice
BPPA e-GP currently publishes Revised Guidelines 2025 and eSTDs.

Use similar discipline:
- scope.
- priced schedule.
- technical compliance.
- qualification.
- contract conditions.
- documented award recommendation.

## Contract forms
### Supply only
For standardized equipment with separate installer.

### Supply + install
Preferred where warranty depends on installation.

### Specialist EPC
For:
- digester.
- gas system.
- integrated generator package.
if interfaces/warranty favor one specialist.

### Civil subcontract
For measured civil works with BOQ.

## Avoid
One vague "turnkey" contract with no interface/schedule/BOQ transparency.
