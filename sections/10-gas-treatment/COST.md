## Media OPEX sensitivity
If industrial H₂S media costs roughly Tk700–1,000/kg as a procurement allowance:

One 25 kg charge:
```
Tk17,500–25,000
```

If one lead bed lasts roughly 1–2 months under actual H₂S conditions, annual media OPEX can become material.

This is why commissioning H₂S measurement is essential.
