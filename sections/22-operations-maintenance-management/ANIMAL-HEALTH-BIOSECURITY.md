# Animal Health and Biosecurity

## Current Bangladesh veterinary baseline
DLS currently publishes:
- Standard Treatment Guidelines for Large Animal (2026).
- Veterinary Antimicrobial Stewardship Guideline (2026).
- disease-specific materials including LSD, mastitis, FMD and deworming.
- livestock biosecurity policy/guidance emphasizing disease prevention, feed management, visitor control, pest management, cleaning/disinfection and herd-health monitoring.

## Biosecurity
- visitor log.
- controlled entry.
- clean footwear/PPE.
- handwash.
- separate clean/dirty tools.
- pest/vector control.
- quarantine/new-animal procedure.
- vehicle control.
- manure removal.

## Disease suspicion
- isolate/restrict movement as veterinarian directs.
- increase observation.
- notify veterinarian/DLS as applicable.
- clean/disinfect.
- control vectors.
- maintain records.

## Vaccination/deworming
Do **not** hard-code a generic calendar.

Veterinarian establishes herd plan from:
- current DLS program.
- disease prevalence.
- vaccine label.
- animal age/pregnancy.
- previous history.

## Veterinary review
At minimum periodic herd-health review plus on-call response.
