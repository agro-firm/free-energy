# Feed Store — Functional and Interior Design

## 1. Storage philosophy
This room is for **short-duration dry inventory**.

It is not:
- a silage bunker
- a wet green-fodder store
- a feed mill
- a chemical warehouse
- a fertilizer store

## 2. Concentrate/rack zone
Store:
- commercial dairy feed bags
- bran/maize/other approved dry ingredients
- sealed protein/mineral ingredients

Rules:
- never place bags directly on floor
- use pallets/racks
- label supplier, lot, received date and expiry/best-before
- FIFO for non-expiring bulk stock
- FEFO for premixes/additives with expiry
- isolate torn/wet bags

## 3. Dry roughage zone
Store:
- dry hay/straw/baled roughage only
- keep dry and ventilated
- inspect for mold/heat/moisture
- never stack against electrical panel
- leave inspection space around walls

Planning dry-roughage stock:
```
80 kg/day × 7 days = 560 kg
```

## 4. Receiving/quarantine
Every delivery stops here before entering storage.

Check:
- bag/bale condition
- water damage
- mold/odor
- insect/rodent damage
- supplier/lot
- quantity/weight
- receipt price
- moisture if relevant

Rejected material leaves the feed chain.

## 5. Weighing/mixing
The base design uses:
- 300 kg digital platform scale
- small ingredient bins
- manual/low-dust ration-prep bench

Optional:
- closed 100 kg batch mixer
- local dust collection

Do **not** install high-dust grain grinding/milling in this compact room without a separate dust/fire engineering design.

## 6. Mineral/premix
- locked or controlled access
- sealed labeled containers
- keep away from moisture
- no pesticides or veterinary medicines
- no cleaning chemicals on same shelf

## 7. Fresh forage
Planning:
```
15 kg/cow/day × 20 = 300 kg/day as-fed
```

Fresh green forage should normally move:
```
delivery → Section 06 canopy/staging → feed preparation → cows
```

Do not fill the dry store with wet forage.

## 8. Environmental monitoring
Recommended:
- temperature/RH logger
- handheld feed moisture meter
- optional dehumidifier
- visual mold inspection
- pest log

## 9. Lighting
Use sealed LED fixtures with clear visibility into racks and corners.

## 10. Interior cleanliness
Preferred method:
- sweep/vacuum dry material
- remove spilled feed immediately
- avoid wet washdown except controlled spot cleaning
- keep floor dry before restocking

## 11. Ergonomics
- heaviest bags on lower levels
- no unstable high manual stacks
- clear 4 ft central aisle
- hand trolley/pallet trolley for bag movement
- platform scale at waist-accessible workflow position
