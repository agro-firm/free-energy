# Long-Lead Register

No fixed lead time is invented before quotes.

## LONG-LEAD-A
Obtain early firm lead times for:
- milk chiller.
- pharma/vaccine refrigerator if imported.
- digester specialist components.
- gas holder.
- H₂S analyzer/gas meter.
- biogas generator.
- screw press.
- solar inverter/modules.
- hazardous-area/fire equipment if imported.

## LONG-LEAD-B
Medium:
- pumps.
- flow meters.
- PLC/VFD.
- ATS.
- tanks.
- fans.

## Local fabrication
Normally easier to schedule:
- steel buildings.
- racks.
- gates.
- curing bays.
- piping supports.
but raw material availability must still be confirmed.

## Register fields
- vendor.
- PO date.
- drawing approval.
- manufacturing.
- FAT.
- ship.
- customs.
- site arrival.
- installation need date.
- float.
