# Manpower Plan

## Planning workforce
Compact-site soft cap:
```
~30–35 persons onsite at one time
```

Above this:
- congestion review.
- welfare check.
- emergency egress review.
- supervisor/HSE increase.

## Indicative phase headcount
| Weeks | Planning total |
|---|---:|
| 1–4 | 10–15 |
| 5–14 | 25–35 |
| 15–24 | 30–35 |
| 25–32 | 20–28 |
| 33–40 | 10–18 |

## Core staff
- Project/site manager: 1.
- Civil/site engineer: 1.
- MEP/process engineer: 1.
- QA/QC: 1.
- HSE: 1.
- Surveyor: part-time/full at critical stages.
- Store/document control: 1 shared.
- Foremen: 2–3 depending workfronts.

## Trades
Variable:
- civil/rebar/formwork.
- steel erection/roof.
- plumbing/pipefitting.
- electrical/instrument.
- vendor technicians.
- helpers.

## Rule
Trade count follows safe simultaneous workfront capacity, not schedule pressure alone.
