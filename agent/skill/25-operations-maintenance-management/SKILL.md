# Operations + Maintenance Management Skill

## Core operating baseline
- Herd: 20 adult cows.
- Milk planning: ~140 L/day.
- Concentrate: ~60 kg/day.
- Dry roughage: ~80 kg/day.
- Fresh forage: ~300 kg/day as-fed.
- Normal water: ~3.30 m³/day.
- Collected dung: ~270 kg/day.
- Slurry: ~0.54 m³/day.
- Biogas: ~8.10–9.18 m³/day.
- Biogas electricity: ~14.5–16.5 kWh/day gross.
- Solar: ~58.35 kWh/day planning.
- Fertilizer base product: ~38.7 kg/day at 70% capture scenario.

These are planning baselines, not guaranteed performance targets.

## Core staffing concept
- Farm/operations manager: 1.
- Livestock/milking operators: 2 staggered.
- Utility/process technician: 1.
- Feed/cleaning/logistics worker: 1.
- Night security/monitoring/relief: contracted or rostered as needed.
- Veterinarian/AI, electrician, refrigeration, gas, generator and solar specialists: on-call/service-contract.

## Daily process rhythm
- two milkings/day.
- four slurry feed batches/day around 6-hour intervals.
- generator starts by gas/storage/essential-load logic, not fixed clock time.
- fertilizer separator normally one daily run.
- solar monitored daily, cleaned by condition.
- critical alarms monitored continuously.

## Maintenance rule
Vendor manuals override generic PM intervals.

## Animal-health rule
Vaccination, treatment, antimicrobial and withdrawal decisions require the current DLS/veterinary program. No medicine is given from a generic AI calendar.

## Management rule
Replace planning assumptions with measured 7-day, 30-day and 90-day baselines after commissioning.
