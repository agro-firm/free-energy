# Stormwater Outfall Decision

## Candidate choices
1. front roadside drain.
2. rear/side approved drain.
3. onsite detention/soakage plus controlled discharge.
4. pumped discharge only if gravity is proven infeasible and legally acceptable.

## Decision inputs
- field invert.
- receiving-water normal/high level.
- road drain capacity.
- backflow risk.
- legal permission.
- flood level.
- maintenance access.

## Preferred hierarchy
Gravity > detention/controlled gravity > pumping.

## Backflow
Where receiving drain can surcharge:
- raise critical floors.
- check HGL.
- use appropriate backflow/flap device if suitable.
- do not rely on a valve as the sole flood strategy.

## IFC
Outfall ID, invert RL, size and authority/owner must appear on IFC site plan.
