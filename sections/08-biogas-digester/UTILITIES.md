# Biogas Digester — Utilities and Process Interfaces

## Slurry feed
From Section 07:
- ~0.54 m³/day
- four ~0.135 m³ batches
- 75 mm / 3-inch-class feed concept
- submerged inlet
- isolation/check/cleanout arrangement

## Digestate outlet
To Section 12:
- ~0.54 m³/day at steady state
- 100 mm / 4-inch-class gravity overflow concept
- sealed outlet chamber
- sample/inspection point
- downstream blockage alarm/inspection strategy

## Raw gas
To Section 09:
- ~8.1–9.18 m³/day
- DN40 / 1.5-inch-class low-pressure concept
- gas-tight isolation valve where allowed
- primary condensate trap
- pressure indicator/transmitter
- P/V relief independent of normal gas transfer
- piping continuously sloped where possible to manage condensate

## Condensate
Biogas condensate is corrosive/dirty.
Route from trap to controlled dirty process drain/collection, not clean stormwater.

## Recirculation
External slurry loop:
- suction lower vessel
- solids-duty pump
- isolation valves
- cleanout
- return below liquid level
- sample point

## Electrical
Base loads are small:
- recirculation pump, if used
- instruments
- gas detector
- control/solenoids
- lighting
- optional small heat-trace/actuator loads

All electrical equipment near gas outlets must follow the final hazardous-area classification.

## Data
Recommended:
- TI-201 slurry temperature
- LI/LIT-201 liquid level
- PI/PIT-201 gas pressure
- optional pH transmitter or routine manual pH
- gas-flow total from downstream gas system
- recirculation pump current/runtime
- outlet/blockage status

## Stormwater
Clean compound rain:
- graded away from manways/pits
- never into digester
- roof/cover runoff to clean site drainage

## Dirty washdown
Any manure/digestate wash:
- contained
- returned to process or dirty wastewater handling
- never to clean stormwater

## Gas storage
Section 08 does not replace Section 09. The 5 m³ headspace is not treated as available operational gas-storage capacity.
