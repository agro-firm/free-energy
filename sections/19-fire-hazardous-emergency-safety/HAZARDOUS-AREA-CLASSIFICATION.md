# Hazardous-Area Classification Framework

## Status
**CLASSIFICATION PENDING PROFESSIONAL STUDY.**

No Zone 0 / Zone 1 / Zone 2 dimensions are frozen here.

## Design basis
Use IEC 60079-10-1 or current authority-accepted equivalent for gas/vapour hazardous-area classification.

Installation and equipment:
- IEC 60079-14/current equivalent.
Inspection/maintenance:
- IEC 60079-17/current equivalent.

Final Bangladesh/FSCD/electrical authority requirements control.

## Required study inputs
For every release source:
- gas composition.
- pressure.
- line diameter.
- normal/abnormal release.
- vent/relief discharge.
- ventilation.
- enclosure/open-air geometry.
- frequency/duration.
- gas density.
- ignition sources.

## Candidate classified locations
- digester gas outlet/manway/relief.
- Section 09 membrane holder connectors/valves/PV relief.
- Section 10 scrubber valves/sample points/condensate drains/booster.
- Section 11 final gas train/solenoid/flexible connection.
- flare/safe vent.

## Outputs
Hazardous-area dossier must show:
- source-of-release tag.
- zone classification.
- horizontal/vertical extent.
- ventilation basis.
- equipment protection level/category.
- temperature class.
- gas group.
- drawing.
- inspection schedule.

## DCF implication
If classified extent conflicts with Section 11, public access, ignition source or boundary:
- relocate equipment.
- re-route gas.
- add engineered ventilation/barrier only if accepted.
- update DCF through change control.
