# Construction + Review Skill

## Current authority
- Section 21 controls construction execution, schedule, logistics and QA/QC.
- Section 20 controls procurement/commercial.
- Section 19 controls construction/fire/hazard permits and emergency rules.
- Section 18 controls survey/levels/drainage/IFC.
- Section 17 controls DCF-1 X/Y.

## Baseline execution
Indicative baseline after IFC/NTP:
- ~40 weeks.
- fast-track planning case ~36 weeks.
- weather/approval-constrained case ~48+ weeks.

This is a planning program, not a contractor promise.

## Critical path
Digester civil → cure/leak test → biological startup → gas quality → generator load test → integrated trial → handover.

## Construction QA
No work is covered/closed until its ITP inspection/hold point is released.

## Mandatory change-control checks
- DCF geometry.
- drainage/elevations.
- safety/hazard zones.
- truck/service access.
- vendor fit.
- process mass/energy balance.
- cost/procurement package.
- as-built/document impact.

## Acceptance rule
No major change becomes canonical until design, safety, procurement and construction controls are updated.
