# Biogas Digester — Safety and Hazard Report

## Main hazards
- methane flammability
- H₂S toxicity
- oxygen deficiency
- pressure/vacuum
- confined space
- biological exposure
- buried structure/fall hazard
- excavation/groundwater
- digestate splash
- electrical ignition near gas

## Confined space
The digester is **no-entry during normal operation**.

Manway entry requires:
- complete shutdown
- feed isolation
- gas isolation
- draining/cleaning
- atmospheric testing
- forced ventilation
- lockout/tagout
- permit
- attendant
- rescue plan
- trained personnel

## Pressure/vacuum
Provide engineered P/V protection.
Never:
- cap a relief line
- place a normal closed valve between vessel and required relief
- pull gas faster than vacuum protection allows
- pressure-test with unsafe gas

## Methane
- no flame/smoking
- appropriate electrical equipment near gas outlet
- leak testing
- gas detector after hazard review
- controlled ventilation

## H₂S
Can be toxic at low concentrations.
- gas detection
- avoid breathing vent gas
- relief/vent terminates safely
- maintenance PPE and gas testing

## Excavation/structure
Construction must address:
- shoring/safe excavation
- groundwater
- uplift
- soil stability
- fall protection around open excavation

## Biological hygiene
Use gloves, boots, splash protection.
Wash after contact.

## Gas startup
Initial gas may contain air/oxygen.
Do not route startup gas blindly to storage/engine. Follow commissioning purge procedure.

## Lightning/bonding
Gas piping and metallic equipment require bonding/earthing/lightning review.

## Emergency
Emergency plan must define:
- gas leak
- high pressure
- H₂S alarm
- fire
- person exposed to gas
- spill/overflow
- structural leak
