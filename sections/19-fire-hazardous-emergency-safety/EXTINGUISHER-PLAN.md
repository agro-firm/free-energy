# Portable Extinguisher Plan

## Status
Provisional planning only. Final type/count/location follows the approved Fire Safety Plan and hazard review.

## Planning locations
### FP-01 Office/front
- multipurpose dry chemical near exit.

### FP-02 Feed store
- multipurpose dry chemical at accessible exit side(s).
Feed/hay is combustible Class-A-type fuel.

### FP-03 Milk/electrical
- multipurpose dry chemical.
- CO₂ near electrical/control equipment if approved.

### FP-04 Cow shed
- multipurpose dry chemical near safe exit/service point, protected from animals/washdown.

### FP-05 Waste/digester approach
- dry chemical positioned outside the gas-release hazard envelope.

### FP-06 Gas holder/treatment
- dry chemical outside classified area, accessible without approaching the leak source.

### FP-07 Generator
- dry chemical for gas/general hazard.
- CO₂ for electrical equipment where suitable.

### FP-08 Fertilizer
- multipurpose dry chemical near exit.

## Gas-fire rule
If biogas is burning:
- alarm/evacuate.
- isolate gas supply if it can be done safely.
- do not simply extinguish a gas flame while uncontrolled gas continues escaping.
