# Construction Phasing

- Section 18 survey levels.
- Section 19 safety holds affecting workfront.
- vendor footprint/foundation data.
- RFQ/PO readiness.

- benchmarks.
- temporary drainage.
- underground utilities.
- tanks/sumps/digester excavation.
