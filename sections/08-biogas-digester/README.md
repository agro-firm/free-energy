# 08 — Biogas Digester

## Status
Planning baseline. Detailed construction design will be added later.

## Baseline
25 m³ nominal digester in a 16 × 20 ft compound

## Purpose
Anaerobically converts manure slurry into biogas and digestate.

## Inputs
Metered slurry from the waste receiving/mixing section.

## Outputs
Raw biogas to gas storage and digestate to fertilizer processing.

## Interfaces
Waste receiving/mixing, gas storage, fertilizer/digestate handling and controls.

## Current design notes
Current planning flow is about 0.54 m³ slurry/day with a 30-day HRT basis. Final vessel type, pressure limits, height, foundation and internals are vendor/engineer items.
