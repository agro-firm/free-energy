# Financial Assumptions

All numbers are illustrative planning inputs.

## Physical baseline
- 20 adult cows.
- Milk engineering baseline ~140 L/day.
- Gross renewable generation ~26.6–27.3 MWh/year.
- Fertilizer base ~14.1 t/year finished solid product.

## Base annual operating assumptions
- Milk: 140 L/day.
- Saleable fraction: 97%.
- Milk price: Tk53/L.
- Concentrate: 60 kg/day at Tk72.5/kg.
- Dry roughage: 80 kg/day at Tk20/kg.
- Fresh forage/silage: 300 kg/day at Tk8.5/kg.
- Labor: Tk1.50m/year planning input.
- Veterinary/breeding: Tk0.20m/year.
- Maintenance/spares: Tk0.35m/year.
- Other operating cost: Tk0.40m/year.
