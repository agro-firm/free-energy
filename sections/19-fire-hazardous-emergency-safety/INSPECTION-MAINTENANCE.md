# Inspection and Maintenance

## Daily/shift
- emergency lane clear.
- exits clear.
- gas detector status.
- no smoking/hot work.
- obvious leaks.

## Monthly
- extinguisher visual checks.
- emergency lights.
- exit signs.
- alarm-panel faults.
- call points/horns test by procedure.
- E-stop accessible.
- gas detector bump-test schedule review.

## Quarterly
- fire alarm functional inspection.
- emergency drill/action review.
- LOTO/hot-work audits.
- spill/first-aid stock.

## Annual / vendor interval
- extinguisher service/refill.
- detector calibration.
- alarm battery test/replacement.
- hazardous-area inspection.
- earthing/lightning review.
- fire-plan review.
- Fire Service/license renewals/reassessment as applicable.

## Change management
Reassess after:
- new gas equipment.
- building move.
- increased herd/process size.
- new chemical/fuel.
- electrical upgrade.
- fire incident/near miss.
