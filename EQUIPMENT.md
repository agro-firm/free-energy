## Common equipment requirements
Every major item needs:
- equipment tag.
- approved vendor/model.
- footprint and service clearance.
- utilities.
- operating weight.
- foundation/load data.
- isolation.
- controls/I/O.
- safety requirements.
- warranty.
- spares.
- O&M manual.
- FAT/SAT where applicable.

## Procurement authority
Use Section 20 for final package ownership, quote normalization, vendor submittals and deduplication.

## Image authority
An image may show generic equipment form only when the exact vendor model is not yet approved.
