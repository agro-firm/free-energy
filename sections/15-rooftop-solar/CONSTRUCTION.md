# Rooftop Solar — Construction and Commissioning

## Preconstruction
- roof structural survey.
- exact azimuth/shading survey.
- module/inverter approved-list check.
- net-metering/utility check.
- electrical single-line approval.
- fall-protection plan.

## Sequence
1. inspect/repair roof.
2. mark structural purlins.
3. install anchors/rails.
4. water-seal penetrations.
5. install modules.
6. bond frames/rails.
7. string wiring.
8. DC isolation/SPD.
9. inverter.
10. ACDB/feed.
11. monitoring.
12. insulation/polarity/Voc tests.
13. inverter commissioning.
14. utility/net-metering process.
15. performance test.

## Hold points
- structural signoff.
- rail torque.
- waterproofing.
- string polarity.
- earth continuity.
- insulation resistance.
- SPD.
- AC protection.
- anti-islanding.
