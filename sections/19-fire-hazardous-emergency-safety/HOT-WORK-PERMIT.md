# Hot Work Permit

## Hot work includes
- welding.
- cutting.
- grinding producing sparks.
- torch work.
- heat gun/open-flame process where ignition risk exists.

## Prohibited without permit
Sections 08–11 gas/process area and any area affected by a gas hazardous zone.

## Permit prerequisites
- work scope/location.
- gas isolation.
- drain/purge where needed.
- calibrated gas test.
- combustible removal.
- extinguisher/fire watch.
- weather/wind consideration.
- detector status.
- LOTO.
- start/expiry time.
- issuer/receiver signatures.

## Gas test
Exact acceptable atmosphere criteria follow the approved safety procedure and detector limits.

## Fire watch
Continue during work and for the approved post-work period.

## Automatic cancellation
- gas alarm.
- fire alarm.
- changing process condition.
- permit expiry.
