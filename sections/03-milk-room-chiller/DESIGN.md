# Milk Room + Chiller — Functional and Interior Design

## Design principle
Milk moves in one direction:
```
cow/milking side → receive/filter → chiller → dispatch → milk truck
```

Cleaning flows remain physically separated from dispatch and testing as much as the compact 14×18 ft room allows.

## Receiving/testing zone
Include:
- sanitary milk connection or closed transfer point
- filter/strainer housing
- sample point
- digital scale/volume log if used
- lactometer/basic QA kit as applicable
- handwash basin
- clean PPE storage

## Chiller zone
Recommended:
- 300 L minimum capacity for daily collection
- 500 L preferred for stronger reserve / alternate-day collection
- SS304 milk-contact surfaces
- agitator
- digital temperature display
- drain/CIP connection
- enough clearance to remove compressor or agitator for service

## Dispatch zone
Directly faces service lane.
Provide:
- sanitary transfer hose
- capped tri-clamp connection
- hose hanger
- truck-side access without entering cow/manure areas
- cleanable threshold

## Wash/CIP zone
Provide:
- deep SS sink
- hot water
- cold water
- chemical dosing/storage
- hose reel
- drying rack
- floor drain
- splash-resistant wall finish

## Interior finishes
- seamless non-slip floor
- coved base
- washable walls to ceiling
- sealed ceiling
- stainless splashbacks behind wash/transfer points
- no timber shelves
- no cardboard storage near wet processing

## Pest/insect control
- self-closing doors
- insect screens
- sealed penetrations
- easy-clean corners
- no open food waste

## Contamination control
Prohibited:
- manure tools
- feed sacks
- veterinary chemical storage
- fertilizer bags
- fuel/gas equipment
- unclean footwear beyond clean threshold

## Power-failure design
Critical priority:
1. chiller/refrigeration
2. temperature monitoring
3. milk-transfer pump as needed
4. lighting/ventilation

Hot-water heater is non-critical and should be load-shed on generator backup.
