# Water Utility — Operations and Maintenance

## Daily
- tank levels
- pump status
- pressure
- visible leaks
- meter total

## Weekly
- pump noise/leak
- valve inspection
- OHT overflow
- filter differential/condition

## Monthly
- inspect/clean accessible strainers
- review water use vs expected
- test standby pump
- inspect tower/ladder

## Tank cleaning
Schedule based on source quality and inspection.
Do not let sludge accumulate.

## Periodic
- water-quality lab tests
- pump service
- meter verification
- level-sensor test
- backflow-device inspection

## KPIs
- L/cow/day
- total m³/day
- m³ milk-room cleaning/day
- m³ digester dilution/day
- pump kWh/m³
- leak loss %
- days storage autonomy
