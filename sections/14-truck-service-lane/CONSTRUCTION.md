# Truck / Service Lane — Construction

## Before construction
- survey levels
- test subgrade/CBR
- confirm actual truck
- swept-path/gate study
- confirm utility crossings
- design drain/outfall
- pavement engineer final thickness/rebar

## Sequence
1. set out 14×95 ft
2. utility sleeves
3. excavation/subgrade prep
4. compact sub-base
5. drain construction
6. form/reinforcement/dowels
7. RCC pavement
8. joints/saw cuts
9. curing
10. gate
11. lighting/CCTV
12. markings/signage
13. truck trial
14. drainage test

## Hold points
- subgrade density
- sub-base thickness
- drain invert
- rebar/dowels
- concrete thickness
- concrete strength
- joint seal
- gate clearance
- swept-path trial
