# Gas Storage — Safety and Hazard Report

## Main hazards
- methane fire/explosion
- H₂S toxicity
- oxygen displacement
- membrane puncture/tear
- overpressure
- vacuum/collapse
- static/lightning
- condensate exposure
- unauthorized access
- hot work

## Holder is low pressure
Do not treat it like an LPG/CNG cylinder or pressure vessel.
No compression/high-pressure storage occurs here.

## Ventilation
Protective frame/enclosure remains open/mesh.
Never build solid walls around holder without engineered ventilation.

## Ignition control
- no smoking
- no open flame
- no welding/cutting while gas-filled
- hazardous-area electrical review
- bonding/earthing
- lightning assessment

## H₂S
Raw gas can contain toxic H₂S.
- gas detection
- keep vent/relief away from personnel
- do not deliberately inhale/test gas by smell
- use proper gas-testing instruments

## P/V protection
Independent mechanical pressure/vacuum protection is mandatory.
Do not:
- block relief
- isolate all relief paths
- exceed vendor pressure
- pull membrane into vacuum

## Membrane protection
- no sharp objects
- no rodent access
- no tools stored on/inside cage
- UV/weather protection
- approved patch kit only
- inspect weld seams

## Emergency vent/flare
Final safe vent/flare location and separation distances require engineer/fire review.

## Access
Lockable cage.
Routine workers service manifold from outside cage where possible.

## Leak response
1. stop ignition
2. alarm
3. isolate gas if safe
4. stop/limit upstream feed
5. ventilate naturally
6. keep people out
7. gas-test
8. repair only after safe isolation

## Lightning
Bond frame and metallic piping.
Final lightning protection is risk-based and must not create puncture hazards to membrane.

## Fire
If membrane is involved in fire:
- evacuate
- isolate upstream if safe
- fire service response
- do not approach pressurized/flammable gas equipment without training
