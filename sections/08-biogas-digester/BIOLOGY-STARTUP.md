# Biogas Digester — Biology and Startup Plan

## Purpose
Anaerobic digestion is a living biological process. Civil completion does not mean immediate full gas production.

## Planning operating conditions
- temperature: ~25–35°C desirable ambient/mesophilic range
- pH: ~6.8–7.5 planning band
- target HRT: ~37 days
- feed TS: ~9.5% planning
- OLR: ~2.05 kg VS/m³·day planning

These are operating targets/assumptions, not legal or vendor limits.

## Inoculum
Preferred startup uses active anaerobic digestate/sludge from a healthy compatible digester.

Planning inoculum range:
```
20–30% of 20 m³ working volume
=4–6 m³
```

Exact inoculum source/quantity requires biogas specialist approval.

## Initial fill
Example concept:
1. leak-tested clean vessel
2. add inoculum
3. add water/slurry progressively
4. exclude air from gas system safely
5. establish low-pressure gas route
6. begin reduced feed

## Feed ramp
Example planning schedule only:
- initial stabilization: no/very low fresh feed
- then ~25% design feed
- advance to 50%
- then 75%
- then 100% only when pH, gas and odor remain stable

Do **not** advance solely by calendar dates.

## Daily startup monitoring
- temperature
- pH
- gas production
- gas pressure
- feed volume
- digestate appearance
- unusual foam/scum
- odor
- leaks

## Optional process tests
Where available:
- alkalinity
- VFA/alkalinity ratio
- TS/VS
- methane fraction
- ammonia
- digestate pH

## First gas
Initial gas can contain significant CO₂/air and may not be suitable for engine use.

Do not send startup gas to generator until:
- oxygen/air is safely purged according to gas-system procedure
- methane quality is acceptable
- Section 09/10 gas systems are commissioned
- vendor/operator approves use

## Biological upset
Warning signs:
- falling pH
- falling gas
- rising VFA
- strong sour odor
- persistent foam
- abnormal digestate

Response:
- reduce feed
- verify dilution
- check temperature
- consult process specialist
- do not compensate by random chemical addition

## Long shutdown
For extended shutdown:
- stop/step down feed
- maintain safe gas handling
- protect vessel from vacuum/pressure
- monitor biology
- restart gradually
