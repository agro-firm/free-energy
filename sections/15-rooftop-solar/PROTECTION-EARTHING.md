# Rooftop Solar — Protection, Earthing and Lightning

## DC
Provide:
- string connectors.
- DC isolator.
- DC SPD Type II.
- string fuses if required by parallel-string/inverter architecture.
- UV-resistant PV cable.
- DC labels.

## AC
Provide:
- dedicated 4-pole breaker.
- AC isolator.
- Type II AC SPD.
- generation meter/monitor.
- anti-islanding through approved inverter.

## Earthing
Bond:
- module frames.
- rails.
- inverter.
- AC/DC DBs.
- metallic cable trays.

Use coordinated farm earth network.

## Lightning
Conduct formal lightning-risk assessment.
Coordinate:
- existing lightning protection.
- PV frame bonding.
- SPD.
- separation from lightning conductors.

Do not simply attach PV rails to lightning down-conductor without design.

## Fire
- DC isolator accessible.
- route cables neatly.
- avoid connector mixing.
- no loose coiled DC cable.
- emergency labels.
- maintain roof access.
