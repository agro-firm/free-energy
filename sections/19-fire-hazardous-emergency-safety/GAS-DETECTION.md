# Gas Detection Plan

## Section 08
Consider fixed:
- CH₄ near gas-manifold/high-level region.
- H₂S where release/maintenance exposure risk exists.

## Section 09
CH₄/H₂S area detection outside/around holder as hazard review determines.

## Section 10
CH₄/H₂S at treatment skid and sample/drain area.

## Section 11
- CH₄/LEL detector high in room.
- CO detector in room/breathing zone.
- H₂S only if risk review requires after treated-gas quality.

## Alarm philosophy
Use at least:
- warning.
- high/trip.
Actual ppm/%LEL setpoints are **VENDOR/ENGINEER**, not invented here.

## Portable meters
Maintain portable calibrated multi-gas detector for:
- maintenance.
- confined-space pre-entry.
- leak investigation.

## Calibration
- bump tests.
- calibration intervals.
- sensor-life replacement.
- records.
Follow manufacturer and safety-program requirements.
