# Waste Receiving + Mixing — Architecture Report

## 1. Design intent
Section 07 is a **covered semi-open dirty-process bay**, not a sealed room. It must contain manure splashes/odor, protect equipment from rain, permit washdown and maintenance, and prevent unsafe gas accumulation around sumps.

## 2. Geometry
```
10 × 14 = 140 ft²
140 × 0.09290304 = 13.0064 m²
```

Use local X=0–14 ft west→east, Y=0–10 ft south→north.

## 3. Three architectural bands
### Inlet/receiving — X 0–4 ft
```
4×10 = 40 ft²
```
Contains:
- cow-shed manure inlet
- coarse removable screen/basket
- grit pocket
- covered receiving sump
- inlet transfer pump

### Mixing/process — X 4–10 ft
```
6×10 = 60 ft²
```
Contains:
- 500 L mixing tank on load cells
- top-entry mixer
- measured water line
- slurry feed pump
- flow meter
- pipe manifold
- wash/cleanout points

### Control/emergency — X 10–14 ft
```
4×10 = 40 ft²
```
Contains:
- ~1.0 m³ emergency sump
- PLC/control panel on raised clean wall
- isolation/valve access
- PPE/spill kit
- service aisle

Area:
```
40+60+40 = 140 ft²
```

## 4. Building form
Recommended:
- steel or masonry/steel covered bay
- roof around 10–11 ft clear height concept
- sides largely open above splash walls
- 2–3 ft high washable curb/splash walls
- roof over all tanks/pumps
- no enclosed ceiling void
- strong cross ventilation

## 5. Odor/gas strategy
Fresh manure can release odor and, if stagnant, methane/H₂S.

Architectural controls:
- keep sumps covered
- vent covers to safe high point
- semi-open perimeter
- no low enclosed electrical recess
- no ignition source
- prevent long manure retention
- optional odor-media/biofilter vent if required

## 6. Floor
- reinforced concrete
- non-slip chemical/manure-resistant finish
- curbed perimeter
- floor slopes to dirty collection point
- no connection to clean stormwater

## 7. Sumps
Receiving and emergency sumps are covered below-grade process vessels.

Rules:
- removable grating/cover for service
- no routine human entry
- provide pumping/cleanout from above
- confined-space label
- structural design for wall/floor/groundwater loads
- cover must support expected pedestrian/service load

## 8. Roof and rain
Clean roof water:
```
roof → gutter → clean stormwater
```

Do not let roof rain enter process sumps because it dilutes slurry and can overload the digester.

## 9. Maintenance access
Maintain at least a continuous ~3 ft practical service path around critical pumps/valves where layout permits. Equipment must be removable vertically or through open side without demolition.

## 10. Materials
- reinforced concrete sumps
- HDPE/PP/FRP or SS304 mixing tank depending vendor
- galvanized/epoxy-coated steel frame
- manure-compatible HDPE/uPVC process pipe where suitable
- corrosion-resistant fasteners
- IP-rated electrical enclosure
- stainless/HDPE removable coarse screen

## 11. Separation
No milk, feed or clean-office traffic crosses this section. It sits downstream of Section 01 manure flow and upstream of Section 08 digester.
