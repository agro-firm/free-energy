# Revenue Model

## Cash revenue
Potential:
1. milk.
2. approved fertilizer.
3. verified livestock/calf sales.
4. electricity export settlement if actually paid/credited.
5. approved value-added products.

## Avoided-cost value
Not cash sales:
- self-consumed solar.
- self-consumed generator electricity.
- fertilizer replacing purchased nutrients.

Total Business Value = Cash Revenue + Realized Avoided Cost.

## Not in base
- calf sales.
- cull sales.
- yogurt/processing.
- carbon credits.
- third-party waste fees.
- grants/subsidies.

Add only after a real commercial basis.
