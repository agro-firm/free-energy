# Feed Store — Operations and Maintenance

## Daily
- record temperature/RH
- inspect floor for water
- inspect roof/door leaks
- inspect spilled feed
- inspect visible pests
- confirm oldest stock picked first
- keep aisle clear

## Every delivery
- weigh/count
- inspect moisture/damage
- record lot/date/price
- quarantine suspect feed
- update stock ledger

## Weekly
- sweep/vacuum behind pallets/racks
- inspect rodent traps
- inspect rack bolts/anchors
- inspect door seals
- check dehumidifier filter/tank/drain
- inspect roughage for heating/mold

## Monthly
- full inventory reconciliation
- expiry/FEFO review
- inspect roof/gutters
- clean fan screens
- review stock loss
- inspect fire equipment
- test scale against reference weight

## Monsoon
- monitor RH more frequently
- inspect threshold after heavy rain
- shorten stock duration if necessary
- run dehumidifier as required
- reject wet feed

## Annual
- structural/rack inspection
- electrical test
- repaint corrosion
- service ventilation/dehumidifier
- review pest-control program
- verify scale calibration

## KPIs
- days of concentrate stock
- days of roughage stock
- feed loss %
- mold/wet rejection kg
- pest incidents
- average RH
- inventory variance
- feed cost/kg
