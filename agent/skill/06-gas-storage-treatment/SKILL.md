# Gas Storage + Treatment Skill

## Canonical order
Digester → primary condensate protection → raw gas holder → H2S treatment → fine moisture separation → gas meter → pressure regulation/boosting as required → generator.

## Section 09 storage baseline
- Gas production: 8.10–9.18 m³/day.
- Nominal raw-gas holder: **8 m³**.
- Zone: **10 × 12 ft**.
- Baseline technology: **custom low-pressure flexible H₂S-compatible membrane gas holder inside a ventilated protective frame**.
- Planning max inflated membrane envelope: about **2.0 × 2.0 × 2.0 m** equivalent.
- A vendor-engineered double-membrane equivalent is acceptable if an 8 m³ unit is available and fits the zone.
- Main storage control is by holder volume/position, not pressure alone.
- 75% demand-enable concept: 6.0 m³.
- 20% low-cutout concept: 1.6 m³.
- Usable control swing: 4.4 m³.

## Section 09 safety/control
- Upstream condensate protection.
- Low-pressure inlet/outlet isolation.
- Independent pressure/vacuum protection.
- Safe emergency vent/flare connection.
- CH4/H2S detection.
- Membrane level/position sensing.
- Low-range pressure measurement.
- Bonding/earthing and lightning review.
- Hazardous-area classification by qualified engineer.

## Section 10 treatment functions
- H2S reduction to generator-maker requirement.
- Fine moisture removal.
- Pressure stabilization/boosting if needed.
- Flow/volume metering.
- Manual and automatic isolation.

## Pressure rule
Do not copy public vendor mbar values as project setpoints. Flexible and double-membrane holders operate at low pressure, but exact operating/relief/vacuum settings must come from the selected holder vendor and gas-system engineer.

## Rule
Never route untreated raw biogas directly to the generator.
