# Gas Storage + Treatment Skill

## Canonical order
Digester → primary condensate protection → raw gas holder → H2S treatment → fine moisture separation → gas meter → pressure regulator → generator.

## Baseline
- Gas production: 8.10–9.18 m³/day.
- Raw gas holder: 8 m³.
- Storage zone: 10 × 12 ft.
- Treatment zone: 8 × 10 ft.

## Treatment functions
- Condensate removal.
- H2S reduction to generator-maker requirement.
- Moisture removal.
- Pressure stabilization.
- Flow/volume metering.
- Manual and automatic isolation.

## Required safety concepts
Pressure relief, safe vent/flare, gas detection, ventilation, bonding/earthing and hazardous-area review.

## Rule
Never route untreated raw biogas directly to the generator.
