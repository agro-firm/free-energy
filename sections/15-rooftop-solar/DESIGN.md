# Rooftop Solar — Functional Design

## Module selection
Baseline uses 625 W N-type bifacial TOPCon class.

Reasons:
- exact 24-module =15.0 kWp.
- SREDA-approved model available.
- high power reduces module count.
- fits one clean row on each roof plane.

## Inverter
15 kW three-phase, 2-MPPT class.

Reasons:
- DC/AC ratio:
```
15.0/15.0=1.00
```
- one MPPT per roof face.
- avoids mismatch between different azimuths.
- current Bangladesh approved 15 kW inverter models exist.

## Orientation
Actual annual yield depends on surveyed ridge direction.

The two-MPPT layout remains useful if the roof faces:
- east/west.
- southeast/northwest.
- south/north.

If one roof face is materially poor/shaded, final design may reconfigure without changing total target.

## Bifaciality
Close-to-metal-roof installation normally provides limited rear gain.
Do not use optimistic bifacial gain in the base energy forecast.

## Shade
Final survey checks:
- trees.
- water tank.
- poles.
- ridge vent.
- nearby buildings.
- lightning masts.

## Cleaning
Panels accessible from roof corridors.
Do not walk on modules.
