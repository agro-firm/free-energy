# 04 — Office + Vet + Biosecurity

## Status
Planning baseline. Detailed construction design will be added later.

## Baseline
12 × 15 ft

## Purpose
Front-of-house control point for records, visitor hygiene, veterinary supplies and basic breeding/health administration.

## Inputs
Staff, visitors, records, medicines/PPE and small veterinary supplies.

## Outputs
Controlled personnel entry, farm records and health-management support.

## Interfaces
Main entrance, cow shed and clean staff circulation.

## Current design notes
Keep this area away from dirty manure handling. Include handwashing, PPE storage, visitor control and secure medicine storage.
