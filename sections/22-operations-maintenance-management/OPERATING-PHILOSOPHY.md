# Operating Philosophy

## Priorities
1. People safety.
2. Animal welfare.
3. Milk food safety/cold chain.
4. Water availability.
5. Environmental containment.
6. Gas/process safety.
7. Equipment reliability.
8. Energy efficiency.
9. Product quality.
10. Cost optimization.

## Operating principle
The plant is one connected system.

A decision in one section can affect another:
- excess wash water dilutes digester feed.
- poor feed changes milk/manure.
- poor gas treatment damages generator.
- generator overload threatens milk cooling.
- blocked drainage threatens hygiene/biosecurity.

## Normal management cycle
```
Observe → Record → Compare → Act → Verify → Improve
```

## Data hierarchy
- alarm/critical data: immediate.
- daily operating log: same shift.
- weekly trend: manager.
- monthly KPI/OPEX review.
- quarterly technical/veterinary review.
- annual management/asset review.
