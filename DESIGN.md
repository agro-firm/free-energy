# Master Design Rules

## 1. No geometry drift
No master document, image, vendor drawing or optimization may silently:
- enlarge the plot.
- move the service lane.
- resize/move a physical section.
- add a new permanent building.
- move solar away from Section 01 roof.

## 2. Physical vs control sections
Physical:
01–15.

Nonphysical management/control:
16–23.

Only 01–15 become structures/areas in site images.

## 3. Clean/dirty hierarchy
Clean:
03,04,05,06,13.

Livestock:
01,02.

Dirty:
07,08,12 wet process.

Gas/energy:
09,10,11.

## 4. Access
- Section 14 remains continuous.
- no permanent storage/parking.
- one truck at a time.
- milk/feed/fertilizer nodes remain separated operationally.

## 5. Utilities
- potable water protected from dirty backflow.
- clean stormwater separate from dirty process water.
- no gas bypass.
- no uncontrolled solar/generator parallel.

## 6. Safety
The following remain hold points until closed:
- digester/generator proximity.
- hazardous-area extent.
- fire-service access.
- surveyed flood/outfall.
- electrical utility/service.
- structural solar uplift.

## 7. Maintainability
Every item requires:
- access.
- isolation.
- removal path.
- inspection space.
- drainage where relevant.

Open/buffer spaces are not available for casual new construction.

## 8. Design change
Any master change follows [MASTER-CHANGE-CONTROL.md](MASTER-CHANGE-CONTROL.md).
