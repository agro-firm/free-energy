# Survey Brief

## Purpose
Capture enough horizontal and vertical data to validate DCF-1 and produce the final grading, drainage and setting-out drawings.

## Boundary/control
Survey:
- P0=(0,0).
- P1=(65,0).
- P2=(65,95).
- P3=(0,95).
- legal/physical boundary markers.
- wall/fence thickness.
- encroachments.

## Permanent benchmarks
Install:
- TBM-01 outside construction disturbance.
- TBM-02 independent check benchmark.

Record:
- easting/northing or local coordinate.
- RL.
- physical description.
- photos.
- benchmark closure.

## Topographic shots
Minimum:
- 10-ft grid across plot.
- every DCF section corner.
- all breaks in slope.
- drain/ditch inverts.
- building thresholds if existing.
- road crown/edges.
- gate throat.
- utility covers.
- visible flood/high-water marks.

## Service lane
At Y:
0, 10, 18, 30, 36, 47, 60, 63, 65, 66, 74, 79, 90, 95 ft.

At each station measure:
- X51 inner/building edge.
- lane center.
- X65 outer/drain edge.
- adjacent drain invert where present.

## Road frontage
Capture at least:
- road center/crown.
- near edge.
- far edge.
- ditch/drain invert.
- upstream/downstream 15–30 m where relevant.
- gate approach obstacles.

## Outfall
Survey:
- candidate outfall invert.
- normal water level.
- observed high-water mark.
- backwater/tidal influence if applicable.

## Deliverables
- signed survey drawing.
- DWG/DXF.
- PDF.
- CSV point file.
- coordinate system/datum statement.
- benchmark sheet.
- site/road/outfall photos.
