# Cow Shed — Cost and Budget Report

## 1. Cost basis

**Location basis:** Bangladesh planning estimate  
**Price basis date:** October 2026  
**Currency:** BDT (Tk)  
**Scope:** Section 01 cow shed only

This estimate is for planning. It is not a contractor quotation.

The design still requires:
- structural drawings
- soil/foundation design
- vendor quotations
- scraper quotation
- fan/trough quotation
- electrical BOQ
- plumbing BOQ
- tax/VAT/transport checks

High-sensitivity items:

2. concrete/reinforcement quantity
3. automatic scraper system
4. galvanizing/corrosion treatment
5. imported equipment
6. transport to site
7. foundation conditions
8. solar-ready structural upgrade
9. VAT/tax/duties

## 9. Operating-cost allowance

The shed creates monthly operating costs such as:
- fans
- lights
- scraper motor
- water pumping
- cleaning materials
- scraper lubrication/parts
- fan maintenance
- corrosion repair

### Concept electrical use example
Fans:
```
1.0 kW × 12 h/day × 30
= 360 kWh/month
```

Lights:
```
0.216 kW × 6 h/day × 30
≈ 38.9 kWh/month
```

Scraper allowance:
```
≈ 60–70 kWh/month
```

Planning section total:
**~460–470 kWh/month before other loads**

Much of this may be offset by project solar, but energy is not free and should still be metered.

### Maintenance reserve
A simple planning reserve of 2% of base CAPEX/year:
```
Tk 3,711,840 × 0.02
= Tk 74,236.8/year
```

Monthly:
```
74,236.8 / 12
≈ Tk 6,186/month
```

This is a reserve, not a vendor maintenance quote.

## 10. Procurement strategy

Obtain at least:
- 3 structural/PEB quotes
- 2 concrete/civil quotes
- 2 scraper-system quotes
- 2 stall/trough quotes
- 2 fan/electrical quotes

Normalize every quote to the same scope before comparing.

Do not choose a cheap quote that omits:
- foundations
- drainage
- galvanizing
- guttering
- installation
- transport
- controls
- testing
- warranties

---

## 11. Cost-control rule

When an actual quote is accepted:
1. replace the relevant ASSUMPTION rate
2. keep the math visible
3. update low/base/high model
4. record vendor name/date
5. do not silently change dimensions to fit a cheaper item
