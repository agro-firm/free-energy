# Milking and Cold-Chain SOP

## Before milking
- operator hygiene/PPE.
- milk room clean.
- chiller ready.
- sanitary hoses/fittings ready.
- treated/withdrawal cow list checked.

## Milking
- identify cow.
- inspect udder/teats.
- segregate abnormal/withdrawal milk.
- use hygienic milking process.
- record yield.

## Cooling
Planning:
- milk moved rapidly toward ~4°C target.
- actual buyer/food-safety requirement governs.
- temperature/time logged.

## Dispatch
- clean vehicle/interface.
- volume recorded.
- dispatch hose capped after use.

## After milking
- drain.
- approved cleaning/CIP.
- rinse/dry as procedure.
- floor/drain clean.
- chemicals locked.

## Rejection
Do not mix:
- antibiotic-withdrawal milk.
- visibly abnormal milk.
- contaminated milk.
- temperature-abused milk unless QA accepts.

## KPIs
- L/day.
- L/lactating cow.
- time-to-cooling.
- rejected L.
- CIP cycles.
- chiller kWh.
