# Water Utility — Architecture Report

## Layout
Use local X=0–12 ft west→east, Y=0–8 ft south→north.

### Below-grade ground tank
X=0–8 ft, Y=0–8 ft.

Planning internal dimensions:
- 7.2 × 7.2 × 5.45 ft
- volume ≈8.00 m³

RCC wall/base/roof thickness are ENGINEER values.

### Pump/control bay
X=8–12 ft, Y=0–8 ft =32 ft².

Contains:
- twin pumps
- pressure vessel
- filters/treatment
- meters
- control panel
- valves/manifold

### Overhead tank
2,000 L tank on independent steel/RCC platform above utility area.
Planning platform about 5×5 ft.
Tank platform elevation around 12–15 ft above grade, final structure ENGINEER.

## Building form
- pump bay roofed and ventilated
- below-grade tank sealed from surface contamination
- access manhole above flood level
- tank vent screened
- overflow visible
- no sewage/process drain adjacent to tank opening

## Surface protection
- utility floor above surrounding grade
- stormwater slopes away
- no manure/digestate storage beside water tank
- chemical dosing cabinet separated from potable fittings

## Materials
- RCC underground tank with potable-water-compatible internal coating
- food-grade plastic overhead tank
- uPVC/HDPE/PPR potable-water pipe as appropriate
- galvanized/stainless supports and fasteners where exposed
