# Fertilizer Processing SOP

## Daily
If buffer/tank capacity available:
- inspect screw press.
- run ~daily batch.
- collect cake.
- liquid to tank.
- date curing bay.

## Base planning
- wet cake ~83.8 kg/day.
- finished product ~38.7 kg/day.
- liquid ~0.456 m³/day.

Actual values replace assumptions after commissioning.

## Curing/drying
- shallow spread.
- turn/aerate.
- rain protection.
- moisture check.

## Bagging
Only after quality acceptance.
25 kg bags concept.

## Commercial product
No nutrient/legal claims without:
- lab results.
- current registration/approval.
