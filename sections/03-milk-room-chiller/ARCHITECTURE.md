# Milk Room + Chiller — Architecture Report

## 1. Purpose
A compact hygienic room where milk is received from the cow shed, filtered/transferred, rapidly chilled, stored briefly, tested, washed/CIP-cleaned and dispatched directly to the milk truck.

## 2. Geometry
```
18 ft × 14 ft = 252 ft²
252 × 0.09290304 = 23.4116 m²
```

Concept clear height:
```
10 ft = 3.048 m
```

Approximate room volume:
```
252 × 10 = 2,520 ft³
≈ 71.36 m³
```

## 3. Architectural zoning
Local plan assumes **south side faces the service lane** and **north side receives clean milk/milking equipment from the cow side**. The whole room may rotate in the final global site plan without changing internal geometry.

### West strip: X 0–6 ft
- Y 0–8: wash/CIP zone, 48 ft²
- Y 8–14: receiving/testing/handwash zone, 36 ft²

### Center strip: X 6–13 ft
- Y 0–14: chiller + service-clearance zone, 98 ft²

### East strip: X 13–18 ft
- Y 0–8: dispatch/transfer zone, 40 ft²
- Y 8–14: clean ingress / milking-equipment docking, 30 ft²

Area check:
```
48 + 36 + 98 + 40 + 30 = 252 ft²
```

## 4. Doors
Concept:
- south dispatch/service-lane door: ~4 ft wide, self-closing, washable
- north clean-ingress door: ~3.5 ft wide
- personnel/service access may be combined with the dispatch door if workflow remains hygienic

## 5. Interior envelope
Recommended:
- dense concrete/masonry or insulated food-room wall system
- full-height washable wall finish: food-grade panel, epoxy/PU coating, FRP or equivalent
- coved wall/floor junctions
- non-slip seamless epoxy/PU floor
- washable light-color ceiling
- sealed penetrations
- insect-proof ventilation openings
- corrosion-resistant hardware

## 6. Cold-chain architecture
The room itself is not necessarily a cold room. The bulk milk cooler/chiller provides product cooling.

Architectural design must:
- allow condenser airflow or remote condenser placement
- keep direct sun off the chiller
- provide equipment service clearance
- prevent hot-water/CIP steam from overheating the chiller zone
- keep truck loading short and clean

## 7. Hygiene separation
The room has three hygiene levels:
1. **clean milk receiving/testing**
2. **cold-storage/dispatch**
3. **wash/CIP**

Dirty cow traffic, manure tools, feed sacks and fertilizer do not enter.

## 8. Ventilation
If condenser heat is released indoors, provide mechanically assisted clean-side intake and service-side exhaust.

Planning 8 ACH:
```
71.36 m³ × 8 = 570.9 m³/h
≈ 336 cfm
```

A 400–500 cfm class ventilation provision is a reasonable concept; final requirement follows the chiller manufacturer.

## 9. Lighting
Use bright, sealed washable LED fixtures with good color rendering for inspection.

Concept target: 6 fixtures × 18 W = 108 W connected lighting.

## 10. Materials
Milk-contact surfaces: SS304 or manufacturer-approved food-grade material.
Room surfaces: non-absorbent, washable, chemical-resistant.
Doors: smooth, washable, insect-tight.
Drain covers: removable, corrosion resistant, easy to sanitize.

## 11. Professional verification
Food hygiene, wastewater, electrical, refrigeration, fire, structural and Bangladesh food-safety requirements require qualified professional and authority review before construction.
