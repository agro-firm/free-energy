## Fence requirements
- no sharp wire ends
- corrosion resistant
- posts/foundation sized for animal impact and wind
- mesh aperture must not trap hooves/head
- interior face kept smooth

## Drain requirements
- grate must support animal hoof loads
- easy to lift/clean by workers
- no opening large enough to trap hoof
- outlet must be roddable/flushable

## Shade canopy
Vendor/engineer must define:
- post section
- foundation
- wind load
- roof material
- gutter
- corrosion system
