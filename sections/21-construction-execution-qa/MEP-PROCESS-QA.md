# MEP / Process Installation QA

## Piping
- material/size.
- route/slope.
- supports.
- valve orientation.
- cleanout.
- pressure/leak test.
- flushing/cleanliness.

## Gas
- gas-compatible materials.
- leak test.
- P/V relief path.
- no unauthorized bypass.
- hazardous-area equipment review.

## Electrical
- cable type/size.
- termination.
- gland.
- labeling.
- insulation resistance.
- earth continuity.
- breaker/settings.
- phase/polarity.

## Instrumentation
- tag.
- range.
- calibration.
- installation orientation.
- loop check.
- PLC mapping.

## Rotating equipment
- foundation.
- alignment.
- rotation.
- lubrication.
- guard.
- vibration/noise.
