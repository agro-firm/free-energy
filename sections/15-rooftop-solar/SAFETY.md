# Rooftop Solar — Safety

## Hazards
- live DC whenever illuminated.
- roof fall.
- wind.
- heat.
- arc fault.
- electrical shock.
- lightning.
- fragile roof sheets.

## Roof work
- fall protection.
- approved anchors/walkways.
- no walking directly on unsupported sheet.
- no work in high wind/rain.

## DC
- matched connectors.
- correct polarity.
- isolation.
- arc-safe procedure.
- label live PV conductors.

## Fire
Maintain access and shutdown labeling.
Use inverter/isolators approved for installation.

## Structure
Never install before structural signoff.

## Generator interaction
Grid-tied inverter cannot be manually forced onto isolated generator bus.
