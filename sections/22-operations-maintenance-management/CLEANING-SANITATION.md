# Cleaning and Sanitation

## Clean-zone order
Clean from cleaner to dirtier areas.

Never use manure tools in:
- milk room.
- feed store.
- office/vet.

## Cow shed
- scraper first.
- targeted wash water.
- trough/manger clean.
- avoid excessive water.

## Milk room
- every milking/dispatch.
- approved chemical concentration/contact.
- rinse/dry as required.
- verify no residue.

## Feed
Dry cleaning preferred.
No wet wash before feed storage unless fully dried.

## Biosecurity
Disinfect footwear/equipment per approved farm plan.

## Chemical control
- labeled.
- SDS.
- locked.
- correct dilution.
- no mixing incompatible chemicals.

## Verification
- visual cleanliness.
- smell/residue.
- ATP/microbiological test if adopted.
- chemical-use trend.
