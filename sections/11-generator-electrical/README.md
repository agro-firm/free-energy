# 11 — Generator + Electrical

## Status
Planning baseline. Detailed construction design will be added later.

## Baseline
12 × 14 ft room; 5 kW rated biogas generator

## Purpose
Converts treated biogas into electricity and integrates generator power with farm electrical controls.

## Inputs
Treated biogas and control signals.

## Outputs
Electrical energy, exhaust heat/gas and operating data.

## Interfaces
Gas treatment, solar/electrical system, farm loads and automation controls.

## Current design notes
Current plan assumes about 4 kW operating output when running and approximately 3.6–4.1 hours/day at baseline gas production. Final equipment data controls actual performance.
