# Master Operations and Maintenance

## Core operating team
- Farm/operations manager ×1.
- Livestock/milking operators ×2.
- Utility/process technician ×1.
- Feed/cleaning/logistics worker ×1.
- night/security/relief as required.
- veterinarian/AI and specialist technicians on-call.

## Daily rhythm
Morning:
- herd/water/alarm check.
- milking.
- cooling.
- CIP.
- feeding.
- manure check.

Day:
- PM.
- feed logistics.
- fertilizer run.
- process monitoring.
- records/admin.

Evening:
- second milking.
- cooling/CIP.
- feed/water.
- slurry/gas check.
- handover.

## Maintenance strategy
- daily operator care.
- weekly inspection.
- monthly planned maintenance.
- quarterly service/calibration.
- annual specialist review.
- runtime/condition-based replacement where appropriate.

Vendor manuals override generic intervals.

## Critical PM
- milk chiller.
- scraper.
- slurry pumps/mixer.
- P/V protection.
- gas detection/H₂S.
- generator.
- water pumps.
- solar protection.
- fire system.

## 90-day rule
After commissioning:
- establish actual 7-day/30-day/90-day baselines.
- replace design assumptions where measured data materially differs.
